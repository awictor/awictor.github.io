# Boiling Point at Altitude Calculator

Calculate the **boiling point of water** at any altitude, in Celsius and Fahrenheit. Water boils cooler at elevation, which lengthens cooking times.

**[Open the tool →](https://awictor.github.io/boiling-point/)**

- ~100 °C − 0.0034 °C per metre of elevation
- Metres or feet input, °C and °F output
- Handles below-sea-level (boils hotter)
- Dark mode, 100% offline, no dependencies, no tracking

## Example

Denver (~1,609 m) → ~94.5 °C. A 3,000 m peak → ~89.8 °C.
