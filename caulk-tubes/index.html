How it works

  • A 10.1 oz tube lays roughly 24 ft of a 1/4" bead. Coverage drops with the square of bead size.
  • So a 1/8" bead covers ~96 ft, 3/8" ~10.5 ft, and 1/2" ~6 ft per tube.
  • Tubes needed = joint length ÷ coverage per tube, rounded up.
  • Buy one extra — you'll waste some priming the nozzle and cutting tips, and beads run wide in practice.
