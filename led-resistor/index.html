About LED resistors

An LED needs a series resistor to limit its current, or it burns out. The resistor drops the leftover voltage: R = (V_supply − V_forward) / I, with current in amps. A red LED (≈1.8–2.0 V) on 5 V at 20 mA needs about 150–160 Ω. Check the power the resistor must dissipate — P = (V_supply − V_forward)·I — and pick a rating above it. Standard E12 values (10, 12, 15, 18, 22…) are what you can actually buy; round up for safety. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
