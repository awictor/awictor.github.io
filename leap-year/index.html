The rule

A year is a leap year if it is divisible by 4 — except century years, which must also be divisible by 400. So 2000 and 2024 are leap years, but 1900 and 2100 are not. This keeps the calendar aligned with the ~365.2425-day solar year. February gains a 29th day and the year runs 366 days. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
