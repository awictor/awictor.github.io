How it works

  • Rule of thumb: 100 CFM per linear foot of cooktop width (so a 30″ range wants about 250 CFM).
  • For gas, also allow 1 CFM per 100 BTU of total burner output — take whichever number is larger.
  • Island hoods lose the wall's help catching rising air, so add about 50%.
  • Bigger fans need make-up air and larger ducts — over 400 CFM many codes require a dedicated make-up air supply.
