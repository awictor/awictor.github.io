About robots.txt

robots.txt sits at the root of your site (/robots.txt) and tells crawlers which paths they may or may not fetch. Each group starts with one or more User-agent lines, followed by Disallow and Allow rules (an empty Disallow: means "allow everything"). Optional Crawl-delay throttles polite bots, and Sitemap lines point crawlers at your sitemap. It's a guideline, not a security control — well-behaved bots obey it, but it never blocks access. Everything is generated locally; nothing leaves your browser.
