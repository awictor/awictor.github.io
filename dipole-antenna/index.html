The 468 rule

A real half-wave wire dipole is a bit shorter than a free-space half wavelength because of the wire's end effect, so hams use length (ft) = 468 ÷ frequency (MHz). Each of the two legs is half of that, 234 ÷ f, which is also the length of a quarter-wave vertical (ground-plane) element. Cut a little long and trim for lowest SWR. A 40 m dipole near 7.1 MHz is about 66 ft tip to tip. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
