Standard drinks vs. what's in the glass

A "drink" isn't a fixed thing — a strong craft beer can hold two or three times the alcohol of a light lager. Convert to a common measure: grams of pure alcohol = volume × ABV × 0.789 (ethanol's density). A US standard drink is 14 g, so a 12 oz 5% beer, a 5 oz glass of wine, and a 1.5 oz shot are each about one. A UK unit is 10 ml of pure alcohol (~7.9 g). Guidelines suggest keeping weekly intake low; this is information, not medical advice. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
