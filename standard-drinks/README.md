# Standard Drinks Calculator

Convert a drink's **volume and ABV** into grams of pure alcohol, **US standard drinks**, and **UK units**.

**[Open the tool →](https://awictor.github.io/standard-drinks/)**

- Pure alcohol (g) = volume × ABV × 0.789
- US standard drink = 14 g; UK unit = 10 ml ethanol
- Presets for beer, wine, spirits, and a UK pint
- Dark mode, 100% offline, no dependencies, no tracking

## Example

A 12 oz (355 ml) 5% beer ≈ 14 g ≈ 1 US standard drink ≈ 1.4 UK units.
