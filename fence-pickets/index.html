How it works

  • Each picket takes up its width plus one gap, so pickets = fence length ÷ (width + gap), rounded up.
  • Posts = length ÷ spacing, rounded up, plus one for the final end post.
  • Rail lumber = fence length × number of rails high (2 for short fences, 3 for tall privacy fences).
  • Add extra for gates, corners, and waste — and check post spacing against your local code.
