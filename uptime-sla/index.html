What the "nines" actually buy you

An uptime SLA sets how much your service is allowed to be down. Allowed downtime is (1 − uptime%) × period. Each extra nine cuts the budget by 10×: 99% is 3.65 days a year, 99.9% is 8.76 hours, 99.99% is ~53 minutes, and 99.999% ("five nines") is just ~5 minutes a year. That's your error budget — spend it on deploys and maintenance windows deliberately. Uses 365-day years and 30-day months. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
