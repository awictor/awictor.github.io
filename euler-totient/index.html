About Euler's totient

Euler's totient φ(n) counts the positive integers up to n that are coprime to n (share no common factor). Using the prime factorization, φ(n) = n · ∏(1 − 1/p) over each distinct prime p dividing n. So φ(36) = 36 · (1−½)(1−⅓) = 12. For a prime p, φ(p) = p − 1; for a prime power, φ(pᵏ) = pᵏ − pᵏ⁻¹. The totient is central to RSA encryption (choosing the public/private exponents) and to Euler's theorem, a^φ(n) ≡ 1 (mod n) for a coprime to n. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
