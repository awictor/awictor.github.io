◈ Diphone

A WebAudio vocal-tract synth. Four corners are the cardinal vowels (beet · bat · boot · bought); drag anywhere between them and the chord glides through vowels like a synthetic choir. Play notes on the keyboard row, sequence syllables, record a phrase, export a WAV.

Audio starts on click (browser policy). Best with headphones.
