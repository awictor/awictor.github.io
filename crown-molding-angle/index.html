How it works

  • Cutting crown flat on the table needs a compound cut: a miter and a bevel together.
  • Miter = atan(sin(spring) × tan(corner ÷ 2)); bevel = asin(cos(spring) × cos(corner ÷ 2)).
  • For a 90° corner and 38° crown that's about 31.6° miter and 33.9° bevel — the classic numbers.
  • Cutting crown nested (upside-down against the fence) uses simple 45° miters instead — this tool is for the flat method.
