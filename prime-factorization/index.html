About prime factorization

Every integer greater than 1 is either prime or can be written as a unique product of primes — the fundamental theorem of arithmetic. Factorizing finds that product: 360 = 2³ × 3² × 5. It's the basis for simplifying fractions, finding greatest common divisors and least common multiples, and much of number theory and cryptography. This tool uses trial division up to √n, so it's instant for everyday numbers. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
