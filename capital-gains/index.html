About

Gains on assets held over one year (366+ days) are taxed at the long-term rate; one year or less is short-term (taxed as ordinary income). Losses aren't taxed and may offset other gains. US long-term brackets are typically 0%, 15%, or 20% by income; short-term uses your marginal rate. Estimates only — not tax advice, and state taxes and the net investment income tax may also apply.
