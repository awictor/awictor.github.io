Wheeler's formula

For a single-layer air-core coil, Wheeler's approximation gives L (µH) = (d²·N²) ÷ (18d + 40ℓ), where d is the coil diameter and ℓ its length in inches, and N the number of turns. Inductance grows with the square of the turns, so doubling the winding quadruples L. At a working frequency the inductive reactance is XL = 2π·f·L.
