How it works

  • 1 MOA subtends about 1.047″ at 100 yards, and scales with distance — roughly 2″ at 200, 5″ at 500.
  • 1 mil (milliradian) subtends exactly 3.6″ at 100 yards, or 3.6 units per 1000 of the same unit.
  • The two relate by 1 mil = 3.4377 MOA, handy when your reticle and turrets speak different languages.
  • Most turrets click in 1/4 MOA or 0.1 mil — divide your needed correction by the click value to get the number of clicks.
  • These are the geometric angular sizes; real bullet drop also needs a ballistic solver for velocity, BC, and conditions.
