Sizing the supply for an LED strip

A strip's draw is its watts per metre × length. Current is watts ÷ voltage, which matters for wire gauge and connectors — a 5 m 14.4 W/m strip at 12 V pulls 72 W and 6 A. Pick a power supply with headroom (20–30%) above the load so it isn't running flat out, which extends its life and handles peak white. For long runs, injecting power at both ends avoids voltage-drop dimming toward the far end. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
