How it works

  • Wing loading = weight ÷ wing area. It sets how fast a wing must move to fly — high loading means higher stall and landing speeds.
  • But raw loading doesn't compare fairly across sizes. Cubic wing loading (WCL = weight ÷ area1.5) does, which is why RC pilots quote it.
  • Rough WCL guide: under 4 floats, 4–6 is a docile trainer, 6–9 sport, 9–11 advanced, 11–13 expert, and over 13 flies like a brick.
  • Cut weight or add wing area to bring a heavy model back into a friendlier band.
  • Area is entered in square inches and converted to square feet (÷144) for the standard oz/ft² figure.
