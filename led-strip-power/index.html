How it works

  • Strip watts = length × watts-per-foot (on the reel or datasheet). Add headroom so the supply isn't maxed out.
  • Supply amps = watts ÷ strip voltage — that's the rating your DC power supply must meet or beat.
  • Running a supply near 100% shortens its life and runs hot; 20% headroom is a good default.
  • For long 12 V runs, inject power at both ends or mid-run to avoid brightness drop-off (voltage sag).
