How it works

Bitrate is data per second. With decimal units (1 MB = 8,000 kb), the relationship is size(MB) = bitrate(kbps) × duration(s) ÷ 8000. To fit a clip into a target size, compute the total bitrate that size allows, then subtract the audio bitrate to get the video bitrate to feed your encoder. A 700 MB, 90 minute encode needs about 1,037 kbps total. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
