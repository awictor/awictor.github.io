A single playhead points up (12 o'clock); the rings spin beneath it, each advancing one step per pulse of the clock. Coprime lengths keep them out of phase — watch the melody reshuffle until every ring snaps back to step 0 together.

Controls: M mutes a ring, S solos it (or click a ring on the stage to solo). len = steps in the loop, fill = Euclidean pulses spread across them, rot = rotate the pattern, pitch = scale degree. Steppers take scroll and press-and-hold; on Tempo, Shift = ±10. Hover any control for a tip. Pause keeps the drift position; ⏮ stops and rewinds to step 0.
