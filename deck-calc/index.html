How it estimates

Boards run along the length; the number of rows across is the deck width ÷ (board width + gap), rounded up. Linear feet of decking = rows × deck length. Divide by your stock board length and round up for the raw board count, then add a waste allowance (~10%) for cuts, angled boards and defects. Nominal "5/4×6" and 2×6 boards are about 5.5″ wide. Excludes joists, fascia and stairs. Everything runs locally.
