The optics

Magnification is telescope focal length ÷ eyepiece focal length, so a shorter eyepiece zooms in more. The focal ratio (f-number) is focal length ÷ aperture — lower is "faster" and brighter for imaging. The exit pupil, aperture ÷ magnification, is the beam width entering your eye; keep it under about 7 mm so no light is wasted. Above roughly 2× the aperture in mm the image just gets dim and fuzzy — that's the max useful magnification. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
