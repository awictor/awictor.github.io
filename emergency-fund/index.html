How much is enough?

A common guideline is 3–6 months of essential expenses (rent/mortgage, food, utilities, insurance, minimum debt payments). Aim higher if your income is variable or a single earner supports the household. Keep it somewhere safe and liquid — a high-yield savings account, not investments.
