How it works

  • Gallons = deck area × coats ÷ coverage rate, rounded up to whole cans.
  • Coverage depends on the product and the wood: transparent stains go furthest; solid stains and thirsty, weathered boards drink more.
  • Include railings and stairs in the area — they add up fast and are easy to forget.
  • A second coat usually needs a bit less than the first; rounding up covers touch-ups.
