# Canning Time Calculator 🫙

Adjust home-canning **processing time and pressure for your altitude** — water-bath and pressure canning. Single offline HTML file, no dependencies, no data leaves your device.

## Features

- Water-bath: adds 5 / 10 / 15 / 20 minutes by altitude band.
- Pressure canning: correct psi for weighted (10/15) and dial gauges (11–14).
- Clear reminder to follow a tested USDA/Ball recipe.
- Dark mode, mobile friendly, works fully offline.

## Usage

Open `index.html` in any browser. Pick the method, enter your altitude (and base time for water-bath).

> This is a guide, not a substitute for a current tested canning recipe. Food safety depends on following one.
