How it works

  • Water boils cooler as you climb, so canning needs adjusting with altitude to stay safe.
  • Water-bath: add 5 min (1,001–3,000 ft), 10 (3,001–6,000), 15 (6,001–8,000), or 20 (above).
  • Pressure, weighted gauge: 10 lb up to 1,000 ft, else 15 lb. Dial gauge: 11 / 12 / 13 / 14 lb by 2,000-ft bands.
  • Always follow a tested recipe (USDA / Ball). This is a guide, not a substitute for a current canning guide.
