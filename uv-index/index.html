Reading the UV index

The UV index scales the sunburning power of the sun: the higher it is, the faster unprotected skin burns, roughly in inverse proportion. Starting from a baseline burn time for your skin type at UV 1, the estimate is base ÷ UV index minutes; sunscreen multiplies that by its SPF — in theory SPF 30 lets you stay out ~30× longer, though real protection is less because people under-apply and it sweats off. Risk bands: 0–2 Low, 3–5 Moderate, 6–7 High, 8–10 Very High, 11+ Extreme. This is a rough guide, not medical advice — reapply every two hours, seek shade at midday, and cover up when the index is high. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
