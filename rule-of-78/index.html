About the Rule of 78

The Rule of 78 (sum-of-the-digits) is a way some precomputed-interest loans allocate the finance charge across the term. For a 12-month loan the month weights 12+11+…+1 sum to 78 — hence the name. It front-loads interest: the lender counts more of it as "earned" early, so paying off a Rule-of-78 loan early rebates you less than simple interest would. The rebate (unearned interest) after m of n payments is total × (n−m)(n−m+1) / [n(n+1)]. Many jurisdictions restrict or ban it for longer loans; this tool shows exactly what it costs.
