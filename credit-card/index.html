How validation works

Card numbers carry a built-in Luhn check digit: double every second digit from the right, sum the digits, and a valid number totals a multiple of 10. That catches typos but does not mean the card exists or has funds. The brand is read from the leading digits (the IIN/BIN range): 4 → Visa, 51–55 and 2221–2720 → Mastercard, 34/37 → American Express, and so on. These are the industry test numbers — safe, non-real. Nothing you type is sent anywhere; it all runs locally.
