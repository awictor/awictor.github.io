About decibels

A decibel is a logarithmic ratio. For power quantities (watts, intensity): dB = 10·log₁₀(P/P₀). For amplitude quantities (voltage, pressure): dB = 20·log₁₀(A/A₀), because power scales with amplitude squared. That's why doubling power adds ~3 dB but doubling amplitude adds ~6 dB. To combine independent sound sources you add their powers, not their dB values: two equal sources give +3 dB, four give +6 dB. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
