How much fresh air a room needs

Air changes per hour (ACH) is how many times a room's entire air volume is replaced each hour. Airflow in cubic feet per minute is CFM = ACH × volume ÷ 60. Typical targets: bedrooms 5–6, kitchens 7–8, bathrooms 8–10, and 6+ is often cited for reducing airborne-illness risk. So a 15×12×8 ft room (1,440 ft³) at 6 ACH needs 144 CFM, replacing the air every 10 minutes. Match this against your fan or HVAC rating. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
