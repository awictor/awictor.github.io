OEE

Three losses in one number

OEE multiplies three factors: Availability (run time ÷ planned production time — downtime loss), Performance (ideal cycle × total parts ÷ run time — speed loss), and Quality (good parts ÷ total parts — defect loss). OEE = A × P × Q. A perfect score is 100%; 85% is considered world-class for discrete manufacturing, and 60% is typical. Because it's a product, one weak factor drags the whole score down — which is exactly what makes it useful for spotting the biggest loss. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
