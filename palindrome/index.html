What counts as a palindrome?

A palindrome reads the same in both directions. This checker ignores case, spaces and punctuation, so "A man, a plan, a canal: Panama" counts. It also scans your text for individual palindromic words like level, noon, kayak and racecar. Numbers work too (12321). Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
