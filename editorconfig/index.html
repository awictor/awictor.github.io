About EditorConfig

.editorconfig lives at your project root and tells every EditorConfig-aware editor how to format files — indent style and size, character set, line endings, and whether to trim trailing whitespace or add a final newline — so a whole team stays consistent regardless of editor. root = true stops the search walking up into parent directories. Sections use glob patterns like [*] (all files) or [*.md] (overrides for one type). Everything is generated locally; nothing leaves your browser.
