How it works

What the timer adjusts for
  • Baseline (large egg, from the fridge, at sea level): soft 6 min, medium 8 min, hard 12 min in already-boiling water.
  • Egg size scales the time — smaller eggs cook faster, jumbos slower.
  • Room-temperature eggs start warmer, so they need about 15% less time and are less likely to crack.
  • Altitude lowers water's boiling point by roughly 1 °C per 285 m, so cooler water needs proportionally longer.
  • Drop eggs into water at a rolling boil, then start the timer. Everything runs in your browser.
