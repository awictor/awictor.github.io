The formulas

A simply supported beam with a central point load deflects δ = P·L³ ÷ (48·E·I); under a uniform load it's 5·w·L⁴ ÷ (384·E·I). A cantilever with an end load deflects P·L³ ÷ (3·E·I) — sixteen times more than the same load supported at both ends. Here E is the modulus of elasticity, I the moment of inertia (for a rectangle, b·h³ ÷ 12). Keep force, length, E, and I in consistent units. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
