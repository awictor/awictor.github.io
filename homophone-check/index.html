How it works

This flags words that have common homophones or are frequently confused — their/there/they're, your/you're, to/too/two, affect/effect, lose/loose, and dozens more — so you can pause and confirm you picked the right one. It doesn't judge whether you're correct (that needs the meaning); it just highlights the risky words and shows the alternatives. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
