How it works

Energy is power over time: kWh = watts × hours ÷ 1000. Multiply by your electricity rate for the running cost. A 1,000 W space heater run 3 hours a day uses 3 kWh — about 45¢ a day at 15¢/kWh, or roughly $164 a year. Check the nameplate wattage on the appliance, and remember that things like fridges cycle on and off, so use their average draw rather than the peak.
