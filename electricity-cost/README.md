# Electricity Cost Calculator

Work out what an appliance costs to run: daily **kWh** and daily, monthly, and yearly **cost** from its wattage, hours of use, and your electricity rate.

**[Open the tool →](https://awictor.github.io/electricity-cost/)**

- kWh = watts × hours ÷ 1000
- Daily, monthly (30 d), and annual cost
- Any currency (uses your per-kWh rate)
- Dark mode, 100% offline, no dependencies, no tracking

## Example

A 1,000 W heater run 3 h/day at $0.15/kWh costs $0.45/day — about $164/year.
