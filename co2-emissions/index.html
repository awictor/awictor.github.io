The factors

Burning one gallon of gasoline releases about 8.887 kg of CO₂, so driving emissions are miles ÷ MPG × 8.887. Air travel is roughly 0.15 kg per passenger-mile. Electricity depends on your grid — around 0.4 kg per kWh on a mixed grid, near zero on renewables. A mature tree absorbs about 21 kg of CO₂ a year, which is how many trees it would take to offset your total. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
