About proportions

A proportion states that two ratios are equal: a/b = c/d. If you know three of the four values, the fourth follows by cross-multiplication — since a·d = b·c, any missing term is just the product of the diagonal divided by the known same-row value. This "rule of three" is everywhere: scaling recipes, converting map distances, resizing images (16/9 = 1920/1080), mixing ratios, and unit pricing.
