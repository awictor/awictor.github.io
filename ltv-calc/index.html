What LTV means

LTV = loan balance ÷ property value. Lenders use it to gauge risk: the lower your LTV, the more equity cushion. On conventional mortgages, private mortgage insurance (PMI) is generally required above 80% LTV and can be removed once you drop below it — so the "pay down to reach threshold" figure shows how much principal to pay off to cancel PMI. Combined LTV (CLTV) adds a second mortgage or HELOC to the numerator. Not financial advice. Everything runs locally.
