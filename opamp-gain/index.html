Inverting vs non-inverting

A non-inverting amplifier has gain 1 + Rf/Rin — always at least 1, and in phase with the input. An inverting amplifier has gain −Rf/Rin, flipping the signal and able to attenuate below unity. Gain in decibels is 20·log₁₀|gain|, so ×10 is 20 dB and ×100 is 40 dB. Setting Rf = 0 in the non-inverting form gives a unity buffer. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
