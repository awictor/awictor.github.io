About red blood cell indices

The RBC indices describe the size and hemoglobin content of red cells and help classify anemia. MCV (mean corpuscular volume) is average cell size: Hct% ÷ RBC × 10. MCH (mean corpuscular hemoglobin) is average hemoglobin per cell: Hgb ÷ RBC × 10. MCHC (mean corpuscular hemoglobin concentration): Hgb ÷ Hct × 100. By MCV, anemia is microcytic (<80 fL), normocytic (80–100), or macrocytic (>100). Educational aid, not medical advice. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
