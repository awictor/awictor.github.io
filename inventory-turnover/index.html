What these mean

Inventory turnover is how many times you sell through and replace your stock in a period: COGS ÷ average inventory. Higher usually means leaner, faster-moving stock (and less cash tied up), while very high can signal stockouts. Days sales of inventory (DSI) flips that into an intuition — the average number of days a unit sits before selling: period ÷ turnover. For Amazon/FBA sellers, watching DSI helps balance restock timing against storage fees. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
