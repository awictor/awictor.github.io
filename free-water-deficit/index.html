About

Free water deficit ≈ TBW × (current Na ÷ target Na − 1), where TBW = weight × the body-water fraction. It estimates the water needed to bring sodium down to target in hypernatremia. Correct slowly — generally no faster than ~10 mEq/L per 24 h — and account for ongoing losses; this is a starting estimate, not a prescription. Education only.
