sell-through rate

What these mean

Sell-through rate is the share of received inventory that sold in a period: units sold ÷ units received. High sell-through means demand is outpacing supply (watch for stockouts); low means overstock. Weeks of supply is how long current stock lasts at the recent sales pace (stock ÷ weekly sales). When weeks of supply drops below your lead time + safety stock, it's time to reorder.
