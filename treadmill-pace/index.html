How it works

  • Pace per mile = 60 ÷ MPH. At 6.0 MPH that's a 10:00 mile; at 10.0 MPH it's 6:00.
  • Pace per km uses km/h (MPH × 1.609), so the per-km pace is always a smaller number than per-mile.
  • Treadmill belts read a touch fast and have no wind resistance — a 1% incline roughly matches flat outdoor effort.
  • Use this to hit a target race pace on the belt, or to translate an outdoor pace to a belt speed.
