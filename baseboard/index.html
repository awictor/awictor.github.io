How to measure trim

Baseboard, crown, and other trim run around the perimeter of the room, 2 × (length + width), minus the width of any doorways that break the run. Add a waste allowance — usually 10% — because trim is mitered at corners and cut to length, and you'll want offcuts long enough to reuse. Then divide by your stick length (trim commonly comes in 8, 12, or 16 ft pieces) and round up. Buy from the same lot so the profile and color match, and account for return runs and closets separately. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
