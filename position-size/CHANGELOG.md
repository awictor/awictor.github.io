# Changelog

## 0.1.0
- First release. Trading position size & risk calculator.
- Position sizing from account / risk% / entry / stop, rounded down to stay within budget.
- Long & short support; amount at risk, risk per unit, position value, % of account.
- Optional target for risk:reward ratio and total potential reward.
- Dark mode, localStorage memory.
- Headless test suite (11 checks, risk-budget sweep) + CI.
