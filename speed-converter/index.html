About these units

Speed is distance over time, and the unit you use depends on the domain: m/s is the SI standard (physics), km/h and mph are everyday road speeds, knots (nautical miles per hour) rule aviation and shipping, and ft/s shows up in US engineering. The conversions are exact by definition — 1 mile = 1609.344 m, 1 nautical mile = 1852 m, 1 foot = 0.3048 m — so, for example, 100 km/h is exactly 62.137 mph.
