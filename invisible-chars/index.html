What it catches

Zero-width spaces/joiners (U+200B–200D), word joiner & BOM (U+2060, U+FEFF), soft hyphen (U+00AD), non-breaking & exotic spaces (U+00A0, U+2000–200A, U+202F, U+205F, U+3000), and bidirectional control marks (U+200E–200F, U+202A–202E, U+2066–2069). Cleaning removes zero-width and directional marks and normalizes exotic spaces to a regular space.
