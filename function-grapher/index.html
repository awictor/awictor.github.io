Function Grapher

plot y = f(x)

About

Type any function of x and see it plotted over your chosen range. The curve is sampled at hundreds of points, the y-axis auto-scales to fit, and the graph is drawn as crisp SVG (no bitmap). Discontinuities and undefined regions (like 1/x at 0 or sqrt(x) below 0) break the line rather than drawing false verticals. Supports sin cos tan exp ln log sqrt abs and constants pi, e — parsed by a real recursive-descent parser, no eval(). Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
