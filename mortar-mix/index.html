How it works

  • Concrete block: about 3 bags of mortar per 100 block. Brick: about 7 bags per 1,000 brick.
  • Sand runs roughly 1 ton per 8 bags of mortar mix — buy it by the ton for anything sizable.
  • Counts round up: you can't buy a partial bag, and a 10% waste cushion is baked into the rules.
  • Joint thickness and block size shift these numbers, so treat the result as a solid starting estimate.
