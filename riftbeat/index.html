HOW TO PLAY

Notes fall down neon lanes toward the judgment line. Hit the matching key exactly when a note crosses the line. Timing is graded Perfect / Great / Good / Miss.
Keys by lane count
4 lanes: D F J K
5 lanes: D F Space J K
6 lanes: S D F J K L
You can also tap / click the lanes with mouse or touch.
Pause Esc · Restart R
The chiptune is composed in code from your seed, and the falling chart is extracted from that synth's own scheduled note-onsets — so notes always land on the beat because they are the beat.

LATENCY CALIBRATION

Tap Space (or click) on every metronome click. We measure your audio + input delay.
