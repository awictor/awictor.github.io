How it works

  • What a pattern really needs is a total yardage, not a skein count — skein sizes differ between yarns.
  • Total yards = pattern skeins × yards per skein. Then substitute skeins = total ÷ yards per new skein, rounded up.
  • A generous new skein (more yards) means fewer balls; a smaller one means more. Never round down — you can't buy part of a skein and dye lots may not match later.
  • Add ~10% for swatching, seaming, and coming up short — more for colorwork or if you can't re-buy the dye lot.
  • Match yarn weight and gauge too, not just yardage, or the finished size will drift.
