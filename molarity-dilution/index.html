About these calculations

Molarity (M) is moles of solute per litre of solution: M = n / V. To make a dilute solution from a concentrated stock, use C₁V₁ = C₂V₂ — the amount of solute stays the same, so the final volume is V₂ = C₁V₁ / C₂ and you top up with solvent. To weigh out a target number of moles, divide the desired mass by the compound's molar mass (e.g. NaCl is 58.44 g/mol, so 58.44 g is exactly one mole).
