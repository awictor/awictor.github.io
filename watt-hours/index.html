Watt-hours, mAh, and pack math

Energy in watt-hours is capacity times voltage: Wh = mAh × V ÷ 1000. That's why you can't compare mAh across different voltages — a 10,000 mAh power bank at 3.7 V holds 37 Wh, the airline carry-on limit is 100 Wh. In a pack, cells in series add voltage (S × cell V) and cells in parallel add capacity (P × cell mAh); total energy multiplies both. A 3S2P pack of 3500 mAh 3.7 V cells is 11.1 V, 7000 mAh, ~77.7 Wh.
