What is Zalgo text?

"Zalgo" text stacks lots of Unicode combining diacritical marks (U+0300–U+036F) on top of ordinary letters, so the characters appear to melt and drip outside their line — the internet's "he comes" cursed-text meme. It's real Unicode, so it pastes into most apps (though some strip or clamp it). Turn the intensity up for more chaos, or use Clean to strip all combining marks and get the original text back. Everything runs locally.
