Pixels, inches, and DPI

Print size is just pixels ÷ DPI: a 6000 px image at 300 DPI prints 20 inches wide. Photo labs like 300 DPI for sharp prints, but big wall art viewed from afar is fine at 150 or even less. To fill a target size, you need inches × DPI pixels; blow an image up bigger than it has pixels for and the effective DPI falls, softening the print. Megapixels are just width × height ÷ a million.
