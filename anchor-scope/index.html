How anchor scope works

Scope is the ratio of rode let out to the vertical distance from the bow roller to the seabed — that's the water depth plus your bow height, measured at high tide so you don't come up short as the water rises. A low, flat angle lets the anchor dig in and hold; a steep angle pulls it up and out. The common guidance is 5:1 in calm conditions and 7:1 or more when it's blowing (all-chain rode can hold at a bit less because its weight flattens the pull). So rode = scope × (depth + bow height). Remember you'll swing in a circle of that radius, so check for room. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
