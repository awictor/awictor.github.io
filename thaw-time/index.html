How it works

  • Refrigerator: about 24 hours per 5 lb (≈4.8 hr/lb). Steady and safest — meat stays below 40 °F the whole time.
  • Cold-water bath: about 30 minutes per pound in a leak-proof bag submerged in cold water, changing the water every 30 minutes.
  • USDA guidance: cook cold-water-thawed meat immediately; refrigerator-thawed meat can be held a day or two.
  • Never thaw on the counter — the surface enters the 40–140 °F danger zone while the center is still frozen.
