How it works

  • Weight = volume × density. Volume (ft³) = (thickness ÷ 12) × (width ÷ 12) × length in feet.
  • Densities are typical at about 12% moisture (kiln-dried). Green (fresh-cut) lumber can weigh 50% more.
  • Use actual dressed dimensions — a "2×6" is really 1.5" × 5.5" once surfaced.
  • Board feet = thickness × width × length(ft) ÷ 12, the standard unit lumber is priced in.
