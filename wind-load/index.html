How it works

Dynamic pressure & drag
  • Dynamic wind pressure is q = 0.00256 × V² psf (V in mph), or q = 0.613 × V² Pa (V in m/s) — the ASCE 7 air-density constants.
  • Force on a surface is F = q × A × Cd, where Cd is the shape's drag coefficient (~1.2 flat wall, ~1.5 sign, ~2.0 open frame).
  • Pressure rises with the square of wind speed — doubling the wind quadruples the load.
  • This is a quick estimate; real code design adds exposure, gust, height, and importance factors. Everything runs in your browser.
