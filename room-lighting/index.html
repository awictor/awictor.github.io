How to light a room

Lighting is planned in footcandles — lumens per square foot — so the total light a room needs is just area × footcandles. Recommended levels depend on the task: about 10–20 fc for living rooms and bedrooms, 30 for dining and bathrooms, 50 for kitchens, and 70+ for detailed workspaces. Divide the total by a bulb's lumen output (printed on the box, not the watts) and round up for the number of fixtures. Spread them out for even coverage, and layer ambient, task, and accent light rather than relying on one bright source. Dimmers let one install cover several levels.
