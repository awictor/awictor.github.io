How it works

  • Brew ratio = yield ÷ dose. 18 g in giving 36 g out is a 1:2 ratio — the classic modern shot.
  • Ristretto (under 1:2) is short and intense; normale (about 1:2 to 1:3) is balanced; lungo (over 1:3) is long and lighter.
  • Weigh both the dry grounds and the liquid in the cup — volume ("2 oz") is unreliable because crema varies.
  • Aim to hit your target yield in roughly 25–32 seconds; adjust grind finer to slow it down, coarser to speed it up.
