# Espresso Ratio ☕

Dial in espresso with a **brew-ratio calculator**: dose in, yield out, and the ratio between them, with ristretto/normale/lungo guidance. Single offline HTML file, no dependencies, no data leaves your device.

## Features

- Solve any of the three: ratio, yield, or dose.
- Classifies the shot as ristretto, normale, or lungo.
- Weight-based (the reliable way to dial in) with brew-time guidance.
- Dark mode, mobile friendly, works fully offline.

## Usage

Open `index.html` in any browser. Pick what to solve for and enter the knowns.

> Weigh both the grounds and the liquid in the cup — volume is unreliable because crema varies.
