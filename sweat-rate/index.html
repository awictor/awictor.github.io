How it works

  • Weigh yourself (ideally nude, towelled dry) before and after a workout, and track fluid you drank.
  • Sweat lost = weight lost + fluid drunk (1 kg of body weight ≈ 1 liter of sweat). Divide by hours for the rate.
  • Because you replaced some fluid, drinking more actually reveals a higher true sweat rate.
  • Aim to drink close to your sweat rate on long efforts, but don't exceed it — over-drinking risks hyponatremia.
  • Sweat rate varies with heat, humidity, and intensity, so re-measure across conditions.
