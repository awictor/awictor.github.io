Why frame time matters

Frame rate and frame time are two views of the same thing: ms per frame = 1000 ÷ FPS. At 60 FPS each frame must be drawn in 16.7 ms, at 120 FPS in 8.3 ms, and at 30 FPS in 33.3 ms. Thinking in milliseconds is how engine and game developers reason about performance, because a fixed budget makes the cost of each system (physics, AI, rendering) concrete — shaving 2 ms off a 16.7 ms frame is a big deal. Smoothness depends on consistent frame times as much as the average: a steady 60 FPS feels better than one that spikes. Total frames over a clip is simply FPS × seconds.
