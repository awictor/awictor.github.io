How the numbers are figured

Publishers count a "standard manuscript page" as 250 words regardless of formatting, so pages = words ÷ 250. Length categories follow the SFWA definitions: flash fiction under 1,000 · short story 1,000–7,499 · novelette 7,500–17,499 · novella 17,500–39,999 · novel 40,000+. Reading time assumes ~238 words/min (silent adult reading); speaking time ~130 words/min (a comfortable narration pace). Everything runs locally.
