Truth Table Generator

boolean logic expression evaluator

About

Type any boolean expression and get its complete truth table — every combination of inputs and the resulting output. Operators can be words or symbols: and/&&/&, or/||/|, not/!/~, and xor/^, with parentheses. Precedence is NOT → AND → XOR → OR (parenthesize to override). Variables are any letters — A, B, P, Q — and the table lists them alphabetically. Handy for digital-logic design, discrete math, and checking identities like De Morgan's laws. Everything is parsed and evaluated locally; nothing leaves your browser.
