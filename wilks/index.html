About the Wilks score

The Wilks score multiplies your total by a coefficient based on bodyweight, so lifters in different weight classes can be compared fairly. Lighter lifters get a higher coefficient (they're relatively stronger for their size). This uses the original Wilks formula (a 5th-degree polynomial). Roughly: 300 is strong, 400 is elite, 500+ is world-class.
