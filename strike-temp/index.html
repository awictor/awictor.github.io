How it works

  • Dry grain is cooler than your mash target, so it pulls heat out of the water. You compensate by heating the strike water a bit above the mash temp.
  • Palmer's infusion formula: strike °F = (0.2 ÷ R)(target − grain) + target, where R is the water-to-grain ratio in quarts per pound.
  • A thicker mash (less water per pound) needs a bigger overshoot; a thinner mash needs less.
  • Warm your mash tun first — a cold vessel steals heat the formula doesn't account for. Many brewers add 1–2 °F for that.
  • Stir well and check within a minute; you can correct with a splash of boiling or cold water if you miss.
