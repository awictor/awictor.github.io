Cords vs face cords

A full cord is a stack measuring 4 ft × 4 ft × 8 ft = 128 cubic feet of wood. A face cord (a.k.a. rick) is a stack 4 ft high × 8 ft long, but only as deep as the logs are long — so with common 16-inch logs, three face cords make one full cord. Enter the log length as the stack depth (in inches) and you'll get both. Depth is entered in inches for convenience and converted to feet internally. Everything runs locally.
