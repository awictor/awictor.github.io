About gear ratios

A gear ratio is the driven (output) tooth count divided by the drive (input) tooth count: ratio = driven / drive. A ratio above 1 is a reduction — the output turns slower but with proportionally more torque; below 1 is an overdrive, faster but with less torque. Output speed = input speed ÷ ratio, and output torque = input torque × ratio, so (ignoring losses) power is conserved. For a compound gear train, multiply the ratios of each stage. That's how a 10T→40T then 20T→40T train gives an 8:1 overall reduction. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
