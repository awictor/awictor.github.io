What's an "echo"?

An echo is the same word popping up again within a few words — usually accidental, and it makes writing feel repetitive ("the report reported… in the report… reported"). Editors hunt these down and vary the wording. Widen the window to catch echoes that are further apart; raise min length to ignore short words; "ignore common words" skips the/and/of and similar, since those naturally recur.
