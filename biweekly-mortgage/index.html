Why biweekly pays off sooner

Instead of 12 monthly payments a year, you pay half your monthly amount every two weeks. There are 52 weeks, so that's 26 half-payments = 13 full payments a year — one extra payment that goes straight at the principal. Over a 30-year loan that typically knocks off several years and tens of thousands in interest. Confirm your lender applies biweekly payments to principal (and doesn't charge a fee) before signing up — or just make one extra payment yourself each year. Everything runs locally.
