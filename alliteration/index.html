About alliteration

Alliteration is the repetition of the same starting sound across nearby words — "Peter Piper picked a peck of pickled peppers". It makes phrases catchy, which is why it's everywhere in poetry, headlines, brand names and tongue-twisters. This finder groups consecutive words that share a first letter into runs; raise the minimum run to show only stronger alliteration, and "skip small words" ignores the/a/of so they don't break a run. It matches by letter (not phonetics), so "phone/fun" won't group. Everything runs locally.
