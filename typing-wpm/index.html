How it works

  • A "word" is standardized as 5 characters, so WPM = (characters ÷ 5) ÷ minutes.
  • Net WPM subtracts your error rate from gross speed, so accuracy matters as much as raw pace.
  • Accuracy is the share of characters that match the target text so far.
  • Average adult typing is ~40 WPM; 60–70 is fast; pro typists hit 100+.
