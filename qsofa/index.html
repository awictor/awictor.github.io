About qSOFA

qSOFA (quick Sepsis-related Organ Failure Assessment) is a fast bedside flag for patients with suspected infection who may be at higher risk of poor outcomes. One point each for: respiratory rate ≥ 22, altered mentation (GCS < 15), and systolic blood pressure ≤ 100 mmHg. A score of ≥ 2 is associated with markedly higher in-hospital mortality and should prompt closer assessment for sepsis and organ dysfunction. A screening aid, not a diagnosis of sepsis.
