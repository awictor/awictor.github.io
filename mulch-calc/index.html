Tips

Volume = area × depth. Mulch is usually spread 2–3 in deep (deeper smothers roots); topsoil/compost 1–3 in for top-dressing. Bulk material is sold by the cubic yard (1 yd³ = 27 ft³ ≈ covers 100 ft² at ~3¼ in); bagged mulch is commonly 2 cu ft per bag. Order ~10% extra for settling and uneven ground.
