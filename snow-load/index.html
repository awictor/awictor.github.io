How heavy is snow?

Roof snow load is depth (ft) × density — fresh light snow is around 5–8 lb/ft³, settled snow 15–20, and wet or icy snow 30+. So 2 ft of 15 lb/ft³ snow is about 30 psf, and on a 1,500 ft² roof that's over 20 tons. The water equivalent is the depth times the snow-to-water density ratio. Most homes are designed for 20–40 psf; check your local code and clear the roof if you're near the limit. Estimates only — consult an engineer for safety decisions.
