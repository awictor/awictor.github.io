# Snow Load Calculator

Estimate **roof snow load** (psf) from snow depth and density, the **total load** on a roof area, and the **water equivalent**.

**[Open the tool →](https://awictor.github.io/snow-load/)**

- Load = depth (ft) × density
- Total roof load and snow water equivalent
- Solve depth for a target load
- Dark mode, 100% offline, no dependencies, no tracking

## Example

2 ft of 15 lb/ft³ snow ≈ 30 psf; on a 1,500 ft² roof that's ~22.5 tons. Estimates only — consult an engineer.
