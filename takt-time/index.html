The heartbeat of lean production

Takt time is the rhythm you must produce at to exactly meet demand: available time ÷ customer demand. If you have 450 productive minutes and need 90 units, takt is 5 minutes per unit — one every 5 minutes, no faster, no slower. Compare it to your actual cycle time: if cycle ≤ takt you can keep up; if cycle > takt you're a bottleneck and will fall behind. Producing faster than takt just builds excess inventory. Use net available time (subtract breaks and planned stops). Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
