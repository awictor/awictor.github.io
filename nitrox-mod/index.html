How it works

  • Oxygen becomes toxic at high partial pressure. Divers cap the ppO₂ at 1.4 ATA for working depth (1.6 for deco/contingency).
  • ppO₂ = O₂ fraction × (depth / 10 + 1) in metres. Set it to the limit and solve for depth: MOD = 10 × (ppO₂max ÷ FO₂ − 1).
  • A richer mix (more O₂) has a shallower MOD — EAN36 tops out well above EAN32.
  • Best mix flips it around: the richest blend whose ppO₂ hits your limit exactly at the planned depth, for maximum no-deco time.
  • Planning aid only. Analyze every cylinder yourself and dive within your training and tables/computer.
