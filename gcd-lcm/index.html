GCD and LCM

The greatest common divisor is the largest integer that divides all your numbers evenly — the key to reducing fractions to lowest terms. The least common multiple is the smallest positive integer they all divide into — used to find a common denominator or align repeating schedules. They're computed with the ancient Euclidean algorithm, and for two numbers they satisfy the tidy identity GCD(a,b) × LCM(a,b) = a × b. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
