Remove Diacritics

Strip accents down to plain ASCII

What this does

This strips diacritics — the accents, tildes, cedillas, and umlauts on letters — leaving the closest plain-ASCII letter: café → cafe, Zürich → Zurich, piñata → pinata. It works by Unicode NFD decomposition (splitting a letter from its accent, then dropping the accent), plus a small map for letters that don't decompose, like ø → o, ß → ss, and æ → ae. Handy for making URL slugs, sortable keys, and accent-insensitive search. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
