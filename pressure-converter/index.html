About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area; the SI unit is the pascal (Pa = 1 N/m²), but it's tiny, so real-world work uses multiples and legacy units. One standard atmosphere (atm) is defined as exactly 101,325 Pa = 1.01325 bar = 760 mmHg ≈ 14.696 psi. bar is common in Europe and weather, psi in the US (tyres, plumbing), and mmHg/inHg in medicine and barometry. This tool converts through pascals so every unit stays consistent. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
