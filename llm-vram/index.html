Will it fit on your card?

The weights alone take parameters (billions) × bytes-per-parameter gigabytes — so a 7B model is 14 GB at FP16, 7 GB at INT8, or 3.5 GB at INT4. Quantizing trades a little quality for a big memory cut, which is how large models run on consumer GPUs. Add overhead (~20%) for the KV cache, activations, and CUDA context; longer contexts need more. So a 7B FP16 model wants ~17 GB and just fits a 24 GB card with room for context. A rough planning estimate, not exact. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
