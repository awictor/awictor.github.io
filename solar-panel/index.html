How sizing works

Divide your daily kWh by the peak sun hours (the equivalent hours of full-strength sun for your location) and by a system efficiency of about 0.75–0.85 to cover inverter, wiring, and heat losses — that's the array wattage you need. Divide by your panel rating and round up for the panel count. A battery bank to carry one day is daily Wh ÷ (voltage × usable depth of discharge) in amp-hours.
