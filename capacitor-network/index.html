Capacitors combine the opposite way from resistors

In parallel, capacitances add: C = C₁ + C₂ + … — the plate area effectively grows. In series, the reciprocals add: 1/C = 1/C₁ + 1/C₂ + …, so the total is smaller than the smallest capacitor (and the voltage rating goes up). Stored energy is E = ½ C V². This is the mirror image of resistor networks, where series adds and parallel divides. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
