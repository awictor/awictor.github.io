# Changelog

## v0.1.0 — 2026-09-11

Initial release.

- Newton's gravitational force F = G·m₁·m₂/r² and gravitational field g = G·m/r²
- Presets for Earth–Moon, Earth–Sun, and two 1 kg masses
- Inverse-square and mass-linear behaviour, distance guards
- Dark mode with persistence, fully offline, no dependencies
- 10 self-tests including two-1kg = G, Earth–Moon force, and 9.8 m/s² surface gravity
