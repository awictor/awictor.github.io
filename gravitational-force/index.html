About Newton's law of gravitation

Every pair of masses attracts with a force F = G·m₁·m₂ / r², where G = 6.674×10⁻¹¹ N·m²/kg² is the gravitational constant and r is the centre-to-centre distance. The force is inverse-square: double the distance and it drops to a quarter. The gravitational field (acceleration) a mass m produces at distance r is g = G·m/r² — plug in Earth's mass and radius and you get 9.8 m/s². Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
