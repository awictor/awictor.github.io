How it works

Take the angle between the wind and the runway centerline. The crosswind component is wind speed × sin(angle) and the headwind component is wind speed × cos(angle) — a negative headwind is a tailwind. A runway "27" points to 270°, so a wind from 300° hits it at 30°, giving half its speed as crosswind. Compare the crosswind to your aircraft's demonstrated limit before you commit.
