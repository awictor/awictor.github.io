How the estimate works

Runtime is capacity ÷ load, in the same time base as the current (mAh ÷ mA = hours). Real batteries never deliver their full rating, so multiply by a derating factor — 0.8 to 0.9 is realistic for steady loads. Energy in watt-hours is capacity (Ah) × voltage. Wiring cells in parallel adds their capacities while keeping voltage the same, extending runtime proportionally. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
