Designing the spine

A print book's spine has to be exactly as wide as the closed page block, or the cover art wraps wrong. Width is just page count ÷ pages-per-inch (PPI), where PPI is a property of the paper stock — printers publish it, and typical values run ~370 (thick) to ~512 (thin), with 444 common for white 50 lb text. Count every sheet's two sides as the page count, use the actual paper your printer will use, and add nothing for the cover itself (that's separate). Below about 80–100 pages many printers can't print spine text at all. One inch is 72 points and 25.4 mm. Always confirm against your printer's own spine spec before finalizing artwork.
