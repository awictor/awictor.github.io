About divisor functions

The divisor sum σ(n) adds up every divisor of n (including 1 and n itself), and τ(n) counts them. The aliquot sum is σ(n) − n — the sum of the proper divisors. Comparing it to n classifies the number: perfect if the proper divisors sum to exactly n (6 = 1+2+3, 28, 496…), abundant if they exceed it (12), and deficient if they fall short (every prime, and every power of 2). σ is multiplicative: σ(12) = σ(4)·σ(3) = 7·4 = 28.
