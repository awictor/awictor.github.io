How per diem adds up

Per diem covers meals & incidentals (M&IE) plus lodging. Under US GSA rules the first and last day of travel pay only 75% of the daily M&IE rate, since you're not away the full day; days in between pay 100%. So a 5-day trip at $60/day is 2 × $45 + 3 × $60 = $270 in M&IE. Lodging is billed per night at the location's cap. Rates vary by city and season — check the official schedule for your destination.
