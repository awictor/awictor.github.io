# Per Diem Calculator

Calculate total travel **per diem** from a daily M&IE rate and trip length — with 75% first/last travel days — plus lodging.

**[Open the tool →](https://awictor.github.io/per-diem/)**

- M&IE with GSA-style 75% travel days (first and last)
- Lodging total and combined trip total
- Adjustable travel-day rate
- Dark mode, 100% offline, no dependencies, no tracking

## Example

5 days at $60 M&IE → 2×$45 + 3×$60 = $270; with 4 nights lodging at $150 → $870 total.
