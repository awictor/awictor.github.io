# Air Fryer Converter 🍟

Convert conventional **oven temperature and time to air fryer settings** (and back). Single offline HTML file, no dependencies, no data leaves your device.

## Features

- Standard rule: drop temperature 25 °F and cut time ~20%.
- Works both directions: oven → air fryer and air fryer → oven.
- Dark mode, mobile friendly, works fully offline.

## Usage

Open `index.html` in any browser. Pick a direction and enter the temperature and time.

> Air fryers run hot and vary — check food early and shake or flip halfway.
