How it works

  • Air fryers are small convection ovens, so they cook hotter and faster.
  • The standard rule: drop the temperature by 25 °F and cut the time by about 20%.
  • Check food early the first time — air fryers vary, and smaller baskets brown faster.
  • Shake or flip halfway through for even results, especially with fries and small pieces.
