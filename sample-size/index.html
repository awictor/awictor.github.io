About sample size

To estimate a proportion within a margin of error e at a chosen confidence level, you need n = z²·p·(1−p) / e², where z is the confidence z-score (1.96 for 95%) and p the expected proportion. Using p = 50% is the safe, most-demanding choice. For a known population, the finite-population correction n / (1 + (n−1)/N) shrinks the requirement. That's why national polls need only ~1,000 people for a ±3% margin regardless of country size. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
