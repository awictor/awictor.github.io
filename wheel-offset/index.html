Offset vs. backspacing

Both describe where a wheel's mounting face sits relative to its width, just from different reference points. Offset (stamped in mm as ET) is the distance from the wheel's centerline to the hub mounting face: positive pulls the wheel inward (tuck), negative pushes it outward (poke). Backspacing is measured in inches from the mounting face to the inner lip: backspacing = width ÷ 2 + offset, and front spacing is the mirror, width ÷ 2 − offset (they add up to the full width). More positive offset means more backspacing and less clearance to suspension; more negative means the wheel sticks out toward the fender.
