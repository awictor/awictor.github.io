Why it moves: each ring repeats a four-tone ramp (black → dark → white → light). In your periphery — where vision is coarse and jittery — this asymmetric luminance step is misread as directional motion. Tiny involuntary eye movements (microsaccades) and blinks refresh the signal, so the "rotation" only lives outside your fovea. Nothing on screen ever changes. Reduce-motion is honored: jitter stays off and the field is a true still image.
