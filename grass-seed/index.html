How to seed the right amount

Seed is sold by weight and applied at a rate per 1000 sq ft, so pounds = area ÷ 1000 × rate. Rates depend on the job and the grass: overseeding a thin lawn takes roughly half the rate of a brand-new lawn, and fine seeds like Kentucky bluegrass go down lighter than big Kentucky-31 tall fescue seed — always check the bag for the species-specific rate. Too little seed leaves gaps; too much makes seedlings compete and thin out. Measure your area (length × width, minus beds and paths), keep the seed-to-soil contact good, and water lightly and often until it germinates. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
