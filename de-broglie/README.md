# de Broglie Wavelength Calculator

Compute the **de Broglie (matter) wavelength** `λ = h/p` of any particle — from momentum, from mass and velocity, or from kinetic energy. One-tap presets for electron, proton, neutron, and alpha particle.

**[Open the tool →](https://awictor.github.io/de-broglie/)**

- Three input modes: mass & velocity, mass & kinetic energy, or momentum
- Reports wavelength (m and nm) plus momentum
- Particle presets with 2019 SI masses
- Dark mode with persistence
- 100% offline, no dependencies, no tracking

## Example

An electron at 1×10⁶ m/s has a wavelength of ≈ 0.727 nm — comparable to atomic spacings, the basis of electron microscopy.
