The Saffir-Simpson scale

The Saffir-Simpson scale rates a hurricane by its 1-minute sustained wind speed. Below 39 mph it's a tropical depression; 39–73 mph a tropical storm; then the five hurricane categories: Cat 1 74–95, Cat 2 96–110, Cat 3 111–129, Cat 4 130–156, and Cat 5 157+ mph. Categories 3 and up are "major" hurricanes, capable of devastating damage. The scale only measures wind — it doesn't capture storm surge, rainfall flooding, or tornadoes, which cause most hurricane deaths, so a "lower" category storm can still be deadly.
