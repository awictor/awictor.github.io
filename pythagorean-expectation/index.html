Pythagorean Expectation

Expected win % from points scored & allowed

How it works

Bill James's Pythagorean formula
  • Expected win % = scored^x ÷ (scored^x + allowed^x) — a team's record tracks how many points it scores versus allows.
  • The exponent is tuned per sport: baseball ≈ 1.83, the NFL ≈ 2.37, the NBA ≈ 13.91 (where scoring margins are far more predictive).
  • Multiply by games played to get expected wins — teams beating this tend to regress; teams below it tend to improve.
  • Equal scoring and allowing lands at exactly .500. Everything runs in your browser.
