GDD for the day

Heat units that drive growth

Plants and insects develop with accumulated warmth, not calendar days. Each day's GDD is (high + low) / 2 − base, never below zero. The base temperature is the threshold below which the organism doesn't grow (often 10 °C / 50 °F for warm-season crops). An optional upper cap limits extreme highs, since growth plateaus in intense heat. Sum GDD over the season to predict flowering, harvest, or pest emergence. Works with either °C or °F as long as the base uses the same scale. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
