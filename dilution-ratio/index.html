Reading dilution ratios

A ratio like 1:10 means 1 part concentrate to 10 parts water — 11 parts in total. So the concentrate you need is total ÷ (ratio + 1) and the rest is water. For 1100 mL at 1:10 that's 100 mL concentrate and 1000 mL water. The units don't matter as long as they match (mL, oz, cups). Note this is a "parts" ratio, not a percentage — a 1:1 mix is half and half, not pure concentrate. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
