How fertilizer rates work

Lawn fertilizer is dosed by pounds of actual nitrogen per 1000 sq ft — usually about 1 lb N per application. The bag's first N-P-K number is the percent nitrogen by weight, so a 20-5-10 product is 20% N. To deliver your target nitrogen you need product = target N ÷ (%N ÷ 100) per 1000 sq ft — a higher-nitrogen product means you spread less of it. Multiply by area ÷ 1000 for the total to buy and load in the spreader. Over-applying nitrogen burns the lawn and runs off into waterways, so split heavy feeding across the season and water it in.
