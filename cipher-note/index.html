CipherNote

Passphrase text encryption (AES-256-GCM)


    
🔒 Encryption uses AES-256-GCM with a key derived from your passphrase via
      PBKDF2 (100k iterations, SHA-256). Everything happens in your browser — nothing is uploaded.
      There is no password recovery: lose the passphrase and the note is gone.
