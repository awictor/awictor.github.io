# Work, Energy & Power Calculator

Compute **work** done by a force (`W = F·d·cosθ`), **power** (`P = W/t`), and **power from force & velocity** (`P = F·v`) — with horsepower output.

**[Open the tool →](https://awictor.github.io/work-energy/)**

- Work with an angle between force and motion
- Power in watts and horsepower
- Consistency between P = W/t and P = F·v
- Dark mode, 100% offline, no dependencies, no tracking

## Example

10 N over 5 m at 0° = **50 J**; 500 J in 10 s = **50 W** (≈ 0.067 hp). A perpendicular force does zero work.
