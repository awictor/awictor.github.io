About

Work is energy transferred by a force acting over a distance: W = F·d·cosθ, where θ is the angle between the force and the motion — a force perpendicular to motion (θ = 90°) does no work. Power is the rate of doing work: P = W/t, and equivalently P = F·v for a constant force. All in SI: joules and watts (1 horsepower ≈ 745.7 W). Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
