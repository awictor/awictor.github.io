# Changelog

## v0.1.0 — 2026-09-11

Initial release.

- Work W = F·d·cosθ, power P = W/t, and P = F·v
- Horsepower conversion
- Angle handling (perpendicular force → zero work; opposing → negative)
- Dark mode with persistence, fully offline, no dependencies
- 10 self-tests including the cos-angle cases and P = W/t ↔ F·v consistency
