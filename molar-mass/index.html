Molar mass, atom by atom

The molar mass is the sum of every atom's standard atomic weight. Write the formula with element symbols capitalized correctly (Na, not NA), counts after each symbol, and parentheses for groups — Ca(OH)2 or Al2(SO4)3. Nested groups work too. Weights are IUPAC standard atomic weights; molar mass in g/mol is numerically equal to the average molecular mass in daltons. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
