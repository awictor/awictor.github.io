Matching your aim across setups

eDPI (effective DPI) = DPI × in-game sensitivity — the single number that lets you compare aim across mice and games. cm/360 is how far you physically move the mouse to spin a full circle: 360 × 2.54 / (yaw × DPI × sens), where the yaw constant is game-specific. To keep the exact same feel on a new DPI, scale sensitivity so eDPI stays constant. Lower eDPI (higher cm/360) suits precise flick-free aim; higher suits fast turns.
