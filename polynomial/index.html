About

Polynomials are entered as coefficient lists, lowest power first: 1, 2, 3 is 1 + 2x + 3x². Addition adds matching powers; multiplication convolves the coefficients; the derivative multiplies each term by its power and drops it one; the integral divides by the new power. Evaluation uses Horner's method — nesting as ((3x + 2)x + 1) — which is fast and numerically stable, with no eval(). Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
