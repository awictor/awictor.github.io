# Changelog

## v0.1.0 — 2026-09-11

Initial release.

- Polynomial add, multiply (convolution), derivative, and integral
- Horner-method evaluation (no eval)
- Coefficient parsing (low→high) and readable output
- Dark mode with persistence, fully offline, no dependencies
- 10 self-tests including (x+1)², derivative/integral round trip, and Horner correctness
