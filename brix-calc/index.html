Sugar, gravity, and alcohol

Degrees Brix is the percent sugar by weight in a solution — grapes are often picked around 22 °Bx. It maps to specific gravity (density relative to water): 22 °Bx ≈ 1.092 SG. Yeast turns that sugar into alcohol, so a single starting reading estimates the potential alcohol if fermented dry — roughly Brix × 0.55–0.59 for wine. Actual ABV depends on how far fermentation goes (measure final gravity too). A refractometer reads Brix; a hydrometer reads SG. Estimates only. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
