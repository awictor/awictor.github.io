About

For a rectangular tank, volume = length × width × height. A US gallon is 231 cubic inches; a litre is 1,000 cm³. Water weighs about 8.34 lb/gal (≈1 kg/L) — so a "20-gallon" tank holds roughly 167 lb of water alone. Add substrate, rock, and the tank itself when planning a stand or floor load. Real capacity is a bit less than the outside dimensions suggest (glass thickness, trim, fill line).
