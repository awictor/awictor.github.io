What is the digital root?

Add up a number's digits; if the total has more than one digit, add those, and repeat until a single digit remains — that's the digital root. The number of additions it takes is the additive persistence. There's a shortcut: the digital root equals 1 + (n − 1) mod 9 (0 for zero), which is why it's the basis of casting out nines, an old trick for checking arithmetic.
