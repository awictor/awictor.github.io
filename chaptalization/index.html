How it works

  • Underripe fruit makes low-sugar must that ferments to weak wine. Chaptalization adds sugar before fermentation to raise the finished alcohol.
  • Starting gravity gives the current potential alcohol: PA% ≈ (SG − 1) × 131.25.
  • Adding sugar raises it: about 16.8 g of sugar per liter lifts potential alcohol by 1% ABV.
  • So sugar to add = volume × (target − current) × 16.83 g. Dissolve fully and re-check gravity before pitching yeast.
  • Don't overshoot your yeast's alcohol tolerance, and note that some regions regulate or ban chaptalization.
