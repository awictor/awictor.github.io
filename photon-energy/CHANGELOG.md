# Changelog

## v0.1.0 — 2026-09-11

Initial release.

- Photon energy from wavelength or frequency (E = hf = hc/λ)
- Convert between nm, µm, THz, joules, and electronvolts
- Visible-spectrum colour swatch for the entered wavelength
- 2019 SI exact constants (Planck, speed of light, electronvolt)
- Dark mode with persistence, fully offline, no dependencies
- 10 self-tests including the 1239.84 eV·nm standard and hf = hc/λ consistency
