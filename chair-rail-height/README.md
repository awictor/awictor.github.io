# Chair Rail Height 📏

Find the classic **chair rail, wainscoting, and picture rail** heights from your wall height. Single offline HTML file, no dependencies, no data leaves your device.

## Use it

Open `index.html` in any browser, or visit the [live version](https://awictor.github.io/chair-rail-height/).

Enter your wall height and a wainscoting fraction. You get the chair rail height, wainscoting top, and picture rail height.

## How it works

- A chair rail traditionally sits about one-third of the way up the wall — 32″ in a standard 8-foot room.
- Wainscoting usually caps at that same third, though board-and-batten often goes taller — adjust the fraction to taste.
- A picture rail runs high, roughly a foot below the ceiling.
- Avoid landing trim exactly at the wall's midpoint — the eye prefers the one-third proportion.
