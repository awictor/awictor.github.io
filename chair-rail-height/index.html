How it works

  • A chair rail traditionally sits about one-third of the way up the wall — 32″ in a standard 8-foot room.
  • Wainscoting usually caps at that same third, though board-and-batten often goes taller — adjust the fraction to taste.
  • A picture rail runs high, roughly a foot below the ceiling.
  • Avoid landing trim exactly at the wall's midpoint — the eye prefers the one-third proportion.
