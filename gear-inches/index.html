Reading the numbers

The gear ratio is chainring teeth ÷ cog teeth — how many wheel turns you get per pedal stroke. Gear inches multiplies that by the wheel diameter, an old but handy way to compare gears (bigger = harder). Development is how far the bike rolls per pedal revolution, ratio × wheel circumference. Multiply development by cadence for speed. A 50×14 on a 700c wheel is about 100 gear inches and rolls ~7.5 m per stroke. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
