The suffix rule

English ordinals add -st, -nd, -rd, or -th to a number. The rule keys off the last digit — 1→st, 2→nd, 3→rd, everything else →th — except the "teens" 11, 12, 13, which always take -th (11th, 12th, 13th), and so does any number ending in those two digits (113th, 512th). That's why 21 is 21st but 11 is 11th. This tool applies the rule both ways. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
