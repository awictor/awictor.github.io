About matrix operations

The determinant tells you whether a square matrix is invertible (non-zero) and how it scales volume; it's computed here by exact cofactor expansion. The inverse A⁻¹ satisfies A·A⁻¹ = I and exists only when det ≠ 0 — found via the adjugate divided by the determinant. Transpose flips rows and columns. Multiplication A×B is defined when A's column count equals B's row count, and is famously non-commutative. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
