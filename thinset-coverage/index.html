How it works

  • A deeper trowel notch lays a thicker bed, so it covers less area per bag. Big-format tile and uneven substrates need bigger notches.
  • Coverage figures are typical for a modified thinset in a 50 lb bag; check your bag — polymer-modified and large-format mortars vary.
  • We add your waste allowance, then round up to whole bags, since you can't buy a partial bag.
  • Back-buttering large tile roughly doubles mortar use — bump the waste figure or step up a notch.
  • Coverage assumes a flat substrate. Bare, porous, or wavy surfaces drink more mortar.
