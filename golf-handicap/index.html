How it works

Each round becomes a score differential: (adjusted score − course rating) × 113 ÷ slope rating, where 113 is the neutral slope. Your handicap index is the average of your best 8 differentials from the last 20 rounds. On a given course your course handicap is index × slope ÷ 113, and your net score subtracts that from your gross. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
