How it works

  • An optional loading phase saturates your muscles fast: about 0.3 g/kg/day for 5–7 days, split into 3–4 smaller servings to ease the stomach.
  • Maintenance then keeps stores topped up at roughly 0.03 g/kg/day — for most people the familiar flat 3–5 g.
  • Loading is optional: skip it and take the maintenance dose daily, and you'll saturate in about 3–4 weeks instead.
  • Creatine monohydrate is the studied form; take it any time of day, consistently, with water.
  • Doses scale with lean mass, so heavier lifters need a bit more; these are general guidelines, not medical advice.
