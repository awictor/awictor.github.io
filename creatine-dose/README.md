# Creatine Dose 💪

Creatine **loading and maintenance doses** from bodyweight, split into servings. Single HTML file, fully offline, nothing leaves your device.

## Why

Creatine dosing is often quoted as a flat "5 g," but the evidence-based amounts scale with bodyweight — especially the optional loading phase. This turns your weight into concrete loading and maintenance numbers, and splits loading into stomach-friendly servings.

## Use it

Open `index.html` in any browser, or use the hosted version:

- **Tool:** https://awictor.github.io/creatine-dose/
- Enter your **bodyweight** (kg).
- Set **loading servings per day**.
- Read the daily loading dose, per-serving amount, and the maintenance dose.

## How it works

- Loading ≈ 0.3 g/kg/day for 5–7 days, split into 3–4 servings.
- Maintenance ≈ 0.03 g/kg/day — for most people the familiar 3–5 g.
- Loading is optional; skipping it just saturates over ~3–4 weeks instead.
- Use monohydrate, take it consistently with water. General guidance, not medical advice.
