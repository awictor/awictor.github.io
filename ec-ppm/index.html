How it works

  • A nutrient meter really measures electrical conductivity (EC, in mS/cm). PPM meters just multiply EC by a fixed factor.
  • Two conventions exist: the 500 scale (PPM = EC × 500, common in the US) and the 700 scale (× 700, common in Europe/Australia).
  • So the same solution reads 750 ppm on a 500-scale meter but 1050 ppm on a 700-scale one — same EC of 1.5.
  • Always note which scale a recipe uses; mixing them is the classic way to over- or under-feed plants.
  • EC is the unambiguous number — quote it when you can, and match your meter's scale otherwise.
