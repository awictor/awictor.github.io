Extraction yield vs. strength

A shot or brew has two independent qualities. Strength is the TDS — the percent of dissolved solids in the cup (how intense it tastes). Extraction yield is how much of the ground coffee's mass ended up in the drink: (TDS% × beverage weight) ÷ dose. The SCA "Golden Cup" sweet spot is about 18–22% — below tastes sour and grassy (under-extracted), above tastes bitter and hollow (over-extracted). Measure TDS with a refractometer and weigh the cup.
