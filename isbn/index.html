About ISBNs

An ISBN uniquely identifies a book edition. The older ISBN-10 ends in a check digit (0–9 or X for ten) validated modulo 11; the current ISBN-13 is an EAN-13 barcode number with a modulo-10 check digit. Every ISBN-10 maps to an ISBN-13 by prefixing 978 and recomputing the check digit; the reverse works only for 978-prefixed ISBN-13s. This tool checks the digit and converts both ways. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
