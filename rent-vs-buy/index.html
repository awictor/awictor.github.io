How the comparison works

For buying: upfront (down payment + closing) + all mortgage payments + property tax, insurance, maintenance and HOA over the years you stay, minus what you net when you sell (appreciated value − selling costs − remaining loan balance). For renting: total rent (growing each year) + renters insurance, minus the investment growth the renter earns by putting the equivalent down payment and closing costs into the market instead. Lower net cost wins. A simplified model with fixed rates — not financial advice. Everything runs locally.
