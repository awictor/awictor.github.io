How it works

  • Spans are for #2 Southern Pine at 40 psf live load (typical residential deck), a common code basis.
  • Bigger joists and tighter spacing span farther; going from 16" to 12" on-center adds meaningful reach.
  • Span is measured between supports (beam to ledger), not the total board length.
  • Always confirm against your local code and species — this is a planning estimate, not a substitute for the span tables in your jurisdiction.
