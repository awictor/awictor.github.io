How it works

  • An ability modifier = ⌊(score − 10) ÷ 2⌋. A 16 gives +3; a 10–11 gives +0; an 8 gives −1.
  • Every +2 of score is one more point of modifier — that's why even scores matter.
  • Proficiency bonus scales with level: +2 at levels 1–4, +3 at 5–8, +4 at 9–12, +5 at 13–16, +6 at 17–20.
  • For a skill or attack you're proficient in, add both: modifier + proficiency. A level-5 character with 16 Dex adds +3 +3 = +6.
  • Scores run 1–30; 20 is the usual player cap (+5), 30 the legendary max (+10).
