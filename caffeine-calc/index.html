How caffeine clears

Caffeine leaves your body on an exponential curve: every half-life — about 5 hours for a typical adult — the amount in your bloodstream halves. So remaining = dose × 0.5^(hours ÷ half-life). To fall below a bedtime threshold takes half-life × log₂(dose ÷ threshold) hours. Smoking shortens the half-life; pregnancy and some medications stretch it past 10 hours. This is an estimate, not medical advice. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
