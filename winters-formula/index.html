Winters' Formula

Expected PaCO₂ in metabolic acidosis

from the ABG, to check compensation
—
expected PaCO₂ (mmHg)  ·  1.5 × HCO₃⁻ + 8 ± 2
—

How to use it

In a metabolic acidosis, the lungs compensate by lowering PaCO₂. Winters' formula predicts the appropriate value: expected PaCO₂ = 1.5 × HCO₃⁻ + 8 (± 2). If the measured PaCO₂ is within that range, respiratory compensation is appropriate. Higher than expected means an additional respiratory acidosis; lower means an additional respiratory alkalosis.
