About

A meeting's cost is simply attendees × hourly rate × hours. Salaries convert to an hourly rate by dividing by 2080 working hours per year (40 h × 52 weeks). Seeing the number — and the per-minute burn rate, or what a weekly recurring meeting costs over a year — makes it easy to decide whether a meeting is worth everyone's time or could be an email. These are salary-cost estimates and exclude overhead. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
