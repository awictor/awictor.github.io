TermConsiderCount

About

Small word choices can exclude people. This flags common non-inclusive terms — tech metaphors (whitelist/blacklist, master/slave), gendered job words (chairman, manpower), casual ableist words (crazy, lame, dummy), and "guys" for mixed groups — and suggests neutral alternatives. Context matters, so treat these as prompts to reconsider, not hard rules.
