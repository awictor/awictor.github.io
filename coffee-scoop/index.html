How it works

  • Water needed = cups × cup size; a coffeemaker "cup" is usually 6 fl oz, not a full mug.
  • Coffee = water weight ÷ ratio. The "golden ratio" is about 1:16–1:18 by weight (grams).
  • A level scoop is 2 tablespoons ≈ 10 g; one tablespoon ≈ 5 g of grounds.
  • Weighing beats scooping — grind size and bean density change what a scoop actually holds.
