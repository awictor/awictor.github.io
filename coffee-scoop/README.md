# Coffee Scoop Calculator 🫘

Work out how much ground coffee you need for a pot — **grams, scoops, and tablespoons** for any number of cups at your chosen strength. Single offline HTML file, no dependencies, no data leaves your device.

## Features

- Golden-ratio brewing (1:16–1:18) from cups and cup size.
- Output in grams, scoops (2 tbsp), tablespoons, and water volume.
- Mild / balanced / strong presets.
- Dark mode, mobile friendly, works fully offline.

## Usage

Open `index.html` in any browser. Enter cups, cup size, and strength.

> A coffeemaker "cup" is usually 6 fl oz, not a full mug. Weighing beats scooping.
