How many lights for a tree

The classic rule is about 100 lights per foot of tree height for a nicely lit tree — so a 7 ft tree wants roughly 700 lights. Bump it to 150 or 200 per foot for a fuller, designer look, and remember taller trees are also wider, so the count grows faster than height alone suggests. Divide by the count on each strand (usually 100) and round up. Watch each string's max connected sets rating so you don't overload a run, and wrap in from the trunk out along each branch for even coverage. For garland or eaves, count ~100 lights per 3–4 ft of run. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
