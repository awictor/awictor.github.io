How it works

Each vertical strip of paper (a "drop") must be as tall as the wall plus one pattern repeat so the pattern lines up between strips — that's the main source of waste. The tool counts how many drops fit down one roll, how many drops it takes to go around the room, and rounds up to whole rolls. Enter the perimeter (add up all wall widths) and ignore small doors/windows for a safe margin. Metric units; a typical roll is 0.53 m × 10 m. Everything runs locally.
