About CURB-65

CURB-65 grades the severity of community-acquired pneumonia from five signs, one point each: Confusion (new), Urea > 7 mmol/L (BUN > 19 mg/dL), Respiratory rate ≥ 30, low Blood pressure (systolic < 90 or diastolic ≤ 60), and age ≥ 65. Higher scores track with higher 30-day mortality. General guidance: 0–1 usually suitable for outpatient care, 2 consider a short admission or supervised outpatient treatment, 3–5 admit and assess for intensive care. A decision aid, not a substitute for clinical judgement. Everything runs locally.
