What is the Verhoeff algorithm?

Published by Jacobus Verhoeff in 1969, it was the first decimal check-digit scheme to detect all single-digit errors and all transpositions of adjacent digits — including the tricky "phonetic" ones (like 13 ↔ 30) that the simpler Luhn algorithm misses. It works over the dihedral group D₅ using a multiplication table, a position-dependent permutation, and an inverse table, so the arithmetic isn't commutative — that's what makes swaps detectable.
