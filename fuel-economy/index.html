MPG vs L/100 km

Fuel economy is measured two opposite ways. MPG and km/L are efficiency (distance per fuel) — higher is better. L/100 km is consumption (fuel per distance) — lower is better — so it's the inverse. The two MPG scales differ because a US gallon (3.785 L) is smaller than an Imperial gallon (4.546 L), making UK MPG figures about 20% higher for the same car. This tool converts cleanly between all four. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
