How baker's percentage works

Bakers write formulas as percentages of the total flour, which is always 100%. Every other ingredient is a percentage of that flour weight, so ingredient = flour × percent ÷ 100. The water percentage is the dough's hydration — 65% is a firm loaf, 75%+ an open, slack crumb. Salt sits around 2%, and a sourdough levain is often 15–25%. Because everything scales off flour, you can resize any recipe just by changing the flour weight, and compare two formulas at a glance. Total dough weight is flour × (1 + sum of the other percentages ÷ 100). Weigh in grams for repeatable results. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
