Which mean to use

The arithmetic mean (sum ÷ count) is the everyday average. The geometric mean (nth root of the product) is right for growth rates and ratios — average investment return, population growth. The harmonic mean (n ÷ sum of reciprocals) is right for rates over a fixed distance — average speed on a round trip, or average of P/E ratios. The root mean square matters for magnitudes that can be negative (AC voltage, error). For positive numbers they always order HM ≤ GM ≤ AM ≤ RMS, with equality only when all values match.
