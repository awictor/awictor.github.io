# Changelog

## 0.1.0
- First release. Training heart rate zone calculator.
- Five zones in bpm from age; three max-HR formulas (Fox, Tanaka, Gulati).
- Karvonen (heart-rate-reserve) method when a resting HR is provided.
- Color-coded zones, dark mode, localStorage memory.
- Headless test suite (9 checks) + CI.
