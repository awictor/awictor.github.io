What the numbers mean

Pitch is written "rise-in-12" — a 6:12 roof rises 6 units for every 12 of horizontal run. Angle = arctan(rise ÷ run). The slope factor = √(1 + (rise/run)²); multiply a roof's flat (plan) area by it to get the true sloped area for ordering shingles, and multiply a horizontal run by it to get the rafter length along the slope. A 12:12 roof is 45° with a slope factor of about 1.414. Enter rise and run in any matching units. Everything runs locally.
