Sailing VMG

Velocity made good toward the wind or a mark

How it works

What VMG means
  • VMG is the part of your speed that actually closes on the target: VMG = boat speed × cos(angle).
  • The angle is between your heading and the straight line to the mark — or to the wind, when working out upwind VMG (your tacking angle).
  • Cross-track speed = boat speed × sin(angle) is the "wasted" sideways motion; the two combine back to your boat speed.
  • Point too high and speed drops; bear away and VMG suffers even though speed climbs — the best VMG is a balance. Everything runs in your browser.
