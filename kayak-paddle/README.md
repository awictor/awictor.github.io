# Kayak Paddle Length 🛶

Find the right kayak paddle length from your **height** and the **width (beam)** of your boat. Single offline HTML file, no dependencies, no data leaves your device.

## Features

- Chart-based sizing for low-angle touring, in the real 5 cm increments paddles are sold in (205–250 cm).
- Boat width is the primary driver; height fine-tunes the pick.
- Inches or centimeters for both inputs; output in cm and inches.
- Dark mode, mobile friendly, works fully offline.

## Usage

Open `index.html` in any browser. Enter your height and your kayak's width.

> Prefer an aggressive high-angle stroke? Size down one increment.
