About angle of view

A lens's angle of view depends on both its focal length and the camera's sensor size: FOV = 2·arctan(d / 2f), where d is the sensor dimension (width, height, or diagonal) and f is the focal length. A shorter focal length or a larger sensor gives a wider view. The crop factor is the full-frame (36×24 mm) diagonal divided by your sensor's diagonal; multiplying the real focal length by it gives the equivalent focal length — the full-frame lens that would frame the same scene.
