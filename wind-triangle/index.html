How it works

  • To track a course in wind you point the nose slightly into it — the wind correction angle (crab).
  • WCA = arcsin( wind speed × sin(wind-to-course angle) ÷ TAS ). Crab right for wind from the right, left for wind from the left.
  • Groundspeed = TAS × cos(WCA) − the headwind component. A headwind slows you; a tailwind speeds you up.
  • If the crosswind exceeds your TAS you can't hold the course at all — the tool flags that.
  • Wind is given as the direction it blows from, matching METARs and ATIS.
