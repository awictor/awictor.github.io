# Changelog

## v0.1.0 — 2026-09-12

Initial release.

- Buoyant force, displaced volume, apparent weight, and float/sink test
- Fluid presets (fresh water, seawater, air) plus custom density
- Standard gravity g = 9.80665 m/s²
- Positive-density and non-negative guards
- Dark mode with persistence, fully offline, no dependencies
- 10 self-tests including float/sink thresholds
