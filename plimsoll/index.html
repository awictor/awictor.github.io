Plimsoll

Water finds its level. Flood each tank until the margins agree.
How to play
  • Each colored region is a tank. Water fills a tank from the bottom up, and its surface is always flat across the tank's full width.
  • The numbers along the top and left count how many watered cells sit in that column / row.
  • Click a cell to raise that tank's surface to that row; click the same surface again to drain one level.
  • Mark a tank as empty air: right-click (mouse), long-press (touch), or press A (keyboard).
  • Clues turn green ✓ when satisfied and red ! when overfilled. Match every clue to win.
  • Arrow keys move the cursor; Enter/Space set the surface; Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo / redo. Stuck? Hint explains one forced move.
