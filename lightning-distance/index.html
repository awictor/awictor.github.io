How it works

Light reaches you almost instantly, but sound travels at about 343 m/s (at 20 °C). Count the seconds from the flash to the thunder and multiply by the speed of sound. Rules of thumb: ~3 seconds ≈ 1 km, ~5 seconds ≈ 1 mile. If it's under ~30 s (≈10 km / 6 mi), the storm is close enough to be dangerous — seek shelter.
