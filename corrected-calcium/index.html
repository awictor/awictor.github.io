CorrectedCalcium

Albumin-corrected calcium
—
mg/dL corrected calcium
Corrected Ca = measured Ca + 0.8 × (4.0 − albumin) mg/dL (SI: + 0.02 × (40 − albumin g/L)). Low albumin lowers total calcium without changing the ionized (active) level, so correction avoids over-diagnosing hypocalcemia. Informational only — not medical advice. 100% offline.
