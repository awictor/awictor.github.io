How it works

  • Grit numbers count abrasive particles per inch of screen, so a higher number means smaller particles and a finer scratch.
  • The micron figures are average particle sizes on the North American CAMI scale (Europe's FEPA “P” grades differ slightly).
  • Sand through the sequence without skipping too far — jumping from 80 to 220 leaves scratches the 220 can't erase. The next-grit suggestion keeps each step sane.
  • Rough shaping 40–80, general smoothing 100–150, pre-finish 180–220, between-coats 320+.
  • Let the paper do the work; pressing hard just clogs it and burnishes the wood.
