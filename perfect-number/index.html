What is a perfect number?

A perfect number equals the sum of its proper divisors (all divisors except itself). The smallest is 6 = 1 + 2 + 3; the next are 28, 496, and 8128. If the divisor sum exceeds the number it's abundant (like 12); if it falls short it's deficient (like 8). Perfect numbers are exceedingly rare — only a handful are known — and each even one corresponds to a Mersenne prime. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
