About the APGAR score

Devised by Dr. Virginia Apgar in 1952, this quickly assesses a newborn at 1 and 5 minutes on five signs — Appearance, Pulse, Grimace (reflex irritability), Activity (muscle tone) and Respiration — each scored 0, 1 or 2 for a total of 0–10. Guidance: 7–10 reassuring, 4–6 moderately low (may need support), 0–3 critically low (needs immediate resuscitation). It guides care, it isn't a long-term prognosis. Everything runs locally.
