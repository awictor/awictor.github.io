Ictus · scansion workbench

Type verse, watch the meter reveal itself, tap a syllable to fix the beat.
How to use: Every syllable is a chip; the mark above is the machine's guess (˘ unstressed, ´ stressed). It's only a proposal. Click (or focus and press Enter) a chip to flip its stress · double-click a chip to split it · click the seam between two chips of a word to merge them (split/merge work best with a mouse; on touch, tap the divider between syllables to merge). Feet alternate shading; an amber underline ▾ flags a foot that deviates from the named meter (hover it). Everything round-trips through the URL — Share hands a friend your exact scansion.
