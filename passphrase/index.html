Why word passphrases?

Four random words from a large list are far easier to remember than xK7#qW2! — and often stronger. Entropy is words × log₂(list size); this list has — words, so each adds about — bits. A capital, number or symbol adds a little more. Everything is generated locally with crypto.getRandomValues and unbiased rejection sampling — nothing leaves your browser.
