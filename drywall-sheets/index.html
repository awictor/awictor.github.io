How it works

  • Sheets = total area × (1 + waste) ÷ area per sheet, rounded up.
  • A 4×8 sheet covers 32 sq ft; 4×12 covers 48 and means fewer seams on tall walls.
  • Include both walls and ceilings in the total area; don't subtract small doors and windows — that's your waste buffer.
  • 10% waste is typical; bump it up for lots of cuts, angles, or an inexperienced crew.
