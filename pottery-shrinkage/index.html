Why clay shrinks

Clay loses water and then densifies as it dries and fires, so a pot comes out of the kiln smaller than you built it — typically 8–15% depending on the clay body and firing temperature. Fired size is wet × (1 − rate/100). To end up at a specific finished size you have to build it bigger: wet = target ÷ (1 − rate/100). Find your own rate from a test tile: mark a known length in the wet clay, fire it, and use (wet − fired) ÷ wet × 100. Always test each new clay and glaze combination, since shrinkage compounds if you don't plan for it — a lid that fit the greenware won't fit the fired jar.
