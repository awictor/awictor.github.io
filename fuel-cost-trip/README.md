# Road Trip Fuel Cost ⛽

Estimate the **fuel cost of a road trip** from distance, your MPG, and gas price — with cost per mile and per person. Single offline HTML file, no dependencies, no data leaves your device.

## Features

- Gallons needed, total cost, cost per mile, and cost per person.
- Split among passengers to see the real carpool cost.
- Dark mode, mobile friendly, works fully offline.

## Usage

Open `index.html` in any browser. Enter distance, MPG, gas price, and party size.

> For a round trip, enter double the one-way distance.
