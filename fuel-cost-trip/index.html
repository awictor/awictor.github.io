How it works

  • Gallons = distance ÷ MPG; total cost = gallons × price per gallon.
  • For a round trip, enter double the one-way distance.
  • Highway MPG is usually higher than the city figure — use the number that matches your route.
  • Splitting among passengers shows the real per-person cost of carpooling.
