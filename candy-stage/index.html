Reading the sugar stages

As sugar syrup boils, more water leaves and the temperature climbs through named stages that set a candy's texture: thread (~230 °F), soft ball (~238) for fudge and fondant, firm ball (~245) for caramels, hard ball (~255) for nougat, soft crack (~275) for taffy, hard crack (~305) for brittle and lollipops, then caramel (~340) as it browns. A calibrated candy thermometer is the reliable guide. Because these temperatures track the boiling point of water, they drop with altitude — subtract about 1 °F per 500 ft above sea level, or your candy will overcook.
