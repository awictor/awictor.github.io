How it works

  • Add up the running watts of everything on at once.
  • Motors (fridge, AC, pump) spike on startup, so add the single largest starting surge — only one typically kicks in at a time.
  • Peak = total running + largest surge. Pick a generator rated at or above that peak.
  • Leave headroom; running a generator at 100% continuously shortens its life.
