How it works

  • C-rate is current expressed as a multiple of capacity. 1C drains a pack in one hour; 2C in half an hour; 0.5C in two.
  • Current (A) = capacity (Ah) × C-rate. A 5 Ah pack at 2C delivers 10 A.
  • Ideal runtime (hours) = 1 ÷ C-rate — so a higher C-rate means a shorter run.
  • Every cell has a max continuous C-rate (LiPo packs print it, e.g. “50C”). Exceeding it overheats and damages the pack.
  • Real runtime is a bit less than ideal — high currents lose capacity to heat and voltage sag (the Peukert effect).
