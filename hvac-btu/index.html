How it works

  • Start from 20 BTU per square foot of floor area — the standard rule for cooling.
  • Adjust for sun: +10% if the room is very sunny, −10% if heavily shaded.
  • Add 600 BTU for each regular occupant beyond two, and 4,000 BTU if it's a kitchen.
  • Air conditioners are sold in "tons" — 1 ton = 12,000 BTU/hr. An oversized unit short-cycles and won't dehumidify well, so size it close, not big.
