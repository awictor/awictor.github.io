How to find your size

Ring sizes come from the band's inside circumference. The US scale is linear: circumference (mm) = 36.537 + 2.5535 × size, so US 7 is about 54.4 mm around and 17.3 mm across. The EU / ISO size is simply that circumference in millimetres. To measure a ring you own, wrap a strip of paper around the inside and read the length, or measure the inner diameter with a ruler and multiply by π.
