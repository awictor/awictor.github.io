How typing speed is measured

A "word" is standardised as 5 characters (including spaces), so gross WPM is (characters ÷ 5) ÷ minutes. Net WPM subtracts one word for each uncorrected error, which is what most tests report. Accuracy is the share of characters typed correctly. For reference, ~40 WPM is average, 65+ is fast, and pro typists exceed 100 WPM. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
