About half-life

In exponential decay, the amount left after time t is N = N₀ · (½)^(t / T), where T is the half-life — the time for half the substance to decay. Equivalently N = N₀ · e^(−λt) with decay constant λ = ln2 / T, and the mean lifetime is τ = 1/λ = T/ln2. The same math describes radioactive isotopes, drug clearance in the body, and capacitor discharge. Carbon-14's half-life of 5730 years is what makes radiocarbon dating possible.
