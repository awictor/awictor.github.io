About force units

Force is mass times acceleration; the SI unit is the newton (1 N = 1 kg·m/s²). Gravitational units express weight directly: a kilogram-force is the pull of Earth's gravity on 1 kg (9.80665 N) and a pound-force is the weight of one pound (≈4.448 N). The dyne is the tiny CGS unit (1 N = 100,000 dyne). Converting through newtons keeps them all consistent — for example 1 kgf ≈ 2.205 lbf, mirroring the kg↔lb mass ratio.
