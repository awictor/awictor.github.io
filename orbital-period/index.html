Kepler's third law

The period of an orbit depends only on its size and the mass it circles: T = 2π √(a³ / GM), where a is the semi-major axis, M the central mass, and G the gravitational constant. Bigger orbits take longer (T² ∝ a³) and a heavier central body speeds everything up. Circular orbital velocity is √(GM / r), which falls off as you move outward. Earth's 149.6 million km orbit around the Sun gives ~365 days at ~29.8 km/s. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
