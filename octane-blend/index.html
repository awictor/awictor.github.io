How octane blending works

Mixing two fuels gives an octane rating that's the volume-weighted average: (VA·OA + VB·OB) ÷ (VA + VB). To reach a target between your low- and high-octane fuels, the fraction of high-octane you need is (target − low) ÷ (high − low) — so half-and-half 87 and 93 gives 90. Multiply by the total volume for how much premium (or race fuel) to add to a partial tank. The target has to sit between the two fuels' ratings; you can't blend down below the lower or above the higher. This uses pump (R+M)/2 numbers — use the same rating scale for both fuels.
