# Yeast Converter

Convert baking yeast between **instant**, **active dry**, and **fresh (cake)** yeast by weight — substitute one for another in any recipe.

**[Open the tool →](https://awictor.github.io/yeast-converter/)**

- Ratio instant : active dry : fresh ≈ 1 : 1.25 : 3
- Enter any form, get all three
- Notes on proofing and freshness
- Dark mode, 100% offline, no dependencies, no tracking

## Example

A 7 g packet of instant yeast ≈ 8.75 g active dry ≈ 21 g fresh.
