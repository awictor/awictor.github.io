Swapping yeasts

By weight the three forms follow roughly instant : active dry : fresh = 1 : 1.25 : 3 — instant is the most concentrated, fresh the least. So a 7 g packet of instant equals about 8.75 g active dry or 21 g fresh. Instant can be mixed straight into the flour; active dry is traditionally proofed in warm water first; fresh is crumbled in and is the most perishable. Dough will still rise with any of them — just give slower forms a little more time. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
