About HAS-BLED

HAS-BLED estimates 1-year major-bleeding risk in atrial-fibrillation patients on anticoagulation — the companion to CHA₂DS₂-VASc (stroke risk). One point each for Hypertension, Abnormal renal and liver function (up to 2), Stroke, Bleeding history, Labile INR, Elderly (>65), and Drugs/alcohol (up to 2). A score of ≥ 3 flags high bleeding risk warranting caution and closer review — not automatically stopping anticoagulation, but addressing modifiable factors. A decision aid, not a directive.
