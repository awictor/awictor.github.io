# Party Servings Calculator

Plan a party: how many **drinks**, pounds of **main protein**, **appetizer pieces**, and **ice** you need for your guest count and party length.

**[Open the tool →](https://awictor.github.io/servings-calc/)**

- ~1 drink per guest per hour
- 6–8 oz main protein and 6 appetizer pieces per guest
- ~1.5 lbs ice per guest
- Dark mode, 100% offline, no dependencies, no tracking

## Example

20 guests over 3 hours ≈ 60 drinks, 10 lbs of protein, 120 appetizer pieces, and 30 lbs of ice.
