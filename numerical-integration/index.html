About Simpson's rule

The composite Simpson's rule approximates a definite integral ∫ₐᵇ f(x) dx by fitting parabolas across pairs of subintervals: (h/3)·[f₀ + 4f₁ + 2f₂ + 4f₃ + … + fₙ] with h = (b−a)/n. It's exact for polynomials up to degree 3 and converges as O(h⁴), so a few hundred intervals nail most smooth functions. The limits a and b accept expressions too (e.g. pi). f(x) is parsed by a real recursive-descent parser — no eval(). Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
