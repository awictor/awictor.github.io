About FFMI

The Fat-Free Mass Index is like BMI but for lean mass: it divides your fat-free mass (weight minus fat) by height squared, so it measures muscularity rather than total size. FFMI = fat-free mass (kg) / height (m)². The normalized version adjusts to a 1.8 m reference height — + 6.1 × (1.8 − height) — so tall and short people compare fairly. Rough guide for men: 18–20 average, 20–22 fit, 22–23 very muscular, and ~25 is often cited as the natural ceiling. Women run about 3–4 points lower.
