How it works

  • A balloon's volume is modeled as a sphere: (4/3)π r³, then converted to cubic feet.
  • Balloons per tank = tank capacity ÷ volume per balloon. Tanks needed rounds up — you can't buy a partial tank.
  • Balloon size is the diameter when inflated. Latex balloons are usually sized 9", 11", or 12".
  • Real fills run a bit under the sphere estimate (necks, under-inflation), so buy a little extra helium for a big event.
