Reading a water report

Hardness is the dissolved calcium and magnesium in water, usually reported as ppm (mg/L as CaCO₃). Water softeners and US labs often use grains per gallon — 1 gpg = 17.12 ppm — while chemistry uses mmol/L (÷ 100.09, CaCO₃'s molar mass). The common scale: soft under 17 ppm, moderately hard 60–120, hard 120–180, very hard above. Hard water leaves scale and spots and cuts soap lather; sizing a softener starts from your gpg.
