How it works

  • Siding is sold by the square — 100 square feet of coverage.
  • Squares = wall area × (1 + waste) ÷ 100, rounded up to whole squares.
  • Measure each wall (width × height), add them up, and don't subtract small windows and doors — that's your waste margin.
  • Add extra for lots of corners, gables, and trim; 10% waste is a common starting point, 15% for complex walls.
