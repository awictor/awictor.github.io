# Changelog

## v0.1.0 — 2026-09-11

Initial release.

- Binomial PMF (exactly k), CDF (at most k), and at-least-k probabilities
- Combinations C(n,k), mean np, and standard deviation √(np(1−p))
- Overflow-safe multiplicative combinations; out-of-range and invalid-p guards
- Dark mode with persistence, fully offline, no dependencies
- 10 self-tests including 252/1024, PMF-sums-to-1, and CDF edges
