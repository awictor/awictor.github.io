About the binomial distribution

For n independent trials each succeeding with probability p, the chance of exactly k successes is P(X = k) = C(n, k)·pᵏ·(1−p)ⁿ⁻ᵏ, where C(n, k) counts the ways to choose which trials succeed. Summing gives the cumulative "at most" and "at least" probabilities. The distribution has mean np and standard deviation √(np(1−p)). Think coin flips, defect rates, or A/B conversions.
