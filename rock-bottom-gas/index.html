Rock Bottom Gas

Minimum reserve for two divers to ascend safely

How it works

The minimum-gas model
  • Pressure: ambient pressure in atmospheres is ATA = depth ÷ 33 + 1 (seawater).
  • Problem solving: 1 minute at full depth for both divers to sort out an out-of-gas emergency.
  • Ascent: from depth to a 15 ft safety stop at your ascent rate, breathing at the average pressure over that climb.
  • Safety stop: 3 minutes at 15 ft.
  • Each phase burns SAC × ATA × minutes × divers. The total is your rock-bottom reserve — surface with at least this much.
  • Converted to tank psi from your cylinder's rated capacity. Everything runs in your browser.
