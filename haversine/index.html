What is the haversine formula?

The haversine formula gives the shortest distance over the Earth's surface — the "great-circle" distance — between two points given their latitude and longitude, treating the Earth as a sphere (mean radius 6371 km). It's the standard for flight paths, delivery-radius checks, and "how far is X from Y" features because it stays accurate near the poles where flat-map math breaks down. The initial bearing is the compass direction (0° = north, 90° = east) you'd first head to follow that great circle. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
