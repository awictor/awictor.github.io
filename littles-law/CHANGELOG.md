# Changelog

## v0.1.0 — 2026-09-11

Initial release.

- Little's Law solver: pick WIP, throughput, or lead time and it computes the other
- M/M/1 queue sidecar: utilization, expected in-system count, expected wait time
- Stability flag when arrival rate ≥ service rate
- Dark mode with persistence, fully offline, no dependencies
- 10 self-tests including a Little's-Law-inside-M/M/1 cross-check
