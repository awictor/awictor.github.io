About Little's Law

Little's Law is L = λ · W: the average work-in-progress equals throughput times lead time. It holds for any stable system regardless of arrival distribution, service time, or scheduling — which is why kanban teams, factories, and networks all use it. Reduce WIP or increase throughput to cut lead time. The M/M/1 sidecar assumes Poisson arrivals and exponential service on a single server; utilization ρ = λ/μ, expected in-system count L = ρ/(1−ρ), and expected time W = 1/(μ−λ).
