How it works

  • As an appetizer, plan about 2 oz of cheese per guest; when it's the main event, closer to 5 oz.
  • Add charcuterie at about 2 oz per guest for grazing, or 3 oz if it's the meal.
  • Offer a spread of textures and milks — roughly one variety per five guests, from three up to about seven.
  • Round each cheese up to what the shop sells, and take it out of the fridge an hour before serving.
