# Cheese Board Amount 🧀

Work out how much **cheese and charcuterie** to buy for a gathering, and how many cheese varieties, from guest count and whether it's an appetizer or the meal. Single offline HTML file, no dependencies, no data leaves your device.

## Use it

Open `index.html` in any browser, or visit the [live version](https://awictor.github.io/cheese-board-amount/).

Enter the number of guests and whether the board is an appetizer or the main event. You get total cheese, charcuterie, and how many cheese varieties to offer.

## How it works

- As an appetizer, plan about 2 oz of cheese per guest; when it's the main event, closer to 5 oz.
- Add charcuterie at about 2 oz per guest for grazing, or 3 oz if it's the meal.
- Offer a spread of textures and milks — roughly one variety per five guests, from three up to about seven.
- Round each cheese up to what the shop sells, and take it out of the fridge an hour before serving.
