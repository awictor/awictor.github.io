How it's estimated

A common rule of thumb sizes a room air conditioner at about 20 BTU per square foot. This tool applies the standard ENERGY STAR adjustments: −10% if the room is heavily shaded, +10% if it's very sunny, +600 BTU for each regular occupant beyond two, and +4,000 BTU for a kitchen. It's a starting estimate — ceiling height, insulation and climate matter, so a professional load calculation is best for big purchases. Everything runs locally.
