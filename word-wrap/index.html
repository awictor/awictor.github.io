WordWrap

Reflow & hard-wrap text

72

Wrapping preserves paragraphs (blank-line separated) and collapses internal line breaks before reflowing. 72 columns is the git-commit-body convention; 80 is classic terminal width. "Unwrap" joins each paragraph back into one line. 100% local.

WordWrap is part of the Toolkit — free, offline, no signup. · Source
