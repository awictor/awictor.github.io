How RAID trades capacity for safety

RAID 0 stripes across all disks for full capacity but zero redundancy — one failure loses everything. RAID 1 mirrors, so usable space equals a single disk. RAID 5 keeps one disk of parity (n−1)×size and survives one failure; RAID 6 keeps two (n−2)×size and survives two. RAID 10 mirrors pairs then stripes, giving half the raw capacity and surviving one failure per mirror. Efficiency is usable ÷ raw. Capacity here uses base-10 TB (drive marketing); your OS may report slightly less in TiB. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
