01 · The trick

Sand runs away from motion

Sprinkle fine sand on a metal plate and vibrate it. Where the plate flaps hardest the grains get flung around; where it holds perfectly still they pile up. The still curves are nodal lines, and sand traces them like chalk.

02 · Nodes vs antinodes

Standing waves have quiet zones

A vibrating plate doesn't ripple randomly. It settles into a standing wave: a fixed pattern of peaks and troughs. The points that never move are nodes; the ones swinging most are antinodes. Grains flee antinodes and collect on nodes.

03 · Eigenmodes & the (n,m) formula

Every plate has favourite shapes

A square plate can only ring in specific shapes — its eigenmodes, indexed by two whole numbers (n, m). Each is a blend of cosine waves along x and y:

φₙₘ(x,y) = cos(nπx)·cos(mπy) − cos(mπx)·cos(nπy)

Bigger n, m ⇒ more lines ⇒ finer lace.

04 · Resonance

The frequency picks the pattern

Drive the plate near a mode's natural frequency fₙₘ and that shape blooms; drift away and it dissolves into noise. That's resonance. Sweeping frequency is like turning a dial through the plate's private alphabet of figures.

05 · Round plates ring too

Circles speak in Bessel

Swap the square for a disc and the math changes to Bessel functions — concentric rings crossed by radial spokes. Same idea, different alphabet. Nodelace does both.

06 · Your turn

Paint with a slider — or your voice

Grab the frequency slider and scrub. Turn on the tone to hear the plate. Or hit the mic and whistle: the sand answers the pitch you sing. Found something beautiful at 428 Hz? The URL is already a shareable link.

How to play

Keyboard: ←/→ tune frequency · Space pause · S shape · H resonance hunt · T tone · M mic · P PNG · G GIF · R reshuffle grains.

Everything you change is written into the page's URL. Copy the address bar (or use “Copy link”) and whoever opens it lands on the exact same figure. A built-in gallery of curated presets lives in the controls.

Nodelace is a single HTML file with zero dependencies and zero network calls. The physics is a driven-damped superposition of true plate eigenmodes; the sand is thousands of grains doing a biased random walk down the amplitude field toward the nodal lines.
