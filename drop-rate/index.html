The gambler's fallacy, defused

If each try has an independent chance p, the odds of getting it at least once in n tries are 1 − (1 − p)ⁿ — you never truly hit 100%, you just approach it. A 1% drop over 100 tries is only about 63%, not the "guaranteed" people expect, because misses compound. To reach a target cumulative chance you need tries = log(1 − target) ÷ log(1 − p), rounded up. The average number of tries to the first success is 1 ÷ p (so 100 for a 1% drop) — but that's a mean, not a promise; plenty of people go well past it. This assumes a flat rate with no pity/bad-luck protection. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
