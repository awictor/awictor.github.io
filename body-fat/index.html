BodyFat

U.S. Navy body fat estimator

The U.S. Navy circumference method estimates body fat from tape measurements. Measure the neck below the larynx and the waist at the navel (women also measure the hip at the widest point). Estimate only — not medical advice. Runs entirely in your browser.
