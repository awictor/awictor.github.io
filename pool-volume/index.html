How it works

Volume is surface area times average depth. For a sloped bottom the average depth is simply (shallow + deep) ÷ 2. A rectangular pool is length × width × avg depth; a round pool is π × radius² × avg depth. Multiply cubic feet by 7.48 for US gallons, then by 3.785 for liters. Knowing the exact gallonage is what makes chlorine, salt, and acid dosing accurate. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
