Initial release.

- Detects the Allow-Credentials + wildcard-origin conflict
- Method checkboxes, expose-headers, copy to clipboard
- Dark mode with persistence, fully offline, no dependencies
- 10 self-tests covering header formatting and the credentials/wildcard rule
