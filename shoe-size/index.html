How it works

  • Each half-size step is 1/6 inch of last length; EU sizing uses the Paris point (2/3 cm).
  • Men's: UK ≈ US − 1, EU ≈ US + 33. Women's: UK ≈ US − 2, EU ≈ US + 30.5.
  • Foot length is the most reliable measure — measure heel to longest toe and match the cm value.
  • These are approximate: brands and lasts vary, so treat the result as a starting point and check the maker's chart.
