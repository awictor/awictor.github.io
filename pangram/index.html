Pangrams & perfect pangrams

A pangram is a sentence that uses all 26 letters of the alphabet at least once — "The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog" is the classic. They're used to show off fonts and test keyboards. A perfect pangram uses each letter exactly once (26 letters total), which is very hard — "Mr Jock, TV quiz PhD, bags few lynx". Green tiles are letters you've used; dashed red tiles are still missing. Everything runs locally.
