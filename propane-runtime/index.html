How propane runtime works

Propane packs about 21,594 BTU per pound (≈ 91,500 BTU per gallon). A full 20 lb BBQ tank therefore holds roughly 432,000 BTU. Divide that by an appliance's rated output in BTU/hr to get burn time: a 30,000 BTU/hr grill runs about 14 hours on a full tank at full blast. Real runtime is a bit shorter — tanks are filled to ~80% for safety, output drops in cold weather, and most burners don't run wide open the whole time. Propane weighs about 4.24 lb per gallon.
