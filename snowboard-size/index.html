How it works

  • Modern sizing scales board length to about 86–90% of your height, tuned by riding style.
  • Freestyle riders size down for spins and presses; freeride riders size up for float and stability.
  • The old chin-to-nose rule (height − 20 to − 15 cm) still makes a good sanity-check range.
  • Weight and boot size matter too — if you're heavy for your height, lean toward the longer end.
