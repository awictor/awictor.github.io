# Snowboard Size 🏂

Find your recommended **snowboard length** from your height and riding style, with the classic chin-to-nose sanity range. Single offline HTML file, no dependencies, no data leaves your device.

## Features

- Length scales to 86–90% of height, tuned by freestyle / all-mountain / freeride.
- Classic chin-to-nose range (height − 20 to − 15 cm) as a cross-check.
- Centimeters or inches.
- Dark mode, mobile friendly, works fully offline.

## Usage

Open `index.html` in any browser. Enter your height and pick a riding style.

> Weight matters too — if you're heavy for your height, lean toward the longer end.
