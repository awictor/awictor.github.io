How charging time works

The energy to add is battery × (end − start) ÷ 100. Divide that by the charger's power to get hours, then divide by an efficiency of about 0.85–0.9 for AC losses and heat. A 60 kWh car from 20% to 80% needs 36 kWh; on a 7 kW home charger that's around 5–6 hours. Real DC fast charging tapers near the top, so 20–80% is the useful range to plan around.
