How many drinks in a keg?

A US half barrel keg holds 15.5 gallons — that's 1,984 fluid ounces, or about 124 pints (16 oz) and 165 twelve-ounce pours. Divide total ounces by your glass size and round down; there's always a little foam and heel loss, so treat it as a ceiling. Dividing the keg price by the number of pours gives your true cost per drink — usually far below bar prices. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
