How sealed enclosure sizing works

A woofer in a sealed box acts like a spring: the trapped air stiffens the suspension, raising both the resonance frequency and the system's damping. From the driver's Thiele-Small specs, Fc = Fs · √(1 + Vas/Vb) and Qtc = Qts · √(1 + Vas/Vb). A smaller box means a higher Fc and Qtc. A Qtc of 0.707 gives maximally flat (Butterworth) response; 0.5 is critically damped and tight; above ~0.8 the bass gets boomy with a peak. To hit a target Qtc, solve for volume: Vb = Vas / ((Qtc/Qts)² − 1) — which requires the target to exceed the driver's own Qts. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
