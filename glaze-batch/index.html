How it works

  • Glaze recipes are written as percentages of dry materials that sum to 100. To mix a batch, multiply each by your batch weight.
  • Grams of an ingredient = batch weight × its percent ÷ 100. A 1,000 g batch of a 40% feldspar glaze needs 400 g feldspar.
  • Colorants and opacifiers are usually added on top of the 100% base, so a recipe can total more than 100 — that's expected.
  • Add water to a workable consistency — often around 80% of the dry weight — then sieve; adjust to the right “cream” thickness.
  • Always weigh dry, wear a mask around silica dust, and test-tile a new batch before glazing real work.
