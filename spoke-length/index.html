How spoke length is calculated

A spoke runs from a hub flange hole to a rim hole in 3D. Treat the hub hole as a point at flange radius f = flange diameter / 2, offset h along the axle; the rim hole sits at radius r = ERD / 2, rotated by the lacing angle. The exact length is √(r² + f² + h² − 2·r·f·cosθ) − hole/2, where the crossing angle θ = 4π · crosses / spokes and hole is the spoke-hole diameter (~2.6 mm). A radial (0-cross) build gives the shortest spoke; more crosses lace the spoke more tangentially and lengthen it. Each flange of a dished wheel has a different offset, so compute the two sides separately. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
