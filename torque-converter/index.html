About torque units

Torque is a rotational force — a force applied at a distance from a pivot — so its units pair a force with a length. The SI unit is the newton-metre (N·m). Mechanics and spec sheets often use pound-force feet (lbf·ft) for engines and lug nuts, and pound-force inches or ounce-force inches for small fasteners and electronics. Handy identities: 1 lbf·ft = 12 lbf·in, and 1 lbf·in = 16 ozf·in. Converts through newton-metres for consistency. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
