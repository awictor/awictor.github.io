About the Tower of Hanoi

Move a stack of n disks from one peg to another, one disk at a time, never placing a larger disk on a smaller one. The minimum number of moves is exactly 2ⁿ − 1, and the optimal solution is beautifully recursive: move the top n−1 disks aside, move the biggest disk, then move the n−1 disks back. The smallest disk moves every other turn (2ⁿ⁻¹ times); each larger disk moves half as often, and the biggest moves just once. The legendary 64-disk temple version would take 18,446,744,073,709,551,615 moves — at one per second, far longer than the age of the universe. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
