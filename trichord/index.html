Trichord

One question, three correct answers — the paradox of a “random” chord.

The question: pick a random chord of a circle. What is the probability it is longer than the side of the inscribed equilateral triangle (i.e. length > r√3)? Three equally valid ways to choose “at random” converge to 1/3, 1/2, and 1/4. “At random” is a measure, not a given.
