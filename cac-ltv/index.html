LTV : CAC ratio

Reading the numbers

CAC is what you pay to win a customer: sales & marketing spend ÷ customers acquired. LTV is the gross profit a customer generates over their lifetime — (ARPA × gross margin) ÷ monthly churn, since average lifetime is 1 ÷ churn months. The LTV:CAC ratio is the headline health metric: around 3:1 is considered healthy (below ~1 you lose money on every customer; far above 3 may mean you're under-investing in growth). CAC payback is how many months of margin it takes to earn the acquisition cost back. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
