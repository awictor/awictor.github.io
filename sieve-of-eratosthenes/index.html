About the sieve

The Sieve of Eratosthenes (3rd century BC) finds every prime up to N with almost no arithmetic: list the numbers, then repeatedly cross out the multiples of each prime you find. What survives is prime. It runs in about O(N log log N) — still the go-to for generating primes in bulk. The count of primes up to N is written π(N): there are 25 primes below 100, 168 below 1000. The gaps between primes grow on average like ln(N), yet primes never run out. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
