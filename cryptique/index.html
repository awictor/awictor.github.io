How to play & keyboard shortcuts

A famous quote is hidden behind a random letter-for-letter cipher. Click a tile and type a letter to guess what that symbol decodes to — it fills in every matching symbol at once. Use the frequency chart and pattern bench as leverage; correctness is checked against the real quote, so no dictionary is needed.

← → move · A–Z fill · Backspace clear · Tab next symbol. A no-hint solve grows your streak (saved on this device). The Daily puzzle is the same for everyone on a given date. Everything runs client-side — the puzzle lives in the URL, so shared links reproduce it exactly.
