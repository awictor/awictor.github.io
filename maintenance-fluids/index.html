mL/day (Holliday-Segar)
Holliday-Segar: 100 mL/kg/day for the first 10 kg, 50 for the next 10, 20 for each kg beyond 20. The 4-2-1 rule (4/2/1 mL/kg/hr) is the hourly shortcut. Maintenance only — deficits and ongoing losses are separate. Informational only, not medical advice. 100% offline. Part of the Toolkit. Source.
