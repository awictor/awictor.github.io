Beef doneness (steaks & roasts)

Note: beef below 145 °F / 63 °C is a personal-preference/food-safety trade-off; the USDA safe minimum is 145 °F with a 3-minute rest.

About

Cook to internal temperature measured with a probe thermometer in the thickest part, away from bone. Whole cuts of beef, pork, lamb and veal are safe at 145 °F (63 °C) with a 3-minute rest; ground meats need 160 °F (71 °C); all poultry 165 °F (74 °C). The doneness table is the customary temperature range cooks target for steak. Education only — follow local food-safety guidance. Everything runs locally.
