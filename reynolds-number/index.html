About the Reynolds number

The Reynolds number is a dimensionless ratio of inertial to viscous forces in a flow: Re = ρ·v·L / μ = v·L / ν, where ρ is density, v velocity, L a characteristic length (e.g. pipe diameter), μ the dynamic viscosity, and ν = μ/ρ the kinematic viscosity. It predicts the flow regime: in a pipe, roughly Re < 2300 is smooth laminar flow, 2300–4000 is transitional, and > 4000 is chaotic turbulent flow.
