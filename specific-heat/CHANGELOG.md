# Changelog

## v0.1.0 — 2026-09-11

Initial release.

- Solve Q = m·c·ΔT for heat energy, mass, specific heat, or temperature change
- Material presets (water, aluminum, iron, copper, air)
- Negative-ΔT (cooling) support and non-zero guards
- Dark mode with persistence, fully offline, no dependencies
- 10 self-tests including the 1 kg water / 10 K = 41860 J benchmark and round trips
