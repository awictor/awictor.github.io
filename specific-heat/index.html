About the heat equation

Q = m·c·ΔT gives the heat energy Q (joules) needed to change the temperature of mass m (kg) by ΔT (K or °C), where c is the material's specific heat capacity — the energy to raise 1 kg by 1 K. Water's high c (4186 J/kg·K) is why it heats and cools slowly. Rearrange to solve for any variable: c = Q/(m·ΔT), m = Q/(c·ΔT), ΔT = Q/(m·c). A negative ΔT (cooling) gives a negative Q (heat released). Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
