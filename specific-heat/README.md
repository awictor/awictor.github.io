# Specific Heat Calculator

Solve the heat equation `Q = m·c·ΔT` for **heat energy, mass, specific heat capacity, or temperature change**, with material presets (water, aluminum, iron, copper, air).

**[Open the tool →](https://awictor.github.io/specific-heat/)**

- Solve for any of Q, m, c, or ΔT
- Common material specific-heat presets
- Negative ΔT (cooling) gives negative Q (heat released)
- Dark mode, 100% offline, no dependencies, no tracking

## Example

Heating 1 kg of water by 10 °C needs **41,860 J** (c = 4186 J/kg·K).
