Local & for testing. This only checks the number's format and Luhn checksum — it can't tell if a card is real, active, or has funds. Nothing is transmitted; don't rely on it for anti-fraud.

Brand is detected from the issuer prefix (IIN); validity is the Luhn (mod-10) checksum that all card numbers satisfy. Runs 100% locally.
