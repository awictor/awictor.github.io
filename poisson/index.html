About the Poisson distribution

The Poisson distribution models the number of independent events in a fixed interval when they occur at a constant average rate λ: P(X = k) = λᵏ·e⁻ᵏ / k! (probability of exactly k events). Both its mean and variance equal λ, so the standard deviation is √λ. It fits arrivals at a queue, calls to a support line, typos per page, radioactive decays, or website hits per minute — anything rare and countable. Computed with a numerically stable recurrence (no factorial overflow). Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
