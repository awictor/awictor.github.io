Knotwright

Lay out a grid and drop walls on cell edges. A faithful mirror-curve / plait solver threads one or more continuous cords through every free cell, weaving strictly over-under-over. Everything round-trips through the URL, so any knot is a shareable link.

The algorithm (Teardown mode shows it live)

The cord travels at 45° across the grid, reflecting off walls and the border like a beam of light in a hall of mirrors. Where two beams cross with no wall, they form an over-under crossing; a checkerboard rule (over ⇔ x-parity ⊕ diagonal orientation) guarantees a globally alternating weave. The number of distinct closed loops is a genuine topological invariant, recomputed on every edit.
