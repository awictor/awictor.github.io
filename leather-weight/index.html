How it works

  • Leather is sold by weight, but the number really describes thickness: 1 oz = 1/64 inch (0.397 mm).
  • So 8 oz leather is 8/64″ = 1/8″ ≈ 3.2 mm — a firm, structural weight.
  • Ranges are common (“5–6 oz”) because natural hides vary; buy a range that brackets your target.
  • Rough guide: 2–3 oz linings & garments, 4–5 oz wallets & light goods, 6–7 oz bags & straps, 8–10 oz belts & holsters, 10 oz+ heavy tack.
  • Split leather to an exact thickness if a project needs it; skiving thins just the edges.
