HSV vs HSL

HSV (Hue, Saturation, Value) — also called HSB (Brightness) — is the model behind Photoshop, GIMP, and Figma color pickers. It differs from CSS's HSL: in HSV, full Value with full Saturation gives a vivid pure hue, and reducing Value darkens toward black. Hue is 0–360°, saturation and value are 0–100%.
