The Tinseth formula

Bitterness depends on how much of the hops' alpha acids isomerize into the wort. Tinseth's utilization combines a boil-time factor (1 − e−0.04t) ÷ 4.15 with a gravity "bigness" factor 1.65 × 0.000125(SG−1) — longer boils extract more, thicker worts extract less. IBU is that utilization times the alpha-acid concentration (AA% × grams × 1000) ÷ litres. Lagers sit near 10 IBU, pale ales 30–45, and hoppy IPAs 60+.
