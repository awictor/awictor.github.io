Palette from Image

Extract colors from a photo — locally

📷 Click or drop an image here

How it works

The image is drawn to a canvas and its pixels are sampled and grouped into color buckets; the most common buckets become your palette (averaged for a clean hex). Everything happens in your browser — the image is never uploaded. Tap a swatch to copy its hex.

100% offline · nothing leaves your browser · part of the Toolkit
