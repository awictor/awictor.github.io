The math

One inch of rain on one square foot of roof yields about 0.623 gallons; in metric, one millimetre on one square metre is exactly one litre. So collected water = area × rainfall × a collection efficiency (typically ~85–90% after splash, evaporation and first-flush diversion). Use the roof's footprint (plan area), not the sloped area — pitch doesn't change the catchment. A planning estimate; local rules on rainwater harvesting vary. Everything runs locally.
