What is small-caps text?

Small caps are Unicode letters shaped like capitals but sized like lowercase (ᴀ, ʙ, ᴄ…). Converting your lowercase letters to them — while leaving actual capitals full-height — gives the tidy "ꜱᴍᴀʟʟ ᴄᴀᴘꜱ" look popular in social-media bios and headings. It's real Unicode, so it pastes anywhere text is accepted. A couple of letters (q, x) lack a standard small-cap glyph and are left as-is. Everything runs locally.
