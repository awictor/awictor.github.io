Combining resistors

In series, resistances simply add: R = R₁ + R₂ + …. In parallel, the reciprocals add: 1/R = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂ + …, so the total is always smaller than the smallest resistor. Two in parallel simplify to R₁R₂ / (R₁ + R₂). Use series to increase resistance or split voltage, and parallel to decrease resistance or share current.
