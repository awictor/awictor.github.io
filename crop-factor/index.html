What "equivalent" means

A smaller sensor captures a narrower slice of the image a lens projects, so it appears more zoomed in. The crop factor is the full-frame diagonal (43.27 mm) divided by the sensor's diagonal. Multiply your lens's focal length by it to get the 35mm-equivalent field of view — a 35 mm lens on APS-C frames like a ~53 mm on full frame. The same factor applies to aperture for depth of field: f/1.8 on APS-C gives background blur like ~f/2.7 on full frame (exposure is unchanged). Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
