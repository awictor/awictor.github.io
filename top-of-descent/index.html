How it works

  • 3:1 rule: descend 3 NM for every 1,000 ft to lose. From FL300, start down about 90 NM out for a comfortable ~3° path.
  • Descent rate: for a 3° glidepath, vertical speed ≈ groundspeed (kt) × 5. At 450 kt that's a shade under 2,400 fpm.
  • Current glidepath: enter your distance remaining and the tool shows the angle you're on now — steeper than 3° means you're high and need to get down.
  • All ballpark planning numbers — fly the published profile, honor crossing restrictions, and adjust for wind and speed changes.
