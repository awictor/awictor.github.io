What net worth tells you

Net worth is everything you own minus everything you owe: total assets − total liabilities. Assets include cash, investments, home equity, and vehicles; liabilities include mortgages, loans, and card balances. Tracking it over time is the clearest single measure of financial progress — more useful than income alone. The debt-to-asset ratio (liabilities ÷ assets) shows how leveraged you are; below 0.5 is generally comfortable. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
