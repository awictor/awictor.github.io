About

High glucose pulls water into the blood, diluting sodium — so measured sodium reads falsely low. Correction adds ~1.6 mEq/L (Katz) or 2.4 mEq/L (Hillier/Adrogué, more accurate above ~400 mg/dL) for every 100 mg/dL of glucose over 100. Reference ranges: 135–145 mEq/L. Educational only, not a substitute for clinical judgment.
