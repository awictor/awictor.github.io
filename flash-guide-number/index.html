What the guide number tells you

A flash's guide number (GN) packages its power into one number so you can trade distance against aperture: GN = aperture × distance. So for a correct exposure, distance = GN ÷ f-number and f-number = GN ÷ distance. GN is quoted at a reference ISO (usually 100) and in meters or feet — keep your units consistent. Raising ISO makes the sensor more sensitive, extending reach by GN × √(ISO ÷ 100): every two stops of ISO doubles the guide number. This is the full-power, direct-flash figure; bouncing, diffusers, and zoom head position all change it, so treat the result as a starting point and chimp the histogram.
