Why paper weight is confusing

US paper is sold by basis weight — the weight of 500 sheets (a ream) at that grade's basis size — so the same "70 lb" means different things for text, cover, and bond because the sheets are different sizes. GSM (grams per square meter) sizes the paper directly and is grade-independent, which is why it's the clearer number. The conversion is GSM = lb × 1406.5 ÷ basis-area(in²), where the basis area is 17×22 for bond, 25×38 for text, 20×26 for cover, and 25.5×30.5 for index. That's why 20 lb bond, 50 lb text, and ~28 lb cover can all be similar GSM. Enter either field for its grade; leave GSM blank to convert from pounds.
