How it works

  • PPI = √(width² + height²) ÷ diagonal inches. The diagonal in pixels divided by the diagonal in inches.
  • Dot pitch is the spacing between pixels: 25.4 mm ÷ PPI. Smaller means sharper.
  • Higher PPI looks crisper, but at normal viewing distance most people stop noticing improvement past ~110 PPI on a desktop monitor (higher for phones held closer).
  • Two screens with the same resolution differ in sharpness only by size — a smaller panel packs the same pixels tighter.
