How it works

The Fox / Hale drag-strip formulas
  • From trap speed: HP = weight × (mph ÷ 234)³ — the classic Fox equation relating terminal speed to power-to-weight.
  • From ET: HP = weight ÷ (ET ÷ 5.825)³, using elapsed time for the quarter mile.
  • Predictions invert those: mph = 234 × (HP ÷ weight)^⅓ and ET = 5.825 × (weight ÷ HP)^⅓.
  • These estimate power at the wheels and assume a decent launch — real numbers vary with traction, air density, and gearing.
