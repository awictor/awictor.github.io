Weighing the fee against the interest

A balance transfer moves debt to a card with a low or 0% intro APR, but usually charges a one-time transfer fee (often 3–5% of the balance). It's worth it when the interest you'd otherwise pay over the intro window exceeds that fee. This compares balance × monthly rate × months at your current APR against the fee plus any intro-APR interest. It assumes the balance stays roughly constant over the window — paying it down changes the exact figures, but the comparison holds. Pay it off before the intro rate ends.
