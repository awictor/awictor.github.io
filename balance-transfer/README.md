# Balance Transfer Calculator

Compare the interest you'd pay **keeping** a credit card balance versus **transferring** it to a low/0% intro-APR card with a transfer fee — and see the net savings.

**[Open the tool →](https://awictor.github.io/balance-transfer/)**

- Interest if you stay vs. transfer cost (fee + intro interest)
- Clear worth-it / not-worth-it verdict
- For anyone weighing a 0% intro offer
- Dark mode, 100% offline, no dependencies, no tracking

## Example

$5,000 at 22% APR over an 18-month 0% window with a 3% fee: staying costs ~$1,650 in interest vs. a $150 fee — a ~$1,500 saving.
