What these ratios tell you

Three dimensionless numbers describe a sailboat's character. The sail area-displacement ratio SA / (Disp/64)^(2/3) is the power-to-weight of sailing: under ~16 is a heavy cruiser, 16–20 a typical cruiser-racer, and over 20 a performance boat. The displacement-length ratio (Disp/2240) / (0.01·LWL)³ shows how heavy the hull is for its length: over ~300 is heavy and sea-kindly, under ~150 is light and lively. The ballast ratio (ballast ÷ displacement) hints at stiffness — how much sail it can carry before heeling — with 35–45% typical for a keelboat.
