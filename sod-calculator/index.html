How to order sod

Sod is priced by the square foot but delivered as pieces (slabs or rolls) and stacked on pallets. Measure your lawn area — break odd shapes into rectangles and add them up — then divide by the piece size and pad with a waste allowance (about 5–10%) for cuts around curves, beds, and edges. A common slab is 16″ × 24″ (about 2 sq ft); rolls and big-roll sizes vary by supplier, and a pallet typically covers ~450 sq ft, so buy whole pallets for big jobs. Lay sod the same day it arrives, stagger the seams like bricks, and water it in right away. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
