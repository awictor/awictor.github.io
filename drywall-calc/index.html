How the estimate works

Wall area is perimeter × ceiling height (add the ceiling separately if you're boarding it). Divide by the area of one sheet — a standard sheet is 4 × 8 ft, or 32 ft² — then add a waste factor (10% is typical) and round up to whole sheets. Figure about 32 screws per sheet for 16″-on-center framing. This covers walls without deducting doors and windows, which the waste factor usually absorbs.
