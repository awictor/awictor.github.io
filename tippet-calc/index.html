How it works

  • Tippet is sized by the “X” system: 0X is 0.011″, and each step up (1X, 2X…) is 0.001″ thinner. So diameter = (11 − X) ÷ 1000 inch.
  • The rule of 11: X-size + breaking strength ≈ 11 for nylon, so 5X is roughly 6 lb. Fluorocarbon and premium brands test higher.
  • Match tippet to fly with the rule of 3/4/5: divide the hook size by 3 (or 4) to get the X-size — a #16 fly pairs well with 4X–5X.
  • Go as light as conditions allow for a natural drift, but heavy enough to turn the fly over and land the fish.
  • Diameter is the reliable spec; pound test varies by material, so trust the thousandths.
