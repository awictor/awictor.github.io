About the ideal gas law

The ideal gas law PV = nRT links pressure, volume, amount, and temperature of a gas, with the universal gas constant R = 8.314462618 J/(mol·K). Rearranged, you can solve for any one variable from the other three. It bundles Boyle's law (P ∝ 1/V), Charles's law (V ∝ T), and Avogadro's law into one equation. A classic check: one mole at 0 °C (273.15 K) and 1 atm (101325 Pa) occupies 22.414 litres — the molar volume at STP. Use SI units (Pa, m³, mol, K).
