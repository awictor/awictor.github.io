How it works

Compression ratio is (swept volume + clearance volume) ÷ clearance volume. Swept volume is what one cylinder displaces from bottom to top; clearance volume is everything left when the piston is at the top — combustion chamber, head gasket, deck height, and any piston dish. To hit a target ratio, the clearance volume you need is swept ÷ (CR − 1). A 500 cc cylinder with 50 cc of clearance runs 11:1. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
