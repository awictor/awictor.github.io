Aircraft Fuel Endurance

Flight time & range from fuel, burn & airspeed

How it works

Endurance, range & reserves
  • Endurance = usable fuel ÷ burn rate. Still-air range = endurance × true airspeed.
  • A VFR reserve (30 min day, 45 min night in the US) is held back: usable-after-reserve = fuel − burn × reserve.
  • Fuel for a leg = (distance ÷ TAS) × burn — always plan to land with your reserve intact.
  • This is still-air planning; real flights add wind, taxi, climb, and weather. Everything runs in your browser.
