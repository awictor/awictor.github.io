How it works

  • Ideal distance ≈ screen diagonal × a factor that depends on resolution.
  • 4K packs pixels tight, so you can sit closer (~1.2× the diagonal); 1080p wants ~1.6× so pixels don't show.
  • Sitting closer fills more of your vision and feels more immersive; farther is easier on the eyes for long sessions.
  • Flip the mode to size a new TV to the couch you already have.
