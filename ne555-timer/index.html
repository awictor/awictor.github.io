The 555 formulas

In astable mode the 555 oscillates at f = 1.44 ÷ ((R1 + 2·R2)·C). The output is high while the capacitor charges through R1+R2 and low while it discharges through R2, so the duty cycle is (R1 + R2) ÷ (R1 + 2·R2) — always above 50% for a basic circuit. In monostable mode a trigger produces a single pulse of width 1.1·R·C. Enter resistance in ohms and capacitance in farads (1 µF = 0.000001 F). Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
