Which method?

Straight-line spreads the depreciable base (cost − salvage) evenly across the asset's life — the simplest and most common. Double-declining balance is accelerated: it applies twice the straight-line rate to the remaining book value each year, front-loading the expense, then switches to straight-line on the remaining balance so it fully reaches the salvage value. Sum-of-years-digits is also accelerated but on a fixed fractional schedule (n, n−1, … over the digit sum). All three fully depreciate to the salvage value.
