# WBGT Heat Stress

Calculate wet bulb globe temperature (WBGT) from wet-bulb, globe, and air temperatures — indoors or in sun — with the activity heat-stress flag (green/yellow/red/black). Free, offline, single-file — nothing leaves your browser.

**Live:** https://awictor.github.io/wbgt-calc/

Part of the [Toolkit](https://awictor.github.io/toolkit/).
