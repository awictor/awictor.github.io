How it works

The WBGT formula & flags
  • Outdoor (with solar load): WBGT = 0.7·Tw + 0.2·Tg + 0.1·Ta.
  • Indoor / no sun: WBGT = 0.7·Tw + 0.3·Tg.
  • Wet-bulb temperature carries 70% of the weight — humidity, which limits sweat evaporation, drives heat stress far more than air temperature alone.
  • The activity flag follows common athletic/military thresholds: green, yellow, red, then black — modify or cancel work as it climbs.
  • This is a "feels-like for exertion," distinct from the heat index. Everything runs in your browser.
