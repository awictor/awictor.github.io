How it works

  • Meat keeps cooking after it leaves the heat — the hot exterior drives heat inward. That's carryover.
  • So you pull it early: pull temp = target − expected rise. Pull a 135°F medium-rare roast at about 127°F.
  • Bigger and hotter-cooked cuts carry over more (8–15°F); thin steaks and low-and-slow barbecue carry over less (a few degrees).
  • Rest tented for 10–20 minutes so the temperature evens out and juices redistribute before slicing.
  • Always hit safe minimums for poultry and ground meat after carryover, not before.
