How it works

  • Protein needs scale with bodyweight, not calories. The math is simply weight (kg) × a goal factor.
  • The RDA is 0.8 g/kg — a minimum to avoid deficiency, not an optimum. Active people and lifters need more.
  • For building or preserving muscle, 1.6–2.2 g/kg is the well-supported range; going much higher rarely adds benefit.
  • Spreading protein across 3–5 meals of 20–40 g each maximizes muscle protein synthesis better than one big dose.
  • Pounds are converted to kilograms (÷ 2.205) before applying the factor.
