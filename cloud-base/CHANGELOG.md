Initial release.

- Metric (÷2.5) and Fahrenheit (÷4.4) formulas, feet and meters
- Input guards rejecting dew point above temperature
- Dark mode with persistence, fully offline, no dependencies
- 10 self-tests including the saturated and monotonic cases
