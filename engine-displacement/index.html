How displacement is found

Each cylinder sweeps a volume equal to π/4 × bore² × stroke. Multiply by the cylinder count for total displacement. With bore and stroke in millimetres the result is cubic millimetres; divide by 1,000 for cc, by a million for litres, and by 16.387 for cubic inches. A 101.6 mm bore, 88.4 mm stroke V8 comes out near 5.7 L (350 ci). Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
