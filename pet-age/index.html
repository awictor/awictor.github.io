Dog & cat age in human years
—
human years (AKC table)
The AKC guideline: a dog's first year ≈ 15 human years, the second ≈ 9 more, then 4–6 per year by size; cats add ~4/year after age two. The 2020 epigenetic formula (UCSD) is 16·ln(age)+31. A fun estimate, not veterinary advice. 100% offline. Part of the Toolkit. Source.
