A1C ↔ estimated average glucose

Estimated average glucose (eAG) uses the ADAG study formula eAG = 28.7 × A1C − 46.7 (mg/dL). Categories follow ADA cutoffs (Normal < 5.7%, Prediabetes 5.7–6.4%, Diabetes ≥ 6.5%). Not medical advice. Runs entirely in your browser.
