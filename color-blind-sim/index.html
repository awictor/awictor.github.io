ColorBlind

Color blindness palette simulator

ColorNormalProtanopiaDeuteranopiaTritanopiaGrayscale

Roughly ~8% of men and ~0.5% of women have some color vision deficiency — most commonly red-green (protanopia/deuteranopia). If two swatches look identical in a column, that pair is a risk in your UI. Simulation uses standard CVD transform matrices (an approximation). Runs 100% locally.
