How it works

  • Stand a known baseline away from the pad and measure the elevation angle to the rocket at apogee with a tracker or altiscope.
  • Altitude above your eye = baseline × tan(angle). Add your eye height for altitude above the ground.
  • At 45° the altitude equals the baseline — a handy sanity check. The estimate gets touchy above ~80°.
  • Slant range = baseline ÷ cos(angle) is the straight-line distance to the rocket, always longer than the altitude.
  • Single-station assumes the rocket goes straight up over the pad. Wind drift is why clubs use two stations and average.
