How it works

  • Frame size scales with your inseam (crotch to floor, barefoot), not just height — leg length is what fits a bike.
  • Rules of thumb: road frame (cm) = inseam × 0.665; mountain = inseam × 0.226 for inches (× 0.574 for cm); hybrid ≈ inseam × 0.63.
  • You want a couple of inches of standover clearance over the top tube — more for a mountain bike, less for road.
  • These get you the right ballpark; final fit depends on reach, saddle height, and stem — test ride before buying.
  • Between two sizes? Size down for a sportier, more maneuverable fit; up for comfort and stability.
