How it works

Temperature, time & infusions
  • Delicate teas (green, white) want cooler water (~80 °C); black, pu-erh, and herbal want near-boiling.
  • Strength stretches or shortens the steep — longer for bolder, shorter for lighter.
  • Gongfu style uses more leaf in a small vessel with many short infusions, each a little longer than the last.
  • These are starting points — taste as you go and adjust. Everything runs in your browser.
