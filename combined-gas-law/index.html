How it works

The gas laws
  • For a fixed amount of gas, P₁V₁ / T₁ = P₂V₂ / T₂ — pressure, volume, and absolute temperature trade off together.
  • Temperatures must be absolute (Kelvin): K = °C + 273.15. Using Celsius gives wrong answers.
  • Special cases: Boyle's (constant T: P₁V₁ = P₂V₂), Charles's (constant P: V₁/T₁ = V₂/T₂), Gay-Lussac's (constant V: P₁/T₁ = P₂/T₂).
