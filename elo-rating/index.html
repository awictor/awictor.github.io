How Elo works

Your expected score against an opponent is 1 ÷ (1 + 10^((opp − you) ÷ 400)) — a 400-point gap means the favourite is expected to score about 91%. After a game your rating moves by K × (actual − expected), where actual is 1 for a win, 0.5 for a draw, 0 for a loss. The K-factor sets volatility: 40 for newcomers, 20 for experienced players, 10 for masters. Beating a stronger opponent gains more than beating a weaker one.
