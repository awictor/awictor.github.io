How it works

  • Gross trailer weight (GTW) = trailer empty weight + everything you load in it.
  • It must stay under your vehicle's tow rating — leave margin for fuel, water, and gear.
  • Tongue weight should be 10–15% of GTW for a bumper-pull trailer; too little causes dangerous sway.
  • Also check payload: tongue weight plus passengers and truck-bed cargo must stay under the truck's payload rating.
