Why power-to-weight matters

On a climb you fight gravity, so what counts is watts per kilogram of body weight — power ÷ weight. It's the great equaliser: a light rider with modest power can drop a powerful heavy one uphill. At threshold (FTP), roughly 2–3 W/kg is recreational, 3–4 is a strong club rider, 4–5 is racing shape, and above 5 W/kg is elite. Enter weight in kg or lb.
