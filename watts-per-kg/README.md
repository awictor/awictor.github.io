# Watts per Kilogram Calculator

Calculate cycling **power-to-weight** (W/kg) from FTP and body weight, the FTP-per-kg **category**, and the watts needed to hit a target W/kg.

**[Open the tool →](https://awictor.github.io/watts-per-kg/)**

- W/kg = power ÷ weight (kg or lb input)
- FTP category from untrained to world class
- Watts needed for a target W/kg
- Dark mode, 100% offline, no dependencies, no tracking

## Example

280 W at 75 kg = 3.73 W/kg — a strong club rider. To reach 4 W/kg at that weight you need 300 W.
