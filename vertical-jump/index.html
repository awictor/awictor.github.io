The physics of hang time

Once you leave the ground you're a projectile, so jump height and air time are locked together by gravity. Height gives hang time as t = 2·√(2h / g) (up and back down), and inversely h = g·t² / 8. A 60 cm vertical is only about 0.7 s of hang time — the sense of "hanging" is mostly illusion. Takeoff speed is √(2gh). Time a jump on video (frames ÷ fps) and this back-solves your vertical. Uses g = 9.80665 m/s². Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
