About the theorem

The Chinese Remainder Theorem says a system of congruences x ≡ a₁ (mod n₁), x ≡ a₂ (mod n₂), … has a unique solution modulo the least common multiple of the moduli — provided they're consistent. The classic 3rd-century problem of Sunzi asks for a number leaving remainders 2, 3, 2 when divided by 3, 5, 7; the answer is 23. This solver combines the congruences pairwise using the extended Euclidean algorithm, so it works even when the moduli aren't coprime, and reports when no solution exists. It's the mathematical backbone of RSA's fast decryption and many hashing schemes. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
