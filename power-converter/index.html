About power units

Power is energy per unit time; the SI unit is the watt (1 W = 1 J/s). Engines are rated in horsepower — but two kinds: mechanical hp (≈745.7 W, used in the US/UK) and metric hp / PS (≈735.5 W, common in Europe). Heating and air-conditioning use BTU per hour (a "1-ton" AC unit is 12,000 BTU/h ≈ 3.5 kW). This tool converts through watts so everything stays consistent.
