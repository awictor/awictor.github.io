How the clock angle works

A clock face is 360°, so the minute hand moves 6° per minute (360 ÷ 60). The hour hand moves 30° per hour (360 ÷ 12) plus 0.5° per minute, since it creeps forward as the minutes pass — that half-degree is what people forget. Each hand's position is measured clockwise from 12. The angle between them is the difference, |30·H + 0.5·M − 6·M|, and if that comes out over 180° we take the shorter way round (360 − angle). At 3:00 the hands are exactly 90° apart; at 3:15 they're only 7.5° apart, not 0, because the hour hand has already moved a quarter of the way toward 4.
