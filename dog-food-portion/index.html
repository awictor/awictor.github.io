How it works

  • Resting energy: RER = 70 × (weight in kg)^0.75 kcal per day.
  • Daily need = RER × an activity multiplier (weight loss 1.0, neutered adult 1.6, active 2.0, puppy 2.5).
  • Cups per day = daily calories ÷ the kcal-per-cup printed on your food's bag.
  • This is a starting estimate — adjust to keep your dog at a healthy weight, and ask your vet for medical cases.
