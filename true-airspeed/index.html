How it works

  • Your airspeed indicator reads dynamic pressure, so in thinner air the same indicated speed means you're actually moving faster through it.
  • True airspeed = calibrated airspeed ÷ √σ, where σ is the air density ratio vs. sea level.
  • σ comes from the standard atmosphere: σ = (1 − 6.875×10⁻⁶ × h)4.2561 for altitude h in feet.
  • Rough check: TAS climbs about 2% per 1,000 ft — 120 kt at 10,000 ft is near 140 kt true.
  • This uses ISA conditions; hot days thin the air further and push TAS higher. Above ~36,000 ft the formula no longer applies.
