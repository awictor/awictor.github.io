How it works

  • Steeper roofs catch more wind-driven rain, so footprint area is scaled by a pitch factor (1.0 to 1.3).
  • Multiply by your local rainfall intensity to get an equivalent area at the chart's 1"/hr basis.
  • A 5" K-style gutter handles about 5,520 of those sq ft; a 6" handles about 7,960.
  • Beyond that, add downspouts or go commercial — and add a downspout roughly every 30–40 ft regardless.
