How it works

  • A well-gapped set has consistent yardage steps — roughly 10–15 yards between clubs — so you always have a club for the distance.
  • Too tight a gap (< 8 yd) means two clubs do the same job; too big (> 15 yd) leaves a distance you can only hit with an awkward stock swing.
  • The even-spacing figure divides your full range by the gaps between clubs — a target to compare your real carries against.
  • Use carry distances (not total with roll) from a launch monitor or range session; gaps drift as clubs and swings change.
  • Fix big gaps by bending lofts, adding a gap wedge, or adjusting shaft/loft on adjacent clubs.
