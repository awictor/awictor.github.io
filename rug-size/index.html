Sizing a room rug

A rug looks best when it leaves an even strip of bare floor framing it — designers suggest a border of about 12–24 inches (1–2 ft) on each side. So the rug's dimensions are room − 2 × border in each direction. In a living room, aim for the rug to sit under at least the front legs of the sofa and chairs; in a dining room it should extend about 24″ past the table so chairs stay on it when pulled out. Rugs come in standard sizes (5×8, 6×9, 8×10, 9×12, 10×14…), so round to the nearest one that keeps a reasonably even border. Bigger rugs make a room feel larger; postage-stamp rugs make it feel choppy. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
