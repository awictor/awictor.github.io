About

Lenders cap your total monthly debt (housing + other) at a share of gross income — the debt-to-income (DTI) ratio, commonly ~36% (up to ~43–50% for some loans). This estimates the biggest loan whose payment fits your remaining room, then adds your down payment for a price. It's principal + interest only — property tax, insurance, HOA, and PMI aren't included, so the real budget is lower. A starting estimate, not a pre-approval.
