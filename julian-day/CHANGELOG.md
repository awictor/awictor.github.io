# Changelog

## v0.1.0 — 2026-09-11

Initial release.

- Gregorian date → Julian Day Number (Fliegel–Van Flandern), plus MJD, weekday, day-of-year
- Julian Day Number → Gregorian date (reverse conversion)
- Dark mode with persistence, fully offline, no dependencies
- 10 self-tests including the J2000, Unix, and MJD epochs and a leap-year span
