About the Julian Day

The Julian Day Number (JDN) is a continuous count of days since noon on 1 January 4713 BC (proleptic Julian calendar) — astronomers use it to compute time spans without calendar quirks. JD 2451545 is noon on 2000-01-01 (the J2000.0 epoch); the Unix epoch (1970-01-01) is JDN 2440588. The Modified Julian Day MJD = JD − 2400000.5 starts at midnight and is common in satellite and geophysics data. This uses the Fliegel–Van Flandern algorithm for exact integer conversion both ways.
