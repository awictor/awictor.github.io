Reading the numbers

TTR (type-token ratio) is unique words ÷ total words — higher means more varied vocabulary, but it drops as text gets longer, so compare similar lengths. Guiraud's index (types ÷ √tokens) is more length-stable. Hapax legomena are words that appear exactly once — a rough sign of richness. Comparisons and non-letters are ignored; matching is case-insensitive.
