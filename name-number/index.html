How it works

  • The Pythagorean system maps A–I to 1–9, J–R to 1–9 again, and S–Z to 1–8, cycling every nine letters.
  • Add the value of every letter in the full name, then reduce to a single digit — keeping the master numbers 11, 22, 33.
  • The result is your Expression Number, said to reflect natural talents and how you present to the world.
  • Non-letters (spaces, hyphens, apostrophes) are ignored, so “Mary-Jane O'Neil” counts just its letters.
  • For fun and reflection — a deterministic bit of letter arithmetic with a long tradition.
