About roasting times

Roasting time scales roughly with weight: total = weight × minutes-per-pound at the recommended oven temperature. These are starting estimates — always confirm with a meat thermometer at the thickest part, away from bone. USDA safe minimums: poultry 165°F, whole cuts of beef/pork/lamb 145°F (with a 3-minute rest), ground meats 160°F. Let large roasts rest 15–20 minutes before carving.
