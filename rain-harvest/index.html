How it works

  • One inch of rain on one square foot is 0.623 gallons — so gallons = area × rainfall × 0.623.
  • Measure the roof's footprint (flat area it covers), not the sloped surface — pitch doesn't change catchment.
  • Real systems lose water to splash, first-flush diversion, and evaporation, so a collection efficiency of 80–90% is realistic.
  • Even a modest roof captures hundreds of gallons per inch — size your barrels and overflow accordingly.
