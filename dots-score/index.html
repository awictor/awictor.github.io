How it works

  • DOTS normalizes a lifter's total by bodyweight so a 60 kg and a 120 kg lifter can be ranked fairly. It replaced the older Wilks formula in most federations.
  • It fits a coefficient to bodyweight: DOTS = total × 500 ÷ (a + b·bw + c·bw² + d·bw³ + e·bw⁴).
  • Lighter lifters get a higher coefficient, offsetting the raw-weight advantage of heavier lifters.
  • Rough guide: ~300 solid, ~400 strong, ~500 elite, ~600 world-class.
  • Use your best competition total in kilograms; the men's and women's coefficient sets differ.
