How it works

  • A deck beam carries the load of the joists framing into it. The longer that joist span, the more tributary load per foot, so the beam can span less between posts.
  • Values follow IRC Table R507.5 for Southern Pine (40 psf live + 10 psf dead), rounded to the nearest inch.
  • Joist span rounds up to the next table column (6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 18 ft) — a 9-ft joist span uses the 10-ft column.
  • Tripling a beam or going one size deeper buys noticeably more span; check the recommendation row.
  • Always verify against your local code and the actual lumber species/grade stamped on your boards.
