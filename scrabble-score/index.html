How scoring works

Each letter in English Scrabble has a fixed point value based on how common it is: vowels and common consonants are worth 1, while rare letters like Q and Z are worth 10. A word's base score is the sum of its tiles. (Premium board squares — double/triple letter and word — and the 50-point bonus for playing all seven tiles depend on the board, so they aren't included here.)
