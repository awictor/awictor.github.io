About WACC

The weighted average cost of capital is a company's blended cost of financing — the minimum return its investments must earn to satisfy both shareholders and lenders. Each source is weighted by its share of total capital: WACC = (E/V)·Re + (D/V)·Rd·(1−Tc), where V = E + D. Debt is multiplied by (1 − tax rate) because interest is tax-deductible (the "tax shield"), making debt cheaper than its headline rate. WACC is the standard discount rate for valuing a firm or a project. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
