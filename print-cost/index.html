What goes into the cost

Filament cost is simply the grams used times the spool's price per gram (spool price ÷ spool weight). Electricity is hours × watts ÷ 1000 × rate — usually small, but it adds up on long prints. Filament length comes from the mass, the material density (PLA ≈ 1.24 g/cm³), and the strand diameter. This covers material and power; add your own margin for machine wear, failures, and labour when pricing a job. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
