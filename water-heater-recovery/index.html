How it works

  • Energy to heat water = gallons × 8.34 lb × temperature rise (°F), in BTU.
  • Recovery time = energy ÷ (heater input × efficiency). Electric watts convert to BTU/hr by ×3.412.
  • Electric elements are ~98% efficient; gas burners are lower (~75–80%), so gas needs more input BTU.
  • Temperature rise is your target minus the incoming cold-water temp — often 40–50 °F in summer, 70+ in winter.
