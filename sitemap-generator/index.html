About sitemaps

A sitemap.xml lists the URLs on your site so search engines can discover and prioritize them. Each <url> has a required <loc> plus optional <lastmod> (ISO date), <changefreq> (how often it changes), and <priority> (0.0–1.0, relative importance). Ampersands and other special characters in URLs must be XML-escaped — this tool does that automatically. Reference it from your robots.txt with a Sitemap: line. Everything is generated locally; nothing leaves your browser.
