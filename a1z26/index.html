About the A1Z26 cipher

A1Z26 is the simplest letter-to-number substitution: A = 1, B = 2, … Z = 26. Encoding replaces each letter with its position in the alphabet; decoding reverses it. Words are separated by a space in both directions, and letters within a word by your chosen separator (a dash by default). It offers no real secrecy — it's a puzzle cipher, beloved in escape rooms, geocaching, and ARGs. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
