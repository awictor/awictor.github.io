Why the speedo lies

Your speedometer counts wheel revolutions and assumes the original tire's rolling diameter. Fit taller tires and each revolution covers more ground, so you're actually going faster than the needle shows: actual = indicated × new ÷ original diameter. Diameter from a tire code is rim + 2 × (width × aspect ÷ 100) ÷ 25.4 inches. A plus-size that changes diameter more than ~3% is usually worth recalibrating.
