How the estimate works

Fabric comes on a bolt of a fixed usable width (often about 40–42″ after trimming selvages from a 44/45″ bolt). Lay your pieces across that width: the number that fit in one row is floor(fabric width ÷ piece width). The rows you need is ceil(pieces ÷ per row), and the length to buy is rows × piece length, converted to yards at 36″. Always include your seam allowance in the piece dimensions, round up when buying, and add a little extra for shrinkage, squaring up, and pattern matching on directional or plaid prints. This assumes pieces aren't rotated to nest — real cutting layouts can do better. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
