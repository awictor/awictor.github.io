How it works

  • Lumens = watts × efficacy (lumens per watt). Watts measure energy used; lumens measure light produced.
  • Typical efficacy: incandescent ~15, halogen ~20, CFL ~60, LED ~90 lm/W. That's why a 10 W LED matches a 60 W incandescent.
  • Shop by lumens, not watts — a 800-lumen LED and 800-lumen incandescent give the same light, at very different power.
  • These are typical figures; check the bulb's label for its exact lumen rating.
