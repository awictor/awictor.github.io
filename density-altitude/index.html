Why it matters

Density altitude is the altitude your aircraft "feels" — how thin the air actually is. First convert the altimeter setting to a pressure altitude: elevation + (29.92 − setting) × 1000. The standard (ISA) temperature there falls about 2°C per 1000 ft from 15°C at sea level. Every degree hotter than standard adds roughly 120 ft of density altitude, robbing engine power, lift, and prop thrust. On a hot day a 5,000 ft field can perform like 8,000 ft.
