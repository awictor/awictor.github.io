Rootbloom

A live explorer for Newton-method fractals: the basin-of-attraction maps you get by iterating z ← z − a·p(z)/p′(z) toward the roots of a complex polynomial. The polynomial is defined by its roots — so you can literally grab a root and drag it, and the fractal reflows in real time.

Methods

Newton — classic z − p/p′. Relaxed Newton — complex coefficient a; spin it around the unit circle to make basins spiral. Halley — third-order, distinct petal geometry. Nova — adds a constant c each step for off-set blooms.

Shareable state

Everything — roots, view, method, coefficients, palette, iteration budget — is packed into the URL after #. Hit Copy link in the panel to grab a shareable URL for the current bloom, or use the gallery presets below.

Requirements

Any WebGL-capable browser with float-precision fragment shaders. Runs fully offline; no network calls, no build step. Deep zoom is limited by 32-bit GPU float precision (roughly 10⁵×).

WebGL unavailable

Rootbloom needs a WebGL context with float-precision fragment shaders to render Newton fractals on the GPU. Your browser or device didn't provide one.

Try a recent version of Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari, enable hardware acceleration, and make sure WebGL isn't blocked.
