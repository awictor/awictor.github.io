Why the setting changes

Adding a crowfoot or extension in line with the handle lengthens the wrench's effective lever, so the fastener sees more torque than the dial shows — you have to dial down the setting to compensate. With wrench length L (pivot to the center of the handle grip) and extension E in the same direction, set the wrench to target × L ÷ (L + E). If the crowfoot sticks out at 90° to the handle it doesn't change the lever length at all — no adjustment needed. Measure L along the actual line of pull, keep the extension collinear, and re-check with the same reference. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
