Frequency ↔ Wavelength

For radio, light & other EM waves

The relationship

For any wave, speed = frequency × wavelength. Electromagnetic waves travel at the speed of light c ≈ 299,792,458 m/s in a vacuum, so λ = c / f and f = c / λ. A 100 MHz FM signal has a ~3-metre wavelength; visible light is hundreds of nanometres. The period is 1/f, and antenna builders care about the quarter- and half-wave lengths (λ/4, λ/2).
