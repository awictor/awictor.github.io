From footprint to shingles

A sloped roof always has more surface than the flat footprint it covers. The pitch — rise in inches per 12 inches of horizontal run — sets a slope multiplier of √(pitch² + 12²) ÷ 12: a flat 0:12 roof is 1.00, a 6:12 about 1.12, and a 12:12 (45°) about 1.41. Multiply the plan footprint by it to get the true roof area. Roofing is sold in squares of 100 sq ft, so divide by 100 and add a waste allowance (about 10%, more for hips, valleys, and complex cuts). Measure the footprint from the eaves including overhangs, and add up each roof plane for a multi-section roof. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
