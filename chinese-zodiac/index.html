How the Chinese zodiac cycles

The Chinese zodiac runs on two interlocking wheels. The 12 animals — Rat, Ox, Tiger, Rabbit, Dragon, Snake, Horse, Goat, Monkey, Rooster, Dog, Pig — repeat every 12 years, so the animal is (year − 4) mod 12. The five elements — Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water — each hold for two years, cycling over 10, so together they form the famous 60-year sexagenary cycle; the element is (year − 4) mod 10, paired up. That anchor makes 1984 the Wood Rat and 2024 the Wood Dragon. Note the Chinese New Year falls in late January or February, so a birthday early in the year may belong to the previous animal — check the exact date for edge cases.
