🔦 How to play

    
Emitters fire colored beams. Left-click / tap a tile to rotate it 90° clockwise; right-click (or long-press on touch) for counter-clockwise. Light a receiver with exactly the color it demands. Colors mix additively: R + G = Yellow, all three = White.

    

    
Tiles

    

      
◇
Mirror — reflects a beam 90° (/ or \).

      
╳
Splitter — passes the beam straight and reflects a copy 90°.

      
▲
Prism — when a beam runs along its axis it fans into primaries: Red turns left, Green goes straight, Blue turns right.

      
▣
Filter — keeps only one primary of a passing beam. Rotating cycles R→G→B.

      
●
Emitter / Receiver / Wall — fixed. Receivers lock when the incoming color matches.

    

    
Keys: Z undo · Y redo · R reset · H hint · N new · arrows move cursor · Space rotate.
