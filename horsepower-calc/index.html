How it works

Horsepower is torque doing work over time: hp = torque (lb-ft) × RPM ÷ 5252. The constant 5252 is where the torque and power curves always cross on a dyno chart, because it converts foot-pounds per minute into the 33,000 that defines one horsepower. Rearrange to get torque from a known power figure, and multiply hp by 0.7457 for kilowatts. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
