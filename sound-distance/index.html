The inverse-square law

For a point source in the open, sound level drops 20 × log₁₀(d₂ ÷ d₁) dB with distance — that's 6 dB per doubling, or 20 dB for ten times the distance. To hit a target level, back-solve the distance. Adding a second equal source raises the level by only 3 dB (levels add as power, not arithmetically): 10 × log₁₀(10^{A/10} + 10^{B/10}). Real rooms reflect sound, so outdoors these are worst-case estimates. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
