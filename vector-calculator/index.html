About vector operations

The dot product A·B = Σ aᵢbᵢ = |A||B|cosθ measures alignment — zero means perpendicular. The cross product (3D) gives a vector perpendicular to both, with length |A||B|sinθ equal to the parallelogram area; it's anti-commutative (A×B = −B×A). The magnitude is the vector's length, and normalizing scales it to length 1 while keeping its direction. These are the building blocks of graphics, physics, and machine learning.
