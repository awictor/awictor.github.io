How it works

  • Rigid-frame formula: L = 2 × chainstay + (chainring ÷ 4) + (cog ÷ 4) + 1 inches.
  • Chain pitch is 1/2 inch, so links = length × 2, then rounded up to an even number (a chain needs matching inner and outer links).
  • Size to the big-big combo (largest chainring and largest cog) so the chain is never too short.
  • Full-suspension bikes need extra length for suspension travel — add per the frame maker's guidance.
