How to play

Shade every cell one of two colors — black or white — so that:

  • All black cells form one connected group (touching edge-to-edge).
  • All white cells form one connected group.
  • No 2×2 area is entirely one color.

Ringed cells are fixed clues you can't change. Tap or click a cell to cycle empty → black → white → empty; with the keyboard, arrow keys move and Space/Enter cycle.

Every puzzle has exactly one solution, reachable by logic alone. Stuck? Hint shows the next forced move and Replay logic walks the whole deduction chain.
