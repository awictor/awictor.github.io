About waist-to-hip ratio

Waist-to-hip ratio (WHR) compares the circumference of your waist to your hips — a simple proxy for how much fat is carried around the abdomen, which is more strongly linked to cardiovascular and metabolic risk than overall weight. Measure both in the same unit (the ratio is unitless). The World Health Organization flags higher risk above 0.90 for men and 0.85 for women. This is an educational aid, not medical advice.
