About filter cutoff

A first-order RC filter's cutoff (−3 dB) frequency is f_c = 1/(2πRC) — the point where the output drops to about 70.7% of the input. Below it a low-pass passes signals; above it a high-pass does. The time constant τ = RC sets how fast the circuit charges (one τ reaches ≈ 63%). An LC tank resonates at f = 1/(2π√(LC)), the heart of oscillators and radio tuning.
