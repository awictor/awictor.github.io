How it works

Blending & dilution
  • Pre-dilution ABV is the volume-weighted average of every pour: Σ(vol × abv) ÷ Σ vol.
  • Chilling melts ice into the drink. Typical water added, as a share of the drink's volume: on the rocks ≈ 15%, stirred ≈ 24%, shaken ≈ 30%.
  • Final volume grows by that fraction; final ABV falls by the same factor — the alcohol itself is conserved.
  • These are craft-bar starting points; real dilution depends on ice, time, and technique. Everything runs in your browser.
