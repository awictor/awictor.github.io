How it works

  • Energy per day (kWh) = watts × hours ÷ 1000. Cost = kWh × your rate per kWh.
  • Find the wattage on the appliance label or nameplate; motors and heaters draw the most.
  • Your rate is on your utility bill — the U.S. average is roughly $0.15–0.18 per kWh.
  • Monthly uses 30 days and yearly 365 — always-on devices add up even at low wattage.
