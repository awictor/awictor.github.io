How it works

  • Every oil needs a specific amount of lye to fully saponify — its SAP value (grams of NaOH per gram of oil).
  • Lye = oil weight × SAP value × (1 − superfat). Superfat leaves a little oil unsaponified for a gentler, more moisturizing bar (5% is common).
  • Water dissolves the lye; a 2:1 water-to-lye ratio is a safe default. Less water gives a harder, faster-tracing batch.
  • Always run your exact recipe through a trusted lye calculator and weigh everything — lye is caustic; wear gloves and eye protection.
  • SAP values here are single-oil averages. Blends should be computed oil-by-oil and summed.
