About the math

Price is the present value of all coupon payments plus the redemption (face) value, discounted at the periodic yield. Yield to maturity is solved numerically (bisection). Macaulay duration is the coupon-weighted average time to cash flows; modified duration ≈ Macaulay ÷ (1 + periodic yield) and estimates the % price change for a 1% change in yield. Prices trade below par when the yield exceeds the coupon (discount) and above par when it's lower (premium).
