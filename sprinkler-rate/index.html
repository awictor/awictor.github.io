How to schedule watering

A zone's precipitation rate is how fast it lays down water, in inches per hour: 96.25 × GPM ÷ area(ft²), where 96.25 converts gallons per minute over an area into inches per hour. Add up the flow of every head running at once and the total area they cover. Then the runtime to apply a target depth is target inches ÷ rate × 60 minutes. Lawns usually want about 1 inch per week total, split into a couple of sessions; if the rate outpaces how fast the soil absorbs (common with clay or slopes), cycle and soak in shorter bursts to avoid runoff. Rates vary head to head, so measure with catch cups for accuracy. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
