About

Checksums detect accidental data corruption. Adler-32 (used in zlib) is faster than a CRC but weaker for short inputs; Fletcher-16 is a lightweight position-sensitive checksum. Both run over the UTF-8 bytes of your text. For cryptographic integrity use a hash (SHA-256), not a checksum.
