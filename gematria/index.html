About

Gematria assigns a number to each letter and sums them. Ordinal uses A=1 … Z=26; Reduction (Pythagorean) collapses each letter to 1–9; Reverse counts A=26 down to Z=1. It's a word-game and numerology curiosity — fun for finding words that share a value. Only letters count; case and punctuation are ignored.
