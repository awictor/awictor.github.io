How much to order

Volume is length × width × depth — convert the depth from inches to feet first (÷12). Landscape suppliers sell by the cubic yard (÷27) or by weight; gravel weighs roughly 1.4 tons per cubic yard, though sand, crushed stone, and topsoil differ. A 20×10 ft area at 4 in deep is about 2.5 yd³, or ~3.5 tons of gravel. Order 5–10% extra for compaction and spillage. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
