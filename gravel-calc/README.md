# Gravel Calculator

Calculate how much **gravel, aggregate, or topsoil** you need: cubic feet, cubic yards, and tons from area and depth.

**[Open the tool →](https://awictor.github.io/gravel-calc/)**

- Volume = length × width × depth (in → ft)
- Cubic yards (÷27) and tons at your material density
- Adjustable tons-per-yard density
- Dark mode, 100% offline, no dependencies, no tracking

## Example

A 20 × 10 ft area at 4 in deep ≈ 2.5 yd³, or ~3.5 tons of gravel. Order 5–10% extra.
