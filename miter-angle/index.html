Frame Miter Calculator

Miter angle & segment lengths for any-sided project

How it works

The frame geometry
  • Miter saw angle for an N-sided frame is 180 ÷ N degrees — 45° for a square, 30° for a hexagon, 22.5° for an octagon.
  • Interior corner angle is (N − 2) × 180 ÷ N. The two identities meet at miter = 90 − interior ÷ 2.
  • Segment length (outer edge) for a ring whose outer points touch a circle of diameter D is D × sin(180° ÷ N).
  • Board footage = N segments × outer length, padded by your cutting-waste allowance.
  • Works for picture frames, hexagonal planters, and segmented turning rings alike. Everything runs in your browser.
