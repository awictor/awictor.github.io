About

FENa = (UNa × PCr) / (PNa × UCr) × 100. It helps distinguish causes of acute kidney injury: <1% suggests prerenal (the kidney is conserving sodium), >2% suggests intrinsic damage (e.g. ATN). Diuretics invalidate FENa — use FEUrea instead (<35% suggests prerenal). Interpretation is clinical; contrast nephropathy and other states can mislead. Education only.
