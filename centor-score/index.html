About the score

The Centor criteria estimate how likely a sore throat is bacterial (group A strep): +1 each for fever, tonsillar exudate/swelling, tender anterior cervical nodes, and absence of cough. The McIsaac modification adjusts for age — +1 if 3–14, 0 if 15–44, −1 if 45+ — since strep is common in children and rare in older adults. Rough guidance: ≤0 very low risk (no test/antibiotics), 1 low, 2–3 moderate (rapid strep test/culture), ≥4 high (test, or consider empiric treatment). A decision aid, not a diagnosis.
