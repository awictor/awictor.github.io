About voltage dividers

A voltage divider is two resistors in series; the output tapped between them is Vout = Vin · R2 / (R1 + R2). It's everywhere — scaling a sensor signal into an ADC's range, setting reference voltages, and reading resistive sensors like thermistors and photoresistors. Equal resistors give half the input. To hit a target output, R2 = R1 · Vout / (Vin − Vout). Keep the divider current well above any load current, or the load will "sag" the output. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
