About

FNV-1a and djb2 are fast non-cryptographic hashes — great for hash tables, cache keys, bloom filters, and sharding, where speed and even distribution matter more than security. They are not collision-resistant and must never be used for passwords or integrity; use SHA-256 for that. Both run over the UTF-8 bytes of your input.
