About grade & slope

Percent grade is rise / run × 100 — a 10% grade rises 10 units over 100 horizontal units. The angle is arctan(rise/run), so grade and angle are not the same: a 100% grade is 45°, not vertical. The ratio form (like 1:12 for an ADA wheelchair ramp, or 1:20) is run per unit rise. Roads are usually signed in percent, roofs in ratio or pitch, and trails in degrees. The slope distance is the true along-the-slope length, √(rise² + run²). Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
