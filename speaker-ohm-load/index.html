How it works

  • Series adds impedances: total = R1 + R2 + … (wire + to −, chaining speakers).
  • Parallel lowers it: total = 1 ÷ (1/R1 + 1/R2 + …), always below the smallest speaker.
  • Amps have a minimum load — dropping below it (too many speakers in parallel) can overheat and damage the amp.
  • Four 8Ω speakers in parallel = 2Ω, which many home amps can't handle; wire them series-parallel instead.
