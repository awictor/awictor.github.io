About MAP

Mean arterial pressure is the average pressure driving blood into the tissues over one cardiac cycle. Because the heart spends about twice as long in diastole as in systole, it's weighted: MAP = (SBP + 2·DBP) / 3. A MAP of roughly 70–100 mmHg is a common target for adequate organ perfusion; sustained values below ~65 raise concern. Pulse pressure (SBP − DBP) reflects arterial stiffness and stroke volume — typically ~40 mmHg. This is an educational aid, not medical advice. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
