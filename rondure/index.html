How to play

Draw one single closed loop that never crosses or branches. It must obey every pearl:
  • White pearl: the loop passes straight through it, and must turn in at least one of the two cells immediately beside it.
  • Black pearl: the loop makes a right-angle turn on it, and must go straight through the next cell out along both arms of that turn.
Click or drag along the edges between cells to lay a segment; click a segment again to erase it. Use Check to surface rule breaks, Hint for one forced segment, and Undo/Redo to step through edits.
