How it works

  • A pipe is a cylinder: volume = π × radius² × length.
  • Use the inner diameter — nominal pipe sizes (like "2-inch") differ from the real bore.
  • Doubling the diameter quadruples the volume, since area grows with the square of radius.
  • Handy for sizing pumps, purge/flush volumes, aquarium plumbing, and winterizing.
