# Pipe Volume 🚰

Calculate the **water volume inside a pipe** from its inner diameter and length, in gallons and liters. Single offline HTML file, no dependencies, no data leaves your device.

## Features

- Cylinder volume: π × radius² × length.
- Gallons, liters, cubic feet, and gallons per foot.
- Dark mode, mobile friendly, works fully offline.

## Usage

Open `index.html` in any browser. Enter the pipe's inner diameter and length.

> Use the inner diameter — nominal pipe sizes differ from the real bore.
