Sizing the hole before you tap

To cut internal threads you drill a hole slightly smaller than the screw, then run a tap. The quick rule of thumb for metric threads is drill = major diameter − pitch — so M10×1.5 uses an 8.5 mm bit, M6×1 uses 5 mm. For a chosen thread engagement, the more precise size is major − (%/100 × 1.0825 × pitch); 75% is typical, lower for tough materials to reduce tapping torque. Pitch converts to threads per inch as 25.4 ÷ pitch. Always confirm against a chart for critical work. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
