How it works

  • Angle of view = 2 × arctan(sensor size ÷ (2 × focal length)). Computed separately for width, height, and diagonal.
  • Shorter focal length or a larger sensor gives a wider view; longer focal length narrows it.
  • That's why a 50 mm lens is "normal" on full frame but acts like a short telephoto on a cropped sensor.
  • Great for planning shots, choosing lenses, or matching coverage across camera bodies.
