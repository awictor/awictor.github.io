# Swim Pace Calculator

Calculate swim **pace per 100** (m or yd), **total time** for a distance, and **speed** from your distance and time. For swimmers and triathletes.

**[Open the tool →](https://awictor.github.io/swim-pace/)**

- Pace per 100 = time × 100 ÷ distance
- Per-100m and per-100yd pace, plus time per km
- Speed in m/s
- Dark mode, 100% offline, no dependencies, no tracking

## Example

1500 m in 25:00 (1500 s) is a 1:40 / 100 m pace at 1.0 m/s.
