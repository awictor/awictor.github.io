How it works

  • Each plant needs a square of open space equal to its spacing, so plants along a side = floor(side ÷ spacing).
  • Total = plants down the length × plants across the width — a simple grid layout.
  • Plants per square foot = (12 ÷ spacing)²: 12" spacing is 1 per sq ft, 6" is 4, 3" is 16.
  • Offset (triangular) planting can fit ~15% more; treat this grid count as a safe minimum.
