Kitchen equivalences (US)

These are US customary volume measures: 1 tablespoon = 3 teaspoons, 1 fluid ounce = 2 tablespoons, and 1 cup = 8 fl oz = 16 tablespoons = 48 teaspoons ≈ 236.6 ml. Converting through millilitres keeps everything exact, which is handy for scaling recipes or working from a metric cookbook. (US cups differ from metric 250 ml cups and UK measures — this tool uses US units.) Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
