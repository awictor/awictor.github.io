How it works

  • SWOLF (“swim golf”) = strokes per length + seconds per length. Like golf, a lower score is better.
  • It rewards efficiency: gliding further per stroke drops the count, and swimming faster drops the time — you can't just thrash to win.
  • Rough 25 m bands: under 30 excellent, 30–40 good, 40–50 average, over 50 developing. Longer pools shift the numbers, so compare like with like.
  • Track it over time in the same pool; a falling SWOLF at the same pace means real technique gains.
  • Watches compute it per length automatically; here you can average a whole set.
