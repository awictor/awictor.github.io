About the Friedewald equation

LDL = Total − HDL − Triglycerides ÷ 5 (mg/dL; divide TG by 2.19 for mmol/L). It's the standard estimate but becomes unreliable when triglycerides are high (≥ 400 mg/dL) — get a direct LDL measurement instead. Non-HDL (Total − HDL) captures all the atherogenic cholesterol and stays valid at high triglycerides. LDL targets: <100 optimal, 100–129 near-optimal, 130–159 borderline high, 160–189 high, ≥190 very high (mg/dL). Education only, not medical advice. Everything runs locally.
