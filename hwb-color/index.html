About HWB

HWB (Hue, Whiteness, Blackness) is a CSS Color 4 model — hwb(300 15% 17%). Start from a pure hue, then mix in white and black. It's intuitive for tints and shades: raise W to lighten, raise B to darken. When W + B ≥ 100%, the result is a gray. Widely supported in modern browsers.
