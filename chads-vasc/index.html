About

CHA₂DS₂-VASc estimates annual stroke/thromboembolism risk in non-valvular atrial fibrillation and guides oral anticoagulation. Points: CHF/LV dysfunction (1), hypertension (1), age ≥75 (2), diabetes (1), prior stroke/TIA/thromboembolism (2), vascular disease (1), age 65–74 (1), female sex (1). This tool is for education and is not a substitute for clinical judgment.
