How it works

  • Each post carries a tributary area — roughly (beam span between posts) × (half the joist span) — of the deck above it.
  • Multiply by the design load (40 psf live + 10 psf dead = 50 psf residential) to get the pounds pressing on that footing.
  • Divide by the soil's bearing capacity (IRC Table R401.4.1) to get the footing's minimum bearing area — weak clay needs a much wider footing than bedrock.
  • We convert that area to the diameter of a round footing. Round up to the next available tube size (a 60 sq ft deck on clay lands near a 20" footing).
  • Always confirm frost depth, local soil tests, and your building department's requirements.
