About Adler-32

Adler-32 is a checksum invented by Mark Adler for zlib. It keeps two running sums modulo 65521 (the largest prime below 2¹⁶): A is 1 plus the sum of all bytes, and B is the sum of all the intermediate A values; the result is (B << 16) | A. It's faster to compute than a CRC-32 but weaker at catching errors, especially in short messages. The classic reference value is Adler-32("Wikipedia") = 0x11E60398.
