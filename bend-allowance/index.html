How it works

  • When you bend sheet metal, the outside stretches and the inside compresses. Somewhere between sits the neutral axis, which keeps its length.
  • The K-factor locates that axis as a fraction of thickness (typically 0.30–0.50). Bend allowance is the arc length of the neutral axis through the bend.
  • BA = angle(rad) × (radius + K × thickness). The flat blank = the flange lengths minus the bend deduction per bend.
  • Cut your blank to this flat length and the part comes out to size after forming — guess and every bend drifts.
  • K-factor depends on material, tooling, and method; dial it in with a test bend for precision work.
