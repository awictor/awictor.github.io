What is APCA and why not the WCAG 2 ratio?

APCA (Accessible Perceptual Contrast Algorithm) is the contrast method proposed for WCAG 3. Unlike the WCAG 2 luminance ratio (a single 1–21 number), APCA returns a signed Lc value (−108…+106) that accounts for text polarity (dark-on-light vs light-on-dark), soft black clamping, and human lightness perception, so it maps far better to real readability. Positive Lc = dark text on lighter background; negative = light text on darker background.
