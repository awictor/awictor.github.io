About these statistics

The mean is the average; the median is the middle value (robust to outliers); the mode is the most frequent. Variance measures spread — the sample version divides by n−1 (Bessel's correction, for a sample of a larger population), the population version by n. Standard deviation is its square root, in the same units as the data. Quartiles split the sorted data into four parts (using linear interpolation), and the interquartile range Q3−Q1 spans the middle half. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
