How it works

  • Salt (lbs) = gallons × (target − current) ppm × 8.34 ÷ 1,000,000 — since water weighs 8.34 lb/gal.
  • Most saltwater chlorine generators target around 3,000–3,400 ppm; check your unit's ideal range.
  • Add salt in stages, run the pump to circulate, and re-test before adding more — you can't easily remove excess.
  • If you're already at or above target, the tool shows zero; you'd dilute with fresh water instead.
