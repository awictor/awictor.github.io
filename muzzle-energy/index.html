The numbers

Muzzle energy is kinetic energy in the shooter's units: E = grains × fps² ÷ 450240 foot-pounds — energy rises with the square of velocity but only linearly with bullet weight. Power factor (grains × fps ÷ 1000) is how action-shooting sports score recoil; USPSA "major" is 165. The Taylor KO value (grains × fps × diameter ÷ 7000) is a rough big-game stopping index. This is a sporting and reloading reference; velocities come straight off a chronograph. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
