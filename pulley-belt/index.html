How it works

For an open belt over two pulleys, the length is 2C + (π/2)(D+d) + (D−d)² ÷ 4C, where C is the center distance and D, d the pulley diameters. The drive ratio is driven ÷ driver: a 3″ driver turning a 6″ driven gives 2:1, so the output turns at half the input speed. Belt surface speed is π × driver diameter × input RPM, shown here in feet per minute. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
