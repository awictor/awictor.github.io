The quadratic formula

A quadratic ax² + bx + c = 0 is solved by x = (−b ± √(b²−4ac)) / 2a. The discriminant b²−4ac decides the roots: positive → two real roots, zero → one repeated root, negative → two complex conjugate roots. The parabola's vertex sits at x = −b/2a. Leading coefficient a must be non-zero, or it isn't quadratic. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
