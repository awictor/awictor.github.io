About lerp & remap

Linear interpolation blends between two values: lerp(a, b, t) = a + (b − a)·t, so t = 0 gives a, t = 1 gives b, t = 0.5 gives the midpoint. Inverse lerp asks "what t produces this value?" — (v − a)/(b − a). Remap combines them to rescale a number from an input range into an output range (e.g. 0–100 °C → 32–212 °F). Values of t outside [0, 1] extrapolate beyond the endpoints; use the clamped form to stay within bounds. Bread and butter for graphics, game dev, animation curves, and normalizing data. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
