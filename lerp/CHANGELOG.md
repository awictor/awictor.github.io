# Changelog

## v0.1.0 — 2026-09-11

Initial release.

- lerp, inverseLerp, remap, remapClamped, and clamp
- Extrapolation outside [0,1] and a clamped remap variant
- Degenerate-range guard (no divide-by-zero)
- Dark mode with persistence, fully offline, no dependencies
- 10 self-tests including the °C→°F remap and lerp/inverse round trip
