HexDump

xxd-style hex dump of text


    
Text is encoded as UTF-8 bytes, so multi-byte characters (emoji, accents) expand to several bytes.
      Columns are the byte offset (hex), the raw bytes, and the printable-ASCII gutter (. for
      non-printable). Runs 100% locally.
