# Changelog

## v0.1.0 — 2026-09-11

Initial release.

- Numerical first derivative (central difference) and second derivative of f(x) at a point
- Tangent-line equation at the point
- Embedded recursive-descent parser for f(x): powers, functions, constants (no eval)
- Dark mode with persistence, fully offline, no dependencies
- 10 self-tests including x², x³, sin, exp, and the tangent to x² at 3
