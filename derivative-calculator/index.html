About numerical derivatives

The derivative f′(x) is the instantaneous slope of a function. This tool estimates it with the central difference [f(x+h) − f(x−h)] / 2h, which is accurate to O(h²), and the second derivative with [f(x+h) − 2f(x) + f(x−h)] / h². The tangent line at x is y = f(x) + f′(x)·(X − x). Type any function of x — powers, sin/cos/exp/ln/sqrt, constants pi, e — parsed by a real recursive-descent parser, no eval(). Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
