# Changelog

## 0.1.0
- First release. Business days calculator.
- Add/subtract working days from a date (forward & backward); count working days between two dates.
- Skips weekends + a custom holiday list; UTC math (no timezone surprises).
- Shows resulting weekday, copy result, dark mode, localStorage memory.
- Headless test suite (10 checks, fixed 2024 vectors) + CI.
