How it works

  • Modern birthstones follow the list standardized by the U.S. jewelry industry in 1912, updated a few times since.
  • Several months have more than one accepted stone — the alternate is shown where there is one.
  • Birth flowers are the traditional English/American monthly blooms.
  • Great for picking jewelry, engraving, or a thoughtful birthday gift.
