How it works

  • In 501 you must finish exactly on a double (or the bullseye, which is the double of 25).
  • The tool searches singles, doubles and trebles to reach your score in as few darts as possible, always landing the last dart on a double.
  • The highest possible three-dart checkout is 170 — T20, T20, Bull.
  • Some scores are impossible with three darts (the “bogey” numbers 169, 168, 166, 165, 163, 162, 159, and 1) — leave yourself a better number.
  • There are often several finishes; this favours a clean route, but pick the double you shoot best.
