How it works

  • Every load has a moment = weight × arm (its distance from the datum). Sum the moments, divide by total weight, and you have the center of gravity.
  • The CG must sit between the forward and aft limits from your POH, and total weight must stay at or under max gross.
  • Too far forward and the nose is heavy on the elevator; too far aft and the aircraft can be dangerously unstable.
  • Enter each station's weight and arm for your specific aircraft — arms come from the type's loading chart.
  • This is a planning aid; always use the official weight-and-balance data and current empty-weight figures for your tail number.
