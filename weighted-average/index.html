About weighted averages

A weighted average multiplies each value by its importance (weight), sums those products, and divides by the total weight: x̄ = Σ(vᵢ·wᵢ) / Σwᵢ. When all weights are equal it reduces to the plain mean. Weights don't need to add up to 1 — only their ratios matter, so [1, 3] and [10, 30] give the same result. Used for grade calculations (exam worth 50%, homework 20%…), portfolio returns, survey scores, and blended interest rates. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
