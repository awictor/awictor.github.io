About least-squares regression

Linear regression finds the straight line y = mx + b that minimizes the total squared vertical distance to your points. The slope is m = Σ(x−x̄)(y−ȳ) / Σ(x−x̄)² and the intercept is b = ȳ − m·x̄. The Pearson correlation r (from −1 to +1) measures how tightly the points cluster around the line, and R² = r² is the fraction of variance the line explains — 1.0 is a perfect fit.
