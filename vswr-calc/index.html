Matching antennas and feedlines

When a load isn't perfectly matched to the line, some power reflects back. The reflection coefficient |Γ| = (VSWR − 1)/(VSWR + 1) measures how much. VSWR of 1:1 is perfect; 2:1 reflects about 11% of the power. Return loss = −20·log₁₀|Γ| (higher dB is better), and mismatch loss = −10·log₁₀(1 − |Γ|²) is the power actually lost to the reflection. Most ham and Wi-Fi gear wants VSWR under about 2:1. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
