About

Type a mathematical expression and get the answer, evaluated by a real recursive-descent parser — not JavaScript's eval(), so it's safe. It honours precedence (2 + 3 × 4 = 14), right-associative powers (2^3^2 = 512), unary minus (-2^2 = -4), parentheses, and postfix factorial (5! = 120). Functions include sin cos tan asin acos atan sqrt cbrt ln log log2 exp abs floor ceil round (trig in radians), and constants pi, e, tau. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
