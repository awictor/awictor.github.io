Future value

What a dollar is worth over time

A lump sum grows by compounding: FV = PV × (1 + r)ⁿ. Rearranged, present value discounts a future amount back to today, and you can also solve for the rate that turns one into the other or the number of periods it takes. The rate and period must use the same unit — annual rate with years, monthly rate with months. This handles a single lump sum; for recurring deposits use an annuity or amortization tool.
