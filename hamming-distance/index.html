What is Hamming distance?

The Hamming distance between two strings of equal length is the number of positions at which the corresponding symbols differ — the minimum number of substitutions to turn one into the other. For binary numbers it's the count of differing bits, which equals the population count of their XOR. It underpins error-detecting and error-correcting codes, and measures mutations between DNA sequences of equal length. (Strings must be the same length; for differing lengths you'd use edit distance instead.) Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
