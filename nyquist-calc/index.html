About the Nyquist theorem

The Nyquist-Shannon theorem says a signal can be perfectly reconstructed only if the sample rate is more than twice the highest frequency present. The Nyquist frequency is half the sample rate — the highest frequency you can represent. Anything above it doesn't vanish; it aliases down to a false lower frequency, folding around the Nyquist limit. That's why CD audio uses 44.1 kHz: comfortably above 2× the ~20 kHz limit of human hearing. Uncompressed PCM data rate is simply sample rate × bit depth × channels.
