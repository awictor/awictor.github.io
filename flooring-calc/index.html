Don't forget the waste factor

Flooring is sold by the box, each covering a fixed area. Measure the room, then add a waste factor for offcuts, mistakes, and future repairs: about 10% for a simple rectangular room, 15% for diagonal layouts or lots of corners, and 20% for herringbone. Divide the padded area by the box coverage and round up — you can't buy a partial box. Buy from the same lot so colors match. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
