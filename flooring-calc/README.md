# Flooring Calculator

Calculate how many **boxes of flooring** you need from room dimensions, box coverage, and a waste factor.

**[Open the tool →](https://awictor.github.io/flooring-calc/)**

- Floor area, area with waste, and boxes to buy (rounded up)
- Waste factor for offcuts and repairs (10–20%)
- For laminate, vinyl, and hardwood
- Dark mode, 100% offline, no dependencies, no tracking

## Example

A 15×12 ft room (180 ft²) at 10% waste needs 198 ft² → 10 boxes of 20 ft².
