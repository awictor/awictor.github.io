Uuid7

UUID v7 generator & inspector


    
UUIDv7 puts a millisecond timestamp in the high bits, so IDs sort by creation time —
      ideal as database primary keys (better index locality than random v4). Generation is local & cryptographically
      random. Runs 100% locally.
