Getting the dose right

Chlorine level is measured in parts per million: 1 ppm is 1 mg per litre, so raising a pool by X ppm needs volume(L) × X ÷ 1000 grams of pure chlorine. But products aren't pure — cal-hypo is ~65% available chlorine, dichlor ~56%, so divide by the available-chlorine fraction to get the actual amount to add. Add chemicals to water (never the reverse), with the pump running, and re-test before swimming. Typical free-chlorine target is 1–3 ppm; shock to ~10. Estimates only — follow the product label.
