Why fullness matters

Curtains only look right when they're wider than the window so they gather into folds instead of hanging flat. That extra is the fullness ratio: measure the rod (not just the glass), then multiply — fabric width = rod width × fullness. A 2× ratio is the everyday standard; sheers can go 1.5× and a rich, drapey look wants 2.5–3×. Divide the needed width by a single panel's flat width and round up to whole panels (they usually come in pairs). Buy the total across all panels, and remember the finished, gathered curtain will look about half as wide as the fabric you started with. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
