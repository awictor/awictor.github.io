# Changelog

## v0.1.0 — 2026-09-11

Initial release.

- Linear (ΔL = α·L₀·ΔT), area (2α), and volume (3α) thermal expansion
- New-length output and material presets (steel, aluminum, copper, glass, concrete)
- Contraction on cooling (negative ΔT)
- Dark mode with persistence, fully offline, no dependencies
- 10 self-tests including the steel benchmark and the 2α/3α relationships
