About the thin lens equation

The thin lens equation relates focal length f, object distance dₒ, and image distance dᵢ: 1/f = 1/dₒ + 1/dᵢ. Magnification is m = −dᵢ/dₒ: negative means inverted, |m| > 1 means enlarged. A positive dᵢ is a real image (on the far side, can be projected); a negative dᵢ is a virtual image (same side, as in a magnifying glass). A converging lens has f > 0; diverging f < 0. Sign convention: distances measured with light travelling left-to-right. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
