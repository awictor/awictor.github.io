The Sabine formula

Reverberation time — how long sound takes to decay 60 dB — is RT60 = 0.161 × V ÷ A, with volume V in m³ and total absorption A in sabins (surface area × average absorption coefficient). Bigger rooms ring longer; more soft, absorptive material shortens it. Studios aim for roughly 0.3–0.5 s, living rooms 0.4–0.6 s, concert halls 1.5–2.5 s. The last figure is the extra absorption you'd need to add to reach your target. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
