About escape velocity

Escape velocity is the speed needed to break free of a body's gravity with no further propulsion: v = √(2GM/r), from a distance r of a mass M. It doesn't depend on the escaping object's own mass. Earth's is about 11.2 km/s; the Moon's just 2.4 km/s. The circular orbital velocity at the same radius is exactly 1/√2 of it (Earth low orbit ≈ 7.9 km/s) — which is why reaching orbit takes less than escaping entirely.
