# Centripetal Force Calculator

Compute the **centripetal force** and **acceleration** of an object in circular motion from its mass, speed, and radius, plus the **angular velocity**, **period**, and equivalent **g-force**.

**[Open the tool →](https://awictor.github.io/centripetal/)**

- `F = mv²/r = mω²r`, `a = v²/r`
- Angular velocity `ω = v/r` and period `T = 2πr/v`
- Acceleration expressed in g's
- Dark mode with persistence
- 100% offline, no dependencies, no tracking

## Example

A 2 kg mass at 10 m/s on a 5 m radius needs 40 N of centripetal force (20 m/s², ≈ 2 g), going around once every π seconds.
