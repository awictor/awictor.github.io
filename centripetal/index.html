About centripetal force

Any object moving in a circle is constantly accelerating toward the center — even at constant speed — because its direction keeps changing. The inward centripetal force required is F = mv²/r = mω²r, and the centripetal acceleration is a = v²/r. Double the speed and the force quadruples; double the radius and (at the same speed) it halves. This governs everything from a car rounding a bend to a satellite in orbit to a centrifuge. Angular velocity ω = v/r and period T = 2πr/v describe how fast it goes around. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
