How it works

  • A foot-candle is one lumen per square foot, so lumens needed = area × target foot-candles.
  • Divide by the lumens each bulb or fixture puts out, then round up for the count.
  • Foot-candles convert to lux by ×10.76 (lux is lumens per square meter).
  • These are general targets — layer ambient, task, and accent lighting rather than relying on one big source.
