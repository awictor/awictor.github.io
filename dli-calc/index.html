How it works

  • PPFD is the instantaneous rate of usable light hitting the canopy (µmol of photons per m² per second).
  • DLI totals that over the day: DLI = PPFD × hours × 3600 ÷ 1,000,000 mol/m²/day.
  • Rough targets: leafy greens/herbs 12–17, most houseplants 10–20, fruiting crops (tomato, pepper) 20–30, cannabis flower 30–45.
  • Too little DLI means leggy, weak growth; too much wastes energy and can bleach leaves. Trade intensity against hours to hit your target.
  • Measure PPFD with a quantum meter at canopy height; light falls off fast with distance from the fixture.
