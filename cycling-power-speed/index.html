How it works

The cycling power model
  • Power at the wheel overcomes three forces: rolling resistance (Crr·m·g·cosθ), gravity on a grade (m·g·sinθ), and aerodynamic drag (½·ρ·CdA·v²).
  • Total power = (Froll + Fgravity)·v + ½·ρ·CdA·v³, divided by drivetrain efficiency.
  • CdA is your drag area (~0.3 on the hoods, ~0.25 in the drops); Crr is tire rolling resistance (~0.004–0.006 on road).
