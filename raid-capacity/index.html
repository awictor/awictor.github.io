How it works

RAID levels & the math
  • RAID 0 stripes across all disks: usable = disks × size, but zero redundancy — one failure loses everything.
  • RAID 1 mirrors one disk's data across all members: usable = size, tolerates disks − 1 failures.
  • RAID 5 uses one disk's worth of parity: usable = (disks − 1) × size, survives 1 failure. Needs ≥3 disks.
  • RAID 6 uses two disks' worth of parity: usable = (disks − 2) × size, survives 2 failures. Needs ≥4 disks.
  • RAID 10 mirrors pairs then stripes: usable = disks ÷ 2 × size, survives ≥1 (up to one per mirror). Needs an even count ≥4.
  • Efficiency = usable ÷ raw. Everything is computed live in your browser — nothing leaves this page.
