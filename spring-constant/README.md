# Hooke's Law Calculator

Solve **Hooke's law** `F = kx` for spring constant, force, or displacement, and get the **elastic potential energy** `E = ½kx²`. For physics students and mechanical/spring design.

**[Open the tool →](https://awictor.github.io/spring-constant/)**

- Solve for force, spring constant, or displacement
- Elastic potential energy for every case
- Linear force, quadratic energy — verified in tests
- Dark mode with persistence
- 100% offline, no dependencies, no tracking

## Example

A 200 N/m spring stretched 0.1 m exerts 20 N and stores 1 J of energy.
