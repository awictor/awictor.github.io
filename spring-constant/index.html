About Hooke's law

Hooke's law says the force a spring exerts is proportional to how far it's stretched or compressed: F = kx, where k is the spring constant (stiffness, in N/m) and x the displacement from rest. Stretching a spring stores elastic potential energy E = ½kx², which it returns when released — the principle behind springs, trampolines, car suspensions, and force gauges.
