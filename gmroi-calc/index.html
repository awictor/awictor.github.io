About GMROI

GMROI (gross margin return on inventory investment) answers "how many dollars of gross profit do I earn for each dollar tied up in inventory?" It's gross margin $ ÷ average inventory cost. A GMROI above 1.0 means the inventory earns back more than it costs; retailers often target 3.0+. It rewards both healthy margins and fast turnover, making it a sharper stocking signal than margin alone — useful for retail and Amazon/FBA assortment decisions.
