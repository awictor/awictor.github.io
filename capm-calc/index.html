About the CAPM

The Capital Asset Pricing Model gives the return an investor should expect for bearing a stock's systematic risk: E(R) = Rf + β·(Rm − Rf). Rf is the risk-free rate, Rm the expected market return, and β (beta) measures how much the asset moves with the market — β = 1 matches the market, β > 1 is more volatile, β < 1 less. The term (Rm − Rf) is the market risk premium. Jensen's alpha is the excess of an asset's actual return over its CAPM expectation — positive alpha means it beat the model.
