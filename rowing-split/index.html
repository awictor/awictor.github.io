The Concept2 formula

On a Concept2 erg, power relates to pace (seconds per metre) as watts = 2.80 ÷ pace³. So your split falls fast as power rises — pulling twice the watts only drops your 500m split by about 20%. Your 500m split for any piece is time × 500 ÷ distance. The monitor's calorie count follows cal/hr = 4 × watts × 0.8604 + 300, which is why easy paces still burn a surprising number of calories.
