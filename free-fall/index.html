Free Fall Calculator

Fall time, drop height & impact speed under gravity

How it works

The kinematics (no air resistance)
  • An object dropped from rest falls h = ½ g t², so the fall time is t = √(2h ÷ g).
  • Its speed at impact is v = √(2 g h) = g t.
  • Change the gravity to see how the same drop plays out on the Moon, Mars, or Jupiter.
  • This ignores air resistance, so real terminal-velocity falls (skydivers, feathers) differ. Everything runs in your browser.
