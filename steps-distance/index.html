From step count to real numbers

Distance is just steps × stride length. Stride is roughly 0.415 × height for walking, which this estimates from your height — measure yours by walking 10 steps and dividing for accuracy. Calories burned walking are about 0.57 kcal per kg per km, so a 70 kg person covering 7 km burns ~280 kcal. The famous "10,000 steps" lands near 7 km for average height. Estimates only.
