pack-years
Pack-years = (cigarettes per day ÷ 20) × years smoked, summed over periods. The USPSTF recommends lung-cancer screening consideration at ≥ 20 pack-years (with age/quit criteria). Informational only — not medical advice. 100% offline. Part of the Toolkit. Source.
