About projectile motion

For a projectile launched from ground level at speed v and angle θ (ignoring air resistance), the horizontal and vertical motions are independent. That gives range R = v²·sin(2θ)/g, max height H = v²·sin²θ/(2g), and time of flight T = 2v·sinθ/g. Range is greatest at 45°, and complementary angles (say 30° and 60°) travel the same distance. Change g to model other worlds — the Moon's 1.62 m/s² sends the same throw about six times farther. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
