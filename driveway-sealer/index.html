How to size sealer

Asphalt sealer is applied by coverage rate — a gallon covers roughly 70–80 sq ft per coat on older, porous blacktop (smooth new asphalt stretches further). So gallons needed is area × coats ÷ coverage, and most driveways get two coats. Sealer is sold in 5-gallon pails, so divide and round up. Measure length × width for the paved area, seal when it's dry and above ~50 °F for a day or two, and don't over-apply — a coat that's too thick cracks and peels. Buy a little extra for touch-ups around edges and cracks. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
