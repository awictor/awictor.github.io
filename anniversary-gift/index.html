How it works

  • Anniversary gifts follow a long-standing list: traditional (Victorian origin) and modern (a 20th-century update).
  • Every year is named through 15, then the big milestones land on 20, 25, 30, and each five years after.
  • Year 25 is Silver, 50 is Gold, 60 is Diamond — the classics everyone knows.
  • Between named years, gift by the next milestone or the couple's own taste — the list is a tradition, not a rule.
