# Desk Height 🪑

Find ergonomic desk heights for **sitting and standing**, plus monitor top height, from your body height. Single offline HTML file, no dependencies, no data leaves your device.

## Use it

Open `index.html` in any browser, or visit the [live version](https://awictor.github.io/standing-desk-height/).

Enter your height in inches. You get the standing desk height, seated desk height, and monitor top height.

## How it works

- Set the work surface at elbow height — forearms roughly parallel to the floor with shoulders relaxed.
- Standing elbow height is about 62% of your stature; a seated desk sits near 40% from the floor.
- The top of the monitor should be at or just below eye level — around 90% of your height.
- These are starting points — fine-tune so wrists stay neutral and your gaze drops slightly to screen center.
