What is EOQ?

The economic order quantity is the order size that minimizes total inventory cost — the point where the cost of ordering (more orders = more fixed cost) exactly balances the cost of holding (bigger orders = more stock sitting on the shelf). The formula is EOQ = √(2 · D · S / H), where D is annual demand, S is the cost per order, and H is the holding cost per unit per year. The reorder point tells you the stock level at which to place the next order given your lead time. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
