# Tank Volume Calculator

Compute the volume of **vertical/horizontal cylindrical** and **rectangular** tanks — including **partial-fill** volume via the circular-segment formula — in cubic metres, litres, and US gallons.

**[Open the tool →](https://awictor.github.io/tank-volume/)**

- Full and partial fill for each shape
- Horizontal cylinder uses the exact circular-segment area
- Litres and US gallons output
- Dark mode, 100% offline, no dependencies, no tracking

## Example

A 1 m-radius, 10 m-long horizontal cylinder filled to 1 m (half) holds 5π ≈ 15.7 m³ (~15,708 L).
