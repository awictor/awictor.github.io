About tank volume

A full cylinder holds π·r²·h; a rectangular tank holds l·w·h. For a vertical cylinder, a partial fill is just the same formula with the fill height. A horizontal cylinder is trickier — the liquid forms a circular segment, with cross-section r²·arccos((r−h)/r) − (r−h)·√(2rh−h²) for fill depth h, times the length. Multiply cubic metres by 1000 for litres or by 264.17 for US gallons. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
