Vernier — sine & atan by shifts and adds

CORDIC rotates a vector by ±arctan(2⁻ⁱ) at each step — a rotation that on real silicon is just a bit-shift and an add, no multiplier. Drag the target; watch the vector ratchet in like a vernier scale. When it settles, (x,y) is (cos θ, sin θ) — scaled by the fixed gain K.
