How it works

Current flows out and back, so the drop is 2 × length × current × R, where R is the wire's resistance per foot (from its AWG size). The industry rule of thumb keeps drop under 3% for branch circuits (5% total including feeders); more than that dims lights and stresses motors. Cut voltage drop by using thicker wire (a lower AWG number), shortening the run, or raising system voltage. Values are for copper at room temperature.
