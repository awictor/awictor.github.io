Relative vs. absolute humidity

Relative humidity is a percentage of what the air could hold at that temperature, so 50% at 30 °C is far more actual water than 50% at 5 °C. Absolute humidity reports the real mass of water vapor per cubic metre of air. It's what matters for humidors, greenhouses, drying, and museum conservation. The formula uses the saturation vapor pressure (Magnus equation) scaled by relative humidity and air temperature. Warm air holds dramatically more moisture.
