Wickfall

Your lantern is your life. Every step burns it down.

You descend a lightless dungeon with a single lantern. Its glow is your sight — and its oil drains every turn. Linger and the dark closes in tile by tile; things that live in the black grow bold. Move fast and you blunder into ambush. Find oil. Go deep. Die well.

Keyboard: arrows / hjkl / numpad move. ? for full controls. Runs autosave — refresh to resume.

How to Play

The Lantern Economy

The lit circle around you is everything you can see — and it shrinks as your oil drains, roughly one unit per turn. At zero oil the wick gutters through a few dimmer ember turns, then blackout: radius 1, and you are nearly blind. Pick up oil flasks to refill.

Monsters standing in darkness gain a lurk bonus (harder to hit, hit harder) and track you by the noise you make moving. Keep them in your light to expose them.

Active light control

Dim (z) halves oil burn and cuts your noise for stealth, at the cost of a smaller circle. Flare (f) spends oil to blast a bright pulse that briefly blinds and exposes adjacent lurkers.
