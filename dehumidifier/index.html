How dehumidifier sizing works

Dehumidifiers are rated by how many pints of water they pull from the air in 24 hours. The classic AHAM chart sizes a unit from two things: the square footage of the space and how damp it feels. A space starts at a base removal for the first 500 sq ft, then adds a fixed amount for every additional 500 sq ft — larger and wetter spaces need more capacity. This tool uses base + step of 10 + 4 (moderate), 12 + 5 (very damp), 14 + 6 (wet) and 16 + 7 (extremely wet) pints per 500 sq ft. The recommended unit rounds up to the nearest common size (20, 30, 35, 50, 70 pint). Newer DOE-labeled units report lower pint numbers for the same real capacity, so match by AHAM rating when you can. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
