# Pressure Calculator

Compute **pressure from force and area** (`P = F/A`), or solve for force or area, with the result shown in pascals, kPa, psi, bar, and atmospheres.

**[Open the tool →](https://awictor.github.io/pressure-calc/)**

- Solve for P, F, or A
- Auto-conversion to kPa/psi/bar/atm
- 1 atm = 101,325 Pa, 1 bar = 100,000 Pa, 1 psi ≈ 6,894.76 Pa
- Dark mode, 100% offline, no dependencies, no tracking

## Example

100 N on 2 m² → **50 Pa**. Same force on 1 m² → 100 Pa.
