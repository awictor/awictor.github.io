About pressure

Pressure is force spread over an area: P = F / A, measured in pascals (1 Pa = 1 N/m²). The same force over a smaller area gives more pressure — which is why a sharp knife or a stiletto heel presses hard. Rearrange for F = P·A or A = F/P. Handy conversions: 1 atm = 101,325 Pa, 1 bar = 100,000 Pa, 1 psi ≈ 6,894.76 Pa. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
