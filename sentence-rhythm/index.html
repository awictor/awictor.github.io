Why rhythm matters

Gary Provost's famous passage says it best: sentences of the same length lull the reader to sleep. Vary short, medium and long sentences and the prose gains music. The variety score is the coefficient of variation (standard deviation ÷ mean) of your sentence lengths — higher means more varied. Aim for a healthy mix; a wall of same-length sentences reads flat.
