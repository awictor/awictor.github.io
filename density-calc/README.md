# Density Calculator

Solve **density** `ρ = m / V` for density, mass, or volume — with material presets and g/cm³ output. For physics, chemistry, and materials work.

**[Open the tool →](https://awictor.github.io/density-calc/)**

- Solve for ρ, m, or V
- Presets: water, aluminum, iron, gold, air
- kg/m³ and g/cm³
- Dark mode, 100% offline, no dependencies, no tracking

## Example

2 kg in 0.002 m³ → **1000 kg/m³** (1 g/cm³) — water.
