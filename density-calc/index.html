About density

Density is mass per unit volume: ρ = m / V. In SI it's kilograms per cubic metre (kg/m³); divide by 1000 for g/cm³ (or equivalently g/mL), so water's 1000 kg/m³ is 1 g/cm³. Rearrange to find any variable: m = ρ·V, V = m/ρ. Density decides whether things float (less dense than the fluid) or sink, and identifies materials — gold's 19.3 g/cm³ is why it feels so heavy.
