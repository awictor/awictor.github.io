About contribution margin

Contribution margin is what each sale contributes toward fixed costs and profit after covering its own variable cost: price − variable cost. The CM ratio is that as a fraction of price. Once contribution margin covers total fixed costs you break even; beyond that it's profit. The units for a profit target = (fixed costs + target profit) ÷ CM per unit — with a target of 0 that's the break-even quantity. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
