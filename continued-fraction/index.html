About continued fractions

Any number can be written as a continued fraction: a₀ + 1/(a₁ + 1/(a₂ + …)), written compactly as [a₀; a₁, a₂, …]. Truncating it early gives convergents — the best possible rational approximations for their size. For π you get 3, then 22/7, then 333/106, then the famously accurate 355/113 (correct to six decimals). Rational numbers terminate; quadratic irrationals like √2 repeat; and e follows the elegant pattern [2; 1, 2, 1, 1, 4, 1, 1, 6, …]. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
