How it works

  • Uncompressed audio stores every sample raw, so its size is simply sample rate × bit depth × channels × time.
  • CD-quality stereo (44.1 kHz, 16-bit) runs about 10 MB per minute — a 3.5-minute song is around 35 MB as a WAV.
  • Doubling the sample rate, bit depth, or channel count doubles the file. Hi-res 96 kHz/24-bit is roughly triple CD.
  • Bitrate = sample rate × bit depth × channels. CD audio is 1,411 kbps — compare that to a 320 kbps MP3.
  • This is the raw PCM size; lossy codecs (MP3, AAC, Opus) are far smaller, lossless (FLAC, ALAC) roughly half.
