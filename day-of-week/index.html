About Zeller's congruence

This finds the weekday of any Gregorian date using Zeller's congruence — a formula, not a lookup table or a Date object, so it works for any year without overflow. It treats January and February as months 13 and 14 of the previous year, then computes h = (q + ⌊13(m+1)/5⌋ + K + ⌊K/4⌋ + ⌊J/4⌋ + 5J) mod 7, where q is the day, K the year-of-century, and J the century. A year is a leap year if divisible by 4, except centuries not divisible by 400 (so 2000 was, 1900 wasn't). Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
