About the Wells DVT rule

The Wells score estimates the pre-test probability of deep vein thrombosis. Each clinical feature adds a point; an alternative diagnosis at least as likely subtracts two. Three-tier: ≤0 low, 1–2 moderate, ≥3 high. Two-tier (modified): ≥2 "DVT likely", <2 "DVT unlikely" — commonly paired with a D-dimer to rule out DVT in the unlikely group. A decision aid, not a diagnosis; use clinical judgment and your local pathway. Everything runs locally.
