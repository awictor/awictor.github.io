How hat sizes work

Measure around your head just above the ears and eyebrows. The US fitted size is the circumference in inches divided by π (a hat is a circle, and this is its diameter) — a 22 in head is about a 7. Fitted hats come in eighths, so the exact value is rounded to the nearest 1/8. The EU size is simply the circumference in centimetres. So 57 cm ≈ EU 57 ≈ US 7.
