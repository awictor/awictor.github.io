How it works

Astronomical distance units
  • Light travels 299,792.458 km/s. A light-year is the distance it covers in one Julian year — about 9.46 trillion km.
  • A parsec is ≈ 3.2616 light-years; an astronomical unit (AU) is the average Earth–Sun distance, 149,597,870.7 km.
  • Everything converts through kilometers, and the light-travel time is just distance ÷ the speed of light.
  • Example: Proxima Centauri is 4.246 light-years away — its light takes 4.246 years to reach us. Everything runs in your browser.
