Pouring round columns

A Sonotube or round form makes a cylinder, so the concrete for one column is π · (diameter/2)² · height, divided by 1728 to convert cubic inches to cubic feet. Multiply by the number of columns for a deck or fence with several piers. For big pours, cubic yards (cu ft ÷ 27) is how ready-mix is ordered; for a bag or two, an 80 lb bag yields about 0.60 cu ft and a 60 lb bag about 0.45. Round bags up and mix a little extra so you can top off without a cold joint. Dig below the frost line and set anchor bolts or a bracket while the concrete is still wet.
