How it works

  • Roasting at 325 °F: about 13 minutes per pound unstuffed, 15 minutes per pound stuffed.
  • Thaw in the fridge at roughly 1 day per 4 pounds — a 16 lb bird needs about 4 days.
  • Plan on about 1 pound per guest for a whole bird (that leaves some for leftovers).
  • Always cook to 165 °F in the thickest part of the thigh — time is only a guide. Let it rest 20–30 min before carving.
