About

MME converts different opioids to a common morphine-equivalent scale to gauge overdose risk. Uses CDC oral conversion factors (methadone is dose-dependent). CDC guidance: reassess at ≥50 MME/day and avoid or carefully justify ≥90 MME/day. Factors are for oral forms; transdermal fentanyl and buccal products differ. Educational only — not a dosing tool or medical advice.
