About Egyptian fractions

Ancient Egyptians wrote every fraction as a sum of distinct unit fractions — fractions with numerator 1, like 1/2 + 1/3. This tool uses the greedy (Fibonacci-Sylvester) algorithm: repeatedly subtract the largest unit fraction that fits, 1/⌈den/num⌉, until nothing remains. The result is always finite and every denominator is different. For example 6/7 = 1/2 + 1/3 + 1/42. All arithmetic here is exact integer math, so there's no rounding — though greedy expansions can grow large denominators quickly. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
