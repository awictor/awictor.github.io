Where the bill comes from

LLM APIs bill per token, usually with output priced higher than input (often 3–5×). Cost per call is (input ÷ 1M × input price) + (output ÷ 1M × output price). A 2,000-in / 500-out call at $3/$15 per million costs $0.0135 — trivial once, but at 1,000 calls a day that's ~$405 a month. As a rough size guide, 1 token ≈ 0.75 words (¾ of a word). Watch output length and cache repeated prompts to cut spend. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
