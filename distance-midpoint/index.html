How it works

The two-point formulas
  • Distance (straight line): √((x₂−x₁)² + (y₂−y₁)²) — the Pythagorean theorem.
  • Midpoint: ((x₁+x₂)/2, (y₁+y₂)/2) — the average of the coordinates.
  • Slope: (y₂−y₁) / (x₂−x₁), undefined for a vertical line.
  • Manhattan distance: |x₂−x₁| + |y₂−y₁| — travel along a grid, not diagonally.
