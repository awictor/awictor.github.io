How it works

  • Ski poles: flip a pole upside down and grip under the basket — your elbow should make a right angle. That works out to about height × 0.70, rounded to the nearest 5 cm.
  • Trekking poles: set them so your elbow is at 90° on flat ground — roughly height × 0.68.
  • Uphill: shorten poles ~5–10 cm for a natural swing; downhill: lengthen them ~5–10 cm to reach the slope.
  • Aggressive alpine skiers sometimes go a touch shorter; deep-powder and touring skiers a touch longer.
  • Adjustable poles let you tune on the trail — set them by feel and use these as a starting point.
