# EV vs Gas Cost Calculator

Compare the **cost per mile** of an electric car versus a gas car and see your **annual fuel savings**.

**[Open the tool →](https://awictor.github.io/ev-savings/)**

- Gas $/mile = price ÷ MPG; EV $/mile = $/kWh ÷ miles-per-kWh
- Annual savings from your mileage
- For anyone weighing an EV switch
- Dark mode, 100% offline, no dependencies, no tracking

## Example

A 3.5 mi/kWh EV at $0.15/kWh (~4.3¢/mi) vs a 28 MPG car at $3.50/gal (~12.5¢/mi) saves ~$1,000/yr over 12,000 miles. Fuel only.
