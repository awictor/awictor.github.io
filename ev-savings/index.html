Cost per mile is the honest comparison

Fuel cost per mile is price ÷ efficiency: for gas that's $/gal ÷ MPG, for an EV it's $/kWh ÷ miles-per-kWh. Electricity is usually far cheaper per mile — a 3.5 mi/kWh EV at $0.15/kWh costs about 4.3¢/mi, versus ~12.5¢/mi for a 28 MPG car at $3.50/gal. Over 12,000 miles that's roughly $1,000 saved a year. This covers fuel only, not purchase price, maintenance, or home-charger install. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
