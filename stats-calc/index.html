Descriptive statistics for a list of numbers

Population vs sample

Variance and standard deviation come in two flavours. Population statistics (dividing by n) treat your numbers as the entire group. Sample statistics (dividing by n−1, "Bessel's correction") treat them as a sample drawn from a larger population and give an unbiased estimate — this is the one you usually want for experimental data. Quartiles split the sorted data into four equal parts (Q1/median/Q3), and the interquartile range (IQR = Q3−Q1) measures the spread of the middle half.
