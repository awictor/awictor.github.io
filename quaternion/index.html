About quaternions

A quaternion q = w + xi + yj + zk extends complex numbers to four dimensions, where i² = j² = k² = ijk = −1. Unlike vectors, quaternion multiplication (the Hamilton product) is non-commutative: ij = k but ji = −k. Unit quaternions are the standard, gimbal-lock-free way to represent 3D rotations in games, robotics, aerospace, and computer graphics — smoother to interpolate than Euler angles or matrices. The conjugate negates the vector part; for a unit quaternion the conjugate equals the inverse. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
