The metrics

NOI = gross rent − operating expenses (before mortgage). Cap rate = NOI ÷ price — the unleveraged yield, used to compare properties. Cash-on-cash = annual pre-tax cash flow (NOI − debt service) ÷ the actual cash you put in — your leveraged return. Gross rent multiplier = price ÷ gross rent — a quick screening ratio (lower is cheaper per rent dollar). Operating expenses exclude the mortgage; debt service is counted only in cash-on-cash.
