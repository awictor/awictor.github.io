How it works

  • The kerfs span the curve, so arc length = radius × angle (in radians): r × angle° × π/180.
  • Cuts needed = arc length ÷ your kerf spacing, rounded up.
  • Find spacing empirically: make one kerf in scrap, lift the end until the kerf just closes, and measure how far along you had to go — that distance is your spacing.
  • Leave a thin, consistent web of wood at the base of each cut so the piece bends smoothly without snapping.
