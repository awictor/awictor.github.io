How it works

  • Wood barely moves along its length but swells and shrinks noticeably across the grain as humidity changes through the year.
  • Change = width × the species' dimensional-change coefficient × the moisture-content swing (in % points).
  • Flatsawn (tangential) boards move roughly twice as much as quartersawn (radial) — a reason quartersawn is prized for stable panels.
  • Design for it: breadboard ends, floating panels, elongated screw holes, and gaps between deck/floor boards.
  • A typical indoor seasonal swing is about 6% MC (roughly 4% winter to 10% summer). Values are averages from the Wood Handbook.
