How the comparison works

Over the lease term: leasing costs the drive-off + all monthly payments + the end-of-lease disposition fee, and you own nothing. Buying costs the down payment + the loan payments you've made by then + any loan balance still owed, minus the car's resale value (the equity you keep). Lower net cost wins. This ignores taxes, insurance and maintenance (similar for both) and the time value of money — a simplified comparison, not financial advice. Everything runs locally.
