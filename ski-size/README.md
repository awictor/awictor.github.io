# Ski Size 🎿

Find the right **ski length** from your height, skill level, and weight. Single offline HTML file, no dependencies, no data leaves your device.

## Use it

Open `index.html` in any browser, or visit the [live version](https://awictor.github.io/ski-size/).

Enter your height and weight, pick a skill level, and get a recommended ski length in centimetres — plus the base length for your skill and the weight adjustment applied.

## How it works

- Skis are sized between your chin and the top of your head — shorter for control, longer for speed and float.
- Beginner skis sit lowest (about height − 18 cm), expert skis reach the top of your head.
- Heavier riders size up a few centimetres for stability; lighter riders size down.
- Powder and freeride skis run longer; carving and park skis run shorter — treat this as a starting point.
