# Changelog

## v0.1.0 — 2026-09-11

Initial release.

- Hyperfocal distance H = f²/(N·c) + f
- Depth of field: near limit, far limit, and total, from focus distance
- Circle-of-confusion presets by sensor size
- Infinite far limit when focused at or beyond the hyperfocal distance
- Dark mode with persistence, fully offline, no dependencies
- 10 self-tests including near = H/2 and infinite far limit at the hyperfocal distance
