About Black-Scholes

The Black-Scholes-Merton model prices European options assuming lognormal returns and constant volatility. With spot S, strike K, volatility σ, risk-free rate r, and time to expiry T: d₁ = [ln(S/K) + (r + σ²/2)T] / (σ√T), d₂ = d₁ − σ√T, and Call = S·N(d₁) − K·e^(−rT)·N(d₂). The Greeks measure sensitivity: Delta to spot, Gamma to Delta, Vega to volatility, Theta to time decay, and Rho to rates. This tool also checks put-call parity (C − P = S − K·e^(−rT)) as a sanity test. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
