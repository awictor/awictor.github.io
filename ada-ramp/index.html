The ADA 1:12 rule

The ADA maximum slope for a new ramp is 1:12 — one inch of rise needs at least twelve inches of run, an 8.33% grade. So a 30″ rise (a common porch height) needs a 30 ft run and, with landings, quite a bit more. The ramp itself (the sloped board) is the hypotenuse, √(rise² + run²). Individual ramp runs may not rise more than 30″ before a level landing is required. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
