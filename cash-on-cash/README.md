# Cash-on-Cash Return Calculator

Calculate **cash-on-cash return**, monthly and annual **cash flow**, and total **cash invested** for a rental property.

**[Open the tool →](https://awictor.github.io/cash-on-cash/)**

- Monthly and annual pre-tax cash flow
- Cash-on-cash return: annual cash flow ÷ total cash invested
- Accounts for leverage (mortgage), unlike cap rate
- Dark mode, 100% offline, no dependencies, no tracking

## Example

$2,000 rent, $500 expenses, $900 mortgage on $65,000 invested → $600/mo, $7,200/yr, ~11.1% cash-on-cash. Estimates only, not investment advice.
