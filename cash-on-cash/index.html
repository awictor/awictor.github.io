What cash-on-cash tells you

Cash-on-cash return is annual pre-tax cash flow ÷ total cash you put in: (rent − expenses − mortgage) × 12 ÷ (down + closing + rehab). Unlike cap rate, it accounts for leverage — the mortgage — so it reflects the actual return on the dollars you spent. Investors often look for 8–12%, but that depends on market and risk. Operating expenses should include taxes, insurance, management, vacancy, and maintenance reserves; leave out the mortgage there since it's entered separately. Estimates only — not investment advice.
