About energy units

The SI unit of energy is the joule (J). Everyday units are just scaled versions: a food Calorie is a kilocalorie (4,184 J), your electricity bill uses kilowatt-hours (1 kWh = 3.6 million J), heating and cooling use BTU (≈1,055 J), mechanical work uses foot-pounds, and physics uses the tiny electronvolt. This tool converts through joules so every unit stays exact. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
