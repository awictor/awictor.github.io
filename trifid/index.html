Trifid Cipher

Delastelle fractionation over a 27-letter cube

How Trifid works

Trifid is Félix Delastelle's 3-D sequel to Bifid. The 27 symbols (A–Z plus one extra) fill a 3×3×3 cube, so every letter has three coordinates — layer, row and column. Within each block (the period), all the layers are written out, then all the rows, then all the columns, and that stream is re-read in groups of three to form the ciphertext. Spreading three coordinates across the block gives stronger diffusion than Bifid's two. Non-letters are dropped and text is upper-cased. Everything runs locally.
