How it works

  • Rennet dose scales with milk volume. Typical single-strength liquid rennet is about 1 ml per 4 L (0.25 ml/L); double-strength is half that.
  • Tablets are dosed by count — roughly one tablet per 19 L — so this shows the fraction of a tablet.
  • The strength slider scales the dose: bump it up for a firmer set or hard cheeses, down for soft, slow-set styles.
  • Dilute liquid rennet in cool, non-chlorinated water before stirring gently into the milk — chlorine and hot water kill activity.
  • Always follow your rennet's label; potency varies by brand and age.
