How an ideal transformer scales

Voltage follows the turns ratio: Vₛ = Vₚ × Nₛ/Nₚ. More secondary turns steps voltage up, fewer steps it down. Current goes the opposite way — Iₛ = Iₚ × Nₚ/Nₛ — so power is conserved (VₚIₚ = VₛIₛ in an ideal transformer). Impedance transforms with the square of the turns ratio, which is why transformers match speaker and tube-amp impedances. Real transformers have losses; this assumes an ideal one.
