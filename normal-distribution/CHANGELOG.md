# Changelog

## v0.1.0 — 2026-09-11

Initial release.

- z-score, cumulative probability Φ(z), percentile, and upper-tail probability
- Probability between two z-scores
- Error-function-based CDF (Abramowitz–Stegun approximation)
- Dark mode with persistence, fully offline, no dependencies
- 10 self-tests including Φ(1)≈0.8413, the 68/95 rules, and symmetry
