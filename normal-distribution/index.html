About the normal distribution

The z-score z = (x − μ) / σ says how many standard deviations a value sits from the mean. The cumulative probability Φ(z) is the area under the bell curve to the left of z — i.e. the fraction of data below x, which is also its percentile. Φ is computed here from the error function (Abramowitz–Stegun approximation, accurate to ~1e-7). Handy landmarks: Φ(0) = 50%, Φ(1) ≈ 84.1%, and ±1.96σ brackets the central 95%.
