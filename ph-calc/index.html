About pH

pH measures how acidic or basic a solution is: pH = −log₁₀[H⁺], where [H⁺] is the hydrogen-ion concentration in mol/L. Its partner pOH = −log₁₀[OH⁻], and at 25 °C they always sum to 14 because water's ion product [H⁺][OH⁻] = 10⁻¹⁴. pH below 7 is acidic, exactly 7 is neutral, above 7 is basic. Each whole pH unit is a tenfold change in acidity. Enter any one of the four quantities and the rest follow. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
