# Changelog

## v0.1.0 — 2026-09-11

Initial release.

- Convert between pH, pOH, [H⁺], and [OH⁻]
- Acidic/neutral/basic classification and colour-coded pH scale
- Uses the 25 °C water ion product Kw = 1×10⁻¹⁴
- Dark mode with persistence, fully offline, no dependencies
- 10 self-tests including neutral water, the Kw relation, and monotonicity
