How it works

  • A chimney drafts by pulling smoke up a flue whose area is proportioned to the fireplace opening it serves.
  • The masonry rule of thumb: flue area ≥ 1/10 of the opening area for square liners, 1/12 for round liners (they draft better), and 1/8 for short or exterior chimneys.
  • We turn that minimum area into a round liner diameter and round up to the next standard clay/steel size (6, 7, 8, 10, 12, 15, 18"…).
  • Too small a flue smokes into the room; oversized cools the gases and drafts poorly — bigger is not better.
  • Follow NFPA 211 and your local code, and account for chimney height and offsets.
