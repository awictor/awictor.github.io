Why avoid clichés?

Clichés and business-speak ("synergy", "circle back", "move the needle") are so overused they've stopped meaning much — readers skim past them. Swapping in plain, specific language makes writing clearer and more memorable. This flags ~60 of the most common offenders so you can decide what to keep.
