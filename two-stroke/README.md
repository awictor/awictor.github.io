# Two-Stroke Fuel Mix Calculator

Calculate how much **two-stroke oil** to add to your fuel for a given gas-to-oil ratio (50:1, 40:1, …), in mL and fluid ounces.

**[Open the tool →](https://awictor.github.io/two-stroke/)**

- Oil = fuel ÷ ratio
- US gallons or liters; oil in mL and fl oz
- Total mix volume and reverse ratio-from-volumes
- Dark mode, 100% offline, no dependencies, no tracking

## Example

1 US gallon of gas at 50:1 needs about 75.7 mL (2.6 fl oz) of oil. Always follow your tool's manual.
