Mixing 2-stroke fuel

A ratio like 50:1 means 50 parts gasoline to 1 part oil, so the oil you add is fuel ÷ 50. Modern air-cooled engines usually call for 50:1; older saws and trimmers often want 40:1 or 32:1 — always follow the tool's manual, and use oil rated for air-cooled two-strokes. Mix in a clean, approved container, shake before fueling, and don't store mixed fuel for months. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
