How to use it

Pot odds are the price of a call: you must win at least call ÷ (pot + call) of the time to break even. Estimate your chance of hitting with the rule of 2 and 4 — multiply your outs by 4 with two cards to come, or by 2 with one. If your draw equity beats the equity the pot demands, calling is profitable in the long run. A 9-out flush draw is about 19% on the river, so you need pot odds better than roughly 4-to-1.
