About confidence intervals

A confidence interval is a range that, over repeated sampling, contains the true value a stated fraction of the time. For a mean: x̄ ± z·s/√n; for a proportion: p ± z·√(p(1−p)/n). The margin of error is the ± part; it shrinks as the sample grows (∝ 1/√n) and widens with higher confidence (z = 1.96 for 95%, 2.576 for 99%). This uses the normal (z) approximation, good for large samples. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
