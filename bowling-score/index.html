How scoring works

Each of the ten frames is up to two rolls. Knock all ten down on the first ball for a strike — worth 10 plus your next two rolls. Clear them in two for a spare — 10 plus your next one roll. Otherwise you score the pins you hit. The 10th frame grants extra fill balls so a closing strike or spare can be bonused. A perfect game of twelve strikes is 300.
