How it works

  • Lap length ≈ factor × bar diameter. A common tension lap for Grade 60 bar is about 40× the diameter.
  • Use a larger factor for top bars, epoxy coating, or lower-strength concrete; codes never allow less than 12 inches.
  • Bar number ÷ 8 gives the diameter in inches (#4 = 4/8 = 1/2").
  • This is a planning estimate — actual lap length must follow ACI 318 and your engineer's drawings.
