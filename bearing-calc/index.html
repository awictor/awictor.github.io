About great-circle bearings

The initial bearing (or forward azimuth) is the compass direction you'd start heading to travel the shortest path along a great circle. Because that path curves, the bearing changes en route — the final bearing is your heading on arrival. The formula is θ = atan2( sin(Δλ)·cos φ₂, cos φ₁·sin φ₂ − sin φ₁·cos φ₂·cos Δλ ), normalized to 0–360°. The back bearing is simply the initial bearing ± 180°. Bearings are given as degrees clockwise from true north and mapped to the 16-point compass. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
