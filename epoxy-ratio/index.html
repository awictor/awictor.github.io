Mixing epoxy

The volume to fill a pour is area × thickness: 1000 cm² at 3 mm deep is 300 mL. Split that by the manufacturer's mix ratio — a 2:1 epoxy is two parts resin to one part hardener, so 300 mL is 200 mL resin and 100 mL hardener. Measure precisely (off-ratio epoxy may never fully cure), mix thoroughly scraping the sides, and respect the pot life and max pour depth on the label.
