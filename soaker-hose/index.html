How it works

  • One inch of water over one square foot is 0.623 gallons, so gallons = area × depth × 0.623.
  • Run time = gallons ÷ the hose flow rate; most soaker hoses run about 0.5 GPM per 50–100 ft at low pressure.
  • Vegetables and lawns usually want about 1 inch of water per week — split it across a couple of sessions.
  • Measure your hose's real flow by timing how long it takes to fill a known container, then use that GPM.
