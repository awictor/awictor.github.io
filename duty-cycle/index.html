About PWM & duty cycle

Pulse-width modulation switches a signal on and off quickly; the duty cycle is the fraction of each period the signal is high, D = t_on / T × 100%. The period is T = 1/f. A microcontroller uses PWM to fake analog output: the average voltage is Vcc × D, so 40% duty on a 5 V pin averages 2 V — enough to dim an LED or slow a motor without wasting power in a resistor. Frequency sets how fast it switches (too low and you see flicker); duty cycle sets the level. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
