How the minimum is calculated

Most issuers set the minimum as a small percent of the balance plus that month's interest, with a dollar floor (often $25–$35). Because the payment shrinks as the balance falls, minimum-only payoff drags on for years and interest piles up. This tool simulates each month for both plans so you can see the gap. Estimates assume a fixed APR and no new charges; your issuer's exact rule may differ. Everything runs locally.
