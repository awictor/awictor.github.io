How it works

  • A feeding ratio like 1:5:5 means 1 part existing starter, 5 parts flour, 5 parts water by weight.
  • So 20 g of starter fed 1:5:5 takes 100 g flour and 100 g water, making 220 g of active starter.
  • Equal flour and water keeps a 100% hydration starter — the most common style.
  • Bigger ratios (more food per part starter) rise more slowly — handy for feeding at night or in a warm kitchen.
  • To hit a target amount, keep starter = target ÷ (1 + flour + water parts); the rest is fresh flour and water.
