Why cut adverbs?

"-ly" adverbs often prop up a weak verb — "ran quickly" is usually better as "sprinted", "said loudly" as "shouted". Editors (and the Hemingway app) flag them so you can choose a stronger verb or delete the crutch. This tool skips common non-adverb "-ly" words like family, reply, only, friendly, and likely. A few adverbs are fine — aim low, not zero.
