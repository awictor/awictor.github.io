Why price per area?

Pizza is priced by name (small/medium/large) but you eat area, and area grows with the square of the diameter — a 16″ pizza has 78% more pizza than a 12″, not 33% more. The best deal is the one with the most square inches per dollar. Set the quantity to compare deals like "two 12″ vs one 18″". Assumes equal toppings and crust. Everything runs locally.
