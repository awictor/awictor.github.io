How it works

  • Backing must be bigger than the top — usually 4″ extra on every side so a longarm has room to clamp and quilt.
  • Standard cotton is only ~42″ usable, so a wide quilt needs several panels seamed selvage-to-selvage. Each seam joins two panels.
  • Yardage = panels × backing length ÷ 36. A 60×80″ top wants a 68×88″ backing — two 42″ panels, about 4.9 yards.
  • Extra-wide backing (108″) often covers a whole quilt in one panel with no seams — less fabric and no matching.
  • Buy a little extra for squaring up and shrinkage; prewash if your top was prewashed.
