About betting odds

The same wager can be quoted four ways. Decimal odds (e.g. 2.50) are the total return per unit staked. American / moneyline odds show profit on a $100 stake: +150 means win $150, −200 means stake $200 to win $100. Fractional (UK) odds like 3/2 give profit-to-stake. Implied probability is simply 1 / decimal — the break-even win rate the price implies (before the bookmaker's margin). Enter any one and the rest update.
