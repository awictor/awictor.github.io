About board feet

A board foot is the standard volume unit for pricing lumber (especially hardwood): one board 1 inch thick, 12 inches wide, and 1 foot long. The formula is BF = thickness(in) × width(in) × length(in) / 144, or thickness × width × length(ft) / 12. A 1×6 board 8 feet long is 4 board feet. Note that "1 inch" refers to the nominal rough thickness — surfaced lumber is actually thinner, but it's still priced by the nominal board foot. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
