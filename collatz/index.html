About the Collatz conjecture

Pick any positive integer. If it's even, halve it; if it's odd, triple it and add one. Repeat. The Collatz conjecture (also called the 3n+1 or hailstone problem) says you always eventually reach 1 — no exceptions have ever been found, yet no one has proved it. The path can be wild: starting from 27 it climbs to a peak of 9,232 and takes 111 steps before crashing to 1. This tool traces the full sequence for any number. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
