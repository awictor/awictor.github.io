# Fuel Range Calculator

Work out your driving **range** on a full tank, **distance to empty** from fuel remaining, **fuel needed** for a trip, and **usable range** keeping a safety reserve.

**[Open the tool →](https://awictor.github.io/fuel-range/)**

- Range = tank × MPG
- Distance to empty from fuel remaining
- Fuel for a trip = distance ÷ MPG
- Usable range keeping an X% reserve
- Dark mode, 100% offline, no dependencies, no tracking

## Example

A 15-gal tank at 30 MPG goes 450 mi (405 mi keeping a 10% reserve). 5 gal left = 150 mi to empty.
