How it works

Range is just tank × MPG — a 15-gallon tank at 30 MPG goes 450 miles. Distance to empty uses whatever fuel is left, and the fuel a trip needs is distance ÷ MPG. Sensible drivers keep a reserve (10% here) rather than running to fumes, which trims usable range to tank × (1 − reserve) × MPG. Real range varies with speed, terrain, and load, so treat these as planning numbers. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
