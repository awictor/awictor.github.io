How wall estimating works

Masonry is counted per square foot of wall face. A standard modular brick with a 3/8″ mortar joint takes about 6.86 bricks per sq ft; an 8×8×16 concrete block (CMU) takes about 1.125 per sq ft. So units = length × height × per sq ft, rounded up and padded with a waste allowance (typically 5–10%) for cuts and breakage. Mortar use depends on unit size: roughly one 80 lb bag lays ~120 bricks or ~28 blocks. Subtract large door and window openings from the area for a big job, and always buy a little extra so everything matches. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
