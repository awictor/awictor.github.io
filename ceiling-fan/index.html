Picking the right fan

A ceiling fan should match the room so it moves air without dominating it. Sizing goes by floor area: up to ~75 sq ft wants a 36″ fan, small bedrooms and kitchens (up to ~144 sq ft) a 42″, standard bedrooms and dens (to ~225 sq ft) a 52″, large rooms (to ~400 sq ft) a 56″, and great rooms a 60″+ or two fans. For airflow and safety the blades should sit 8–9 ft above the floor and at least ~18″ from any wall, so on ceilings above 9 ft you add a downrod of roughly ceiling height − 9 ft; at 8–9 ft use a flush "hugger" mount.
