About the speed of sound

In dry air, sound travels at about v = 331.3 + 0.606·T m/s, where T is the temperature in °C — roughly 343 m/s at 20 °C. It's faster when warmer because the air molecules move quicker. Mach number is an object's speed divided by the local speed of sound: Mach 1 is the sound barrier. And since light is effectively instant, counting the seconds between a lightning flash and its thunder and multiplying by the speed of sound tells you how far away the strike was — about a kilometre every 3 seconds.
