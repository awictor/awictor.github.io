About repeating decimals

Every fraction is either a terminating decimal (like 1/8 = 0.125) or a repeating one (like 1/7 = 0.142857142857…). This tool runs exact long division, tracking remainders to detect where the cycle begins, and writes it in parenthesis notation: 1/7 = 0.(142857), 1/6 = 0.1(6). Going the other way, any repeating decimal is a rational number — 0.(3) = 1/3 — recovered exactly with integer arithmetic (no floating-point rounding). Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
