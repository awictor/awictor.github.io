How employer matching works

A common match is "50% up to 6%": the employer adds 50¢ for every $1 you contribute, but only on the first 6% of your salary. Contributing at least the cap captures the full match — anything less leaves free money behind. The 2025 employee deferral limit is $23,500 (plus catch-up if 50+); the employer match doesn't count against it.
