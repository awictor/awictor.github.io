US butter measures

American recipes measure butter by the stick, which is a quarter-pound: 1 stick = 8 tablespoons = ½ cup = 4 oz ≈ 113.4 g. The wrapper is even marked in tablespoons so you can cut what you need. Everything here converts through grams as the common base. Note this is US butter — a European block is usually 250 g (not 4 sticks exactly), and butter is measured by weight, so a "cup" assumes it's packed solid, not melted. For precise baking, weigh in grams. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
