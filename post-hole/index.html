How much concrete a post needs

A set post fills a round hole minus the space the post itself takes up, so concrete volume is hole − post. The hole is a cylinder, π · (diameter/2)² · depth; the post here is treated as a square column, side² · depth; divide by 1728 to turn cubic inches into cubic feet. Rules of thumb: dig the hole about 3× the post width across and bury ⅓–½ of the post's above-ground height, below your local frost line. A 60 lb bag of mixed concrete yields about 0.45 cu ft and an 80 lb bag about 0.60. Round up and buy a spare bag — it's cheaper than a second trip.
