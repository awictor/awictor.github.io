About the GCS

The Glasgow Coma Scale grades consciousness by the best response in three areas — eye opening (1–4), verbal (1–5) and motor (1–6) — summed to a total of 3–15. Severity bands: 13–15 mild, 9–12 moderate, 3–8 severe (a total ≤ 8 generally indicates coma). Report the components too (e.g. "E3 V4 M5 = 12"), since the pattern matters. A clinical aid, not a substitute for full assessment. Everything runs locally.
