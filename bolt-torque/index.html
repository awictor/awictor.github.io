Tightening torque

The torque–tension relationship

Tightening a bolt stretches it, and that stretch creates the clamp force holding a joint together. The short-form estimate is T = K · D · F, where T is torque, D the nominal diameter, F the target clamp force, and K a friction factor (nut factor). K is roughly 0.20 dry, 0.15 lubricated, and lower with anti-seize — most of your torque fights friction, not tension, so lube dramatically changes the result. Diameter here is in millimetres (converted to metres internally). Always defer to the manufacturer's spec. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
