About coordinate formats

The same GPS point can be written three ways. Decimal degrees (DD) like 40.7128, −74.0060 is compact and what most software uses. Degrees-minutes-seconds (DMS) like 40°42′46″N is traditional on maps and in surveying. Degrees decimal minutes (DDM) like 40°42.768′N is standard in marine and aviation navigation. One degree = 60 minutes = 3600 seconds; the sign becomes a hemisphere letter (N/S for latitude, E/W for longitude). Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
