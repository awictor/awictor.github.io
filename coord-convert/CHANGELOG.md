# Changelog

## v0.1.0 — 2026-09-11

Initial release.

- Convert decimal degrees to DMS (degrees-minutes-seconds) and DDM (degrees decimal minutes)
- Hemisphere letters (N/S, E/W) with correct sign handling
- Latitude/longitude range validation
- Dark mode with persistence, fully offline, no dependencies
- 10 self-tests including DMS/DDM round trips and negative-coordinate sign splitting
