How it works

  • Time how long a known container takes to fill, then GPM = gallons ÷ (seconds ÷ 60).
  • Federal limits: kitchen/bath faucets 2.2 GPM, showerheads 2.5 GPM (lower in some states).
  • A low-flow aerator can cut a faucet to 1.0–1.5 GPM and pays for itself fast on the water bill.
  • Daily and yearly figures assume the tap runs the full minutes you enter.
