# Brine Calculator

Calculate salt for a **percent brine**, **equilibrium (EQ)** brining as a percent of total weight, resulting **concentration**, and **pink curing salt** for a target nitrite ppm.

**[Open the tool →](https://awictor.github.io/brine-calc/)**

- Percent brine: salt = water × %
- EQ brine: salt as % of (meat + water) — can't over-salt
- Salt concentration and pink cure #1 dosing
- Dark mode, 100% offline, no dependencies, no tracking

## Example

A 5% brine of 1 L water = 50 g salt. EQ brining 2 kg total at 2% = 40 g. 156 ppm cure #1 for 1 kg ≈ 2.5 g. Follow trusted curing guidance.
