Brining vs. equilibrium curing

A traditional percent brine dissolves salt equal to a percentage of the water's weight — a 5% brine is 50 g salt per litre. With equilibrium (EQ) brining you weigh the salt as a percentage of the total (meat + water), so the food can never over-salt no matter how long it sits — great for repeatable results. For cured meats, pink curing salt #1 (6.25% sodium nitrite) is dosed to a target ppm; 156 ppm is the classic ~2.5 g per kg. Follow trusted curing guidance for safety.
