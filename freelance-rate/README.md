# Freelance Rate Calculator

Calculate the **hourly rate a freelancer needs** from a target income, business expenses, and billable hours — plus billable capacity and effective rate.

**[Open the tool →](https://awictor.github.io/freelance-rate/)**

- Required rate = (target income + expenses) ÷ billable hours
- Billable hours per year from weekly hours × working weeks
- Income-from-rate and effective-rate helpers
- Dark mode, 100% offline, no dependencies, no tracking

## Example

$80k take-home, $20k expenses, 1,200 billable hours/yr → ~$83/hour.
