Why your rate isn't your salary ÷ 2080

Freelancers only bill a fraction of their working hours — the rest goes to admin, sales, and downtime — and they cover their own expenses, taxes, insurance, and time off. Your required rate is (target income + expenses) ÷ billable hours. Fewer billable hours or higher costs push the rate up. If you want to take home $80k, spend $20k running the business, and can bill 1,200 hours a year, you need $83/hour. Build in a buffer for slow months.
