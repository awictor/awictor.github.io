How it's estimated

Take-home = gross − pre-tax deductions − income tax − FICA. FICA is 6.2% Social Security (up to the annual wage base, $168,600 for 2024) + 1.45% Medicare on all wages. Because everyone's brackets and state taxes differ, you supply your own effective income-tax rate (total income tax ÷ taxable pay) and it's applied to pay after pre-tax deductions. A simplified US estimate — not tax advice; use your pay stub or a full withholding calculator for exact figures. Everything runs locally.
