Your climb
How to play

Goal: be the first climber to permanently anchor 3 couloirs (the gullies numbered 2–12) to the summit.

Each turn: roll four dice, split them into two sums, and slide a temporary piton up each matching couloir. You get up to 3 active pitons per turn. Keep rolling to climb higher — but if a roll can place none of your active pitons you slip and lose everything gained this turn. Bank & anchor at any point to lock in your progress and end your turn safely.

Geometry: center couloirs (7) are the tallest climbs but easiest to hit; edge couloirs (2/12) are short but rarely rolled, so pressing on the edges is riskier.

Nerve meter: shows the exact bust chance, expected gain, and roll-vs-bank EV for your current position — the same math the AI plays.

Markers: ● you · ● AI · dashed ring = an unbanked piton · flag = a claimed couloir.
