How it works

  • Every 33 ft of seawater adds one atmosphere, so at depth you breathe gas faster in proportion to the pressure.
  • SAC normalizes your consumption back to the surface: SAC = psi used ÷ (minutes × ATA). It's a personal constant you can plan any dive with.
  • Multiply by your tank factor (cu ft ÷ service psi) to get RMV — your true breathing volume in cubic feet per minute.
  • Duration at a new depth = tank psi ÷ (SAC × ATA). Deeper means shorter, fast.
  • Measure SAC on a relaxed, flat dive; stress and work raise it. Always plan gas with a reserve.
