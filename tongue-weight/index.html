Why tongue weight matters

Tongue weight is the downward force the loaded trailer puts on the hitch ball. For a stable tow it should be 10–15% of the trailer's total loaded weight. Too little (under 10%) and the trailer can start swaying — a dangerous fishtail that gets worse with speed. Too much (over 15%) and it lifts the tow vehicle's front wheels, lightening the steering and overloading the rear axle and hitch. Aim for the middle of the range and adjust by shifting cargo: move weight forward to add tongue weight, back to reduce it, keeping about 60% of the load ahead of the trailer axle.
