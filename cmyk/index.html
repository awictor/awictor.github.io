About CMYK

CMYK (Cyan, Magenta, Yellow, Key/black) is the subtractive color model used by printers. Screens use additive RGB, so print colors are converted to CMYK. This tool uses the standard device-independent (naive) conversion — actual output depends on your printer, inks, and ICC profile. Total ink coverage (C+M+Y+K) matters for print: many presses cap it around 240–300% to avoid smearing and drying problems.
