Bisection vs Newton

Bisection needs a bracket [a, b] where f changes sign; it halves the interval each step and always converges (slowly, linearly) to the root between them. Newton's method starts from a single guess and follows the tangent: xₙ₊₁ = xₙ − f(xₙ)/f′(xₙ) — much faster (quadratic) but it can diverge or land on the wrong root if the guess is poor.
