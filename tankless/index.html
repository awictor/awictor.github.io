How tankless sizing works

A tankless heater must raise incoming water to your target temperature on the fly, so it's sized by two numbers: the flow rate you need at once (GPM) and the temperature rise — target minus incoming. Water takes about 500 BTU/hr to raise 1 gallon per minute by 1 °F, so BTU = GPM × 500 × rise. Cold northern groundwater (~40 °F) needs a much bigger rise than warm southern water (~65 °F), which is why the same unit delivers fewer gallons per minute up north. Add up simultaneous fixtures (a shower is ~2 GPM, a sink ~1) for peak flow. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
