What is a happy number?

Take a number, sum the squares of its digits, and repeat. If you eventually reach 1, it's a happy number; otherwise you fall into a loop that always includes 4 (…4, 16, 37, 58, 89, 145, 42, 20, 4…) and the number is "unhappy". For example 19 → 82 → 68 → 100 → 1. The happy numbers below 50 are 1, 7, 10, 13, 19, 23, 28, 31, 32, 44, and 49. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
