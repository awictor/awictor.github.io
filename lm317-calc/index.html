How the LM317 works

The LM317 holds a fixed 1.25 V reference between its output and adjust pins. With R1 from output to adjust and R2 from adjust to ground, the output is V = 1.25 × (1 + R2/R1). A common R1 is 240 Ω. In constant-current mode a single resistor sets the current at 1.25 ÷ R. Watch power dissipation — the regulator burns (Vin − Vout) × I as heat, which sets your heatsink.
