Dice notation

Tabletop dice use the form NdM — roll N dice with M sides — optionally with a flat modifier: 3d6+2 rolls three six-sided dice and adds 2. Leave N off for a single die (d20), and combine terms with + and − (2d6+1d4-1). The min, average, and max are computed from the notation without rolling; the Roll button generates an actual random result with the per-die breakdown. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
