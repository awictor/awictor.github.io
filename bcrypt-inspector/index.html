Reading a bcrypt hash

Format: $2<variant>$<cost>$<22-char salt><31-char hash>. The cost factor sets 2^cost key-expansion rounds — each +1 doubles the work. Cost 10 = 1,024 rounds; a modern recommendation is 12+ (4,096+). Higher cost = slower to crack but slower to verify. Variants (2a/2b/2x/2y) are compatibility tags; 2b is current.
