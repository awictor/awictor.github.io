How it works

  • Grout fills the joints between tiles. Per square foot: ((L + W) ÷ (L × W)) × joint width × joint depth × 144 cubic inches.
  • Smaller tiles need much more grout — more joints per square foot. Mosaics can use several times what large-format tile does.
  • A 25 lb bag of sanded grout yields roughly 380 cubic inches mixed. We add 10% for waste and mixing loss.
  • Always buy a little extra from the same lot — grout color can vary batch to batch.
