Why the jackpot is so hard to win

Order doesn't matter in a lottery, so the number of possible tickets is a combination: C(n, k) = n! / (k!·(n−k)!). A bonus ball drawn from a separate pool multiplies the odds by that pool's size. Picking 5 of 69 has ~11.2 million combinations; add a Powerball from 26 and it's over 292 million to one. Buying two tickets only doubles a near-zero chance. This is the math, not gambling advice.
