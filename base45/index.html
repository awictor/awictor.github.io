About Base45

Base45 (RFC 9285) packs binary data into a 45-character alphabet chosen from the QR-code "alphanumeric" mode, so it stores more per QR module than Base64 — which is why it encodes EU Digital COVID Certificates. Bytes are taken in pairs: each pair (a 16-bit number) becomes three Base45 characters; a lone trailing byte becomes two. Text is treated as UTF-8. Known vector: "AB" → BB8. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
