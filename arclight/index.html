How to Play Arclight

The Arc Chain. Every card has a conductance color and a shape: ◆ Copper (attack), ■ Cobalt (defense), ▲ Verdigris (power), ● Scrap (neutral). Play cards of the same color back-to-back to raise your Arc. Each point of Arc multiplies the numbers on your next same-color card (+0.5× per Arc by default). Play a different color and the current grounds out — Arc resets to 0. Scrap cards are neutral: they neither build nor break the chain. ⚡ Discharge. Discharge cards spend ALL stored Arc for a burst that scales with how much you'd banked — the payoff for a long chain. Statuses. Vulnerable (◇ +50% dmg taken), Weak (~ -25% dmg dealt), Overclock (↑ strength), Corrosion (☣ poison), Grounded (⏚ energy denial), Static (✦ retaliate). Enemies telegraph their intent one turn ahead and react to your play: they guard when you stack Arc, apply Weak to attack-heavy hands, and enrage at low HP. Keyboard: 1–9 play the card in that hand slot, E end turn, Esc deselect. Colorblind-safe: shapes + icons encode color, never color alone. Autosaves to your browser; runs are seeded and shareable via the URL.
