Modular Exponentiation

(base ^ exponent) mod m

—

Why fast exponentiation?

Computing bⁿ mod m by multiplying b out n times is hopeless when n is huge — and it overflows. Exponentiation by squaring does it in about log₂(n) steps: square the base and take the modulus at each bit of the exponent. This is the workhorse of public-key cryptography — RSA and Diffie-Hellman raise numbers to enormous exponents mod a large modulus. This tool uses big integers, so exponents in the thousands are instant and exact. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
