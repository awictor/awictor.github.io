About the humidex

The humidex is the Canadian index of how hot it feels when humidity is factored in — analogous to the US heat index but computed from air temperature and dew point. It adds a humidity term to the air temperature via the vapour pressure: humidex = T + 0.5555·(e − 10). Comfort scale: below 30 is comfortable, 30–39 is some discomfort, 40–45 is great discomfort (avoid exertion), and 46+ is dangerous. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
