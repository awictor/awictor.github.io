How tiers work

Tiered commission is marginal, like tax brackets: each portion of sales within a tier earns that tier's rate. E.g. 5% up to $100k, then 10% above — on $150k you earn 5% × $100k + 10% × $50k = $10,000. Leave the top tier's "up to" blank for "and above".
