How cats age

Cats grow up fast, then slow down. The widely used veterinary guideline counts the first year as about 15 human years, the second year adds 9 more (24 total), and every year after that adds roughly 4. So a 3-year-old cat is about 28 in human terms, and a 10-year-old is about 56. Indoor cats often reach their late teens or beyond. This is an estimate — breed, weight, and health all matter.
