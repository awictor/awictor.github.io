AuthCode

Offline TOTP / 2FA authenticator

------
🔒 Codes are computed entirely in your browser — the secret never leaves this page and is not sent anywhere. It is saved to this browser's local storage for convenience; clear the field to remove it.

Enter the base32 secret your service showed when you set up 2FA (the string behind the QR code). Standard 30-second, 6-digit TOTP (RFC 6238).

AuthCode is part of the Toolkit — free, offline, no signup. · Source
