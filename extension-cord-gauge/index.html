How it works

  • Lower AWG = thicker wire. Longer cords and bigger loads need thicker wire to avoid voltage drop and overheating.
  • Based on 120 V household cords: a 10 A load is fine on 16 AWG at 25 ft but needs 12 AWG by 100 ft.
  • Watts ÷ 120 V gives amps; a device's amp draw is usually on its label.
  • Never chain extension cords, and unroll a cord fully — coiled cords under load trap heat.
