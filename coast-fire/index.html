What is Coast FIRE?

You've hit Coast FIRE when the money you've already invested will grow — with no further contributions — into your full retirement target by the time you retire. After that, you only need to earn enough to cover today's expenses; compounding does the rest. Target = annual spending ÷ withdrawal rate (the 4% rule gives 25×). The coast number discounts that target back to today at your real return: coast = target ÷ (1 + r)years. Estimates only, not financial advice. Everything runs locally.
