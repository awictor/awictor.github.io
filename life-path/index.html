How it works

  • Numerology adds up all the digits of your birth month, day, and year, then reduces the total to a single digit.
  • Reduction repeats until one digit remains — except the master numbers 11, 22, and 33, which are kept as-is.
  • Example: 1990-07-04 → 1+9+9+0+0+7+0+4 = 30 → 3+0 = 3.
  • Each number carries a traditional theme — 1 leader, 5 freedom, 7 seeker, 8 achiever, and so on.
  • This is for fun and reflection, not fortune-telling — a tidy bit of digit arithmetic with a long history.
