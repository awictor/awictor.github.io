About RPN

Reverse Polish Notation (postfix) writes operators after their operands, so no parentheses are ever needed: 3 4 + means 3 + 4, and 5 1 2 + 4 * + 3 - means (5 + ((1+2)×4)) − 3 = 14. It's evaluated with a stack — push numbers, and when an operator appears pop the top two, apply it, and push the result. HP calculators and the Forth language use RPN; it maps directly to how a CPU stack works. Supports + − * / ^ %. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
