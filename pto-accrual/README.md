# PTO Accrual Calculator

Calculate paid time off **accrual per hour** worked or **per pay period** from an annual PTO allowance, plus PTO earned to date.

**[Open the tool →](https://awictor.github.io/pto-accrual/)**

- Accrual rate per hour = annual PTO ÷ hours worked per year
- Per-pay-period accrual and PTO earned so far
- Hours and days views
- Dark mode, 100% offline, no dependencies, no tracking

## Example

80 hours/year over a 2,080-hour year ≈ 0.0385 hr per hour worked; at 1,040 hours you've banked ~40 hours (5 days).
