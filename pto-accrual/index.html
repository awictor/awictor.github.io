How PTO adds up

Many employers grant PTO by accrual — you earn a little with every hour worked rather than getting it all up front. The per-hour rate is annual PTO ÷ hours worked per year: 80 hours (two weeks) over a 2,080-hour year is about 0.0385 hours of PTO per hour worked. Multiply by hours worked so far to see what you've banked, or divide the annual allowance by pay periods to see each paycheck's accrual. Divide hours by your workday length to read it in days. Check your employer's policy for caps and carryover. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
