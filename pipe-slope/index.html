How it works

Drainage slope basics
  • Total fall = run × slope. At the standard 1/4" per foot, a 40 ft run drops 10 inches.
  • Percent grade = (fall ÷ 12) ÷ run × 100. A quarter-inch per foot is about 2.08%; an eighth-inch is 1.04%.
  • Most codes want at least 1/4" per foot for pipes up to 3", and allow 1/8" per foot for 4" and larger. Too flat won't clear solids; too steep lets water outrun them.
