Dialing in a cut

Every material and tool has a recommended surface speed in feet per minute (SFM). Spindle RPM comes from RPM = (SFM × 12) / (π × diameter) — smaller tools spin faster. Feed rate is how fast the tool advances: IPM = RPM × chip load × flutes, where chip load is the bite each cutting edge takes. Too slow rubs and dulls the tool; too fast breaks it. Start conservative and tune by sound and chips. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
