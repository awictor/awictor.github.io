Kinetic energy vs. momentum

An arrow's punch comes from its kinetic energy: KE = weight × speed² ÷ 450240, in ft·lb with weight in grains and speed in fps. Because it depends on speed squared, a fast light arrow shows big KE numbers. Momentum (weight × speed ÷ 225218) scales only with speed but rewards heavy arrows, and many hunters trust it as the better predictor of penetration through bone and hide. Rough bowhunting guidance by KE: under 25 ft·lb for small game, 25–41 for deer-sized animals, 42–65 for elk and black bear, and 65+ for the toughest game.
