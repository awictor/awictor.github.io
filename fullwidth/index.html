What is full-width text?

Full-width (or "fullwidth") characters are Unicode variants of the normal ASCII letters, digits and punctuation that each occupy a full em square — the size of a CJK character. Mapping A→Ａ, 1→１ and space→an ideographic space gives the spaced-out "ｖａｐｏｒｗａｖｅ" look popular in social-media bios and posts. It's real Unicode, so it pastes anywhere text is accepted. Non-ASCII characters (accents, emoji) pass through unchanged. Everything runs locally.
