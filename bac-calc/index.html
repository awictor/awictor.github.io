Estimate only. The Widmark formula is a rough approximation — real BAC depends on food, metabolism, medication and more. This can be off by a wide margin. Never use it to decide whether to drive. If you've been drinking, do not drive.

"Standard drink" defaults to 14 g of pure alcohol (US); the UK/AU unit is ~8–10 g. A 12 oz 5% beer, 5 oz 12% wine, or 1.5 oz 40% spirit each ≈ one US standard drink. Runs 100% locally.
