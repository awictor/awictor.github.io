# Changelog

## 0.1.0
- First release. Blood alcohol content (BAC) estimator (Widmark formula).
- Weight (kg/lb), sex, standard drinks, elapsed hours; adjustable grams/drink & elimination rate.
- Time until sober and until under 0.08; plain-language impairment status.
- Prominent "estimate only, never drive" safety framing. Dark mode, localStorage memory.
- Headless test suite (11 checks, known Widmark values) + CI.
