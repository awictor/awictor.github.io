The formula

gtt/min = (volume in mL × drop factor) ÷ time in minutes. The drop factor is printed on the IV tubing package — macrodrip sets are 10, 15 or 20 gtt/mL; microdrip (pediatric) sets are 60 gtt/mL. Count the drops in the drip chamber for one minute and adjust the roller clamp to match. Flow rate (mL/hr) and total infusion time are shown too.
