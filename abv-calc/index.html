About these numbers

ABV (alcohol by volume) is estimated from how much sugar the yeast converted — the drop from original gravity (before fermentation) to final gravity (after). The simple formula, (OG − FG) × 131.25, is quick and fine below ~6%. The standard formula corrects for higher gravities. Apparent attenuation, (OG − FG)/(OG − 1), is the percentage of sugar the yeast fermented. °Plato is the brewing sugar scale (roughly grams of extract per 100 g of wort). Typical ale: OG 1.050, FG 1.010 → ~5.3% ABV, 80% attenuation. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your browser.
