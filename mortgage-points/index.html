How to read it

Discount points are prepaid interest: each point costs 1% of the loan and lowers your rate a little. The lower rate shrinks your monthly payment; divide the points cost by that monthly saving to get the break-even — the number of months you must keep the loan before the points pay for themselves. Stay past break-even and you come out ahead; sell or refinance before it and you lose money. The "net saved over term" assumes you hold the loan to the end. A simplified model — not financial advice.
