How it works

  • A fully drawn, capped frame holds roughly 7 lb (deep), 6 lb (medium), or 3.5 lb (shallow) of honey.
  • Multiply by frames per super and how full the frames actually are — early or dearth-year supers are rarely 100% capped.
  • Honey weighs about 12 lb per gallon; a standard 16 oz jar holds ~1.5 lb, so the jar count is harvest ÷ jar weight.
  • Only harvest capped honey — uncapped cells are too high in moisture and can ferment.
  • Leave the bees enough stores for winter; these figures are the surplus you take off, not the whole colony.
