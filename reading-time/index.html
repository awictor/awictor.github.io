How it's estimated

Reading time is simply word count ÷ reading speed. Silent reading of ordinary prose averages around 238 words per minute (the slider lets you adjust for your audience — slower for technical or ESL readers). Speaking time uses a slower ~130 wpm, the comfortable pace for reading aloud or presenting — handy for timing a speech or video script. These are estimates; dense or unfamiliar material reads slower.
